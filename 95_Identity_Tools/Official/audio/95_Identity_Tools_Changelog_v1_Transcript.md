## About This Record

This transcript accompanies the **95_Identity_Tools_Changelog_2026_11_09** audio record preserved within the Brisart Research Archive.

The recording documents the conflict between the stability guarantees of the Brisart Identity Tools LTS-2028 release line and the security changes required by the transition from BSR2 alpha 3 to BSR2 alpha 4 encryption.

It examines the October 23, 2026 development commentary *When the LTS Promise and Security Fix Disagree* together with the subsequent Brisart Identity Tools changelog record documenting the adopted release-line, security, compatibility, and migration strategy.

The recording preserves contemporary discussion of the reasoning surrounding the LTS encryption dilemma and the resulting approach to maintaining the affected Identity Tools release lines.
