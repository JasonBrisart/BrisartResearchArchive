## The pivot convening of this quarter

**Convening 2 — Mapping The Functions.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Convening 1:** every statement names the limits of the authority claimed
- **Convening 2:** every function has a named breakage and a minimum instrument
- **Convening 3:** the dispute resolves without the author present

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Convening | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | claim authority over the specification itself | Draw the distinction: you govern the examination. Not the framework. |
| 2 | propose maximal instruments | Ask what the smallest sufficient version would be. |
| 3 | claim authority to revise the specification | Restate the distinction and require it in the document. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
