# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 5** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students record class expectations across a broken pattern. Evidence: every student has a completed nine-card tally

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name the three things a contradiction can do. Evidence: every student classifies at least six correctly

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students count contradictions needed for durable change. Evidence: the class has a pooled number greater than one

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students observe a broken expectation reappearing. Evidence: every student writes both explanations and admits the question is open

**5.** *(2 points)* a correct expectation that leaves no trace.

**6.** *(2 points)* when what happened does not match what was expected.
