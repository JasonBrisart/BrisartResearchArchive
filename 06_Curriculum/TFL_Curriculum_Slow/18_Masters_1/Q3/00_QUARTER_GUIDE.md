## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 16 — The Executability Test.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 13:** every section is attempted
- **Session 14:** every student produces two design revisions from the paper
- **Session 15:** every change names its source
- **Session 16:** every student receives a question list on the complete design
- **Session 17:** the second run produces fewer questions
- **Session 18:** every design is correctly labelled hypothetical or approved

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | run out of time on estimation | Flag the item weights before they start. |
| 14 | treat the mark as the outcome | Require the revisions rather than the mark. |
| 15 | revise without recording sources | Require the log. |
| 16 | select a reader familiar with their project | Assign readers across topics. |
| 17 | defend the original wording | Point at the evidence: somebody asked. |
| 18 | treat the requirement as paperwork | State the consequence: unapproved work is neither publishable nor defensible. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
