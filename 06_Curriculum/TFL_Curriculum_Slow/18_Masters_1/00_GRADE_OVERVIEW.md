## Entry check — before session one

**Master's Year 1 assumes College Year 4.** The student defends a capstone and states inference ceilings both ways.

- [ ] Place falsification before advocacy without exception
- [ ] Write a falsification condition with a demonstrated firing pathway
- [ ] State the inference ceiling for both positive and null results
- [ ] Defend a capstone against rival, measurement, inference, and scope challengers

If a box will not tick, return to `../17_College_4/` and reteach that outcome before starting. Advancement follows demonstrated readiness, never the calendar.

## Exit outcomes

- Write a Sim(t) protocol with blind coding and reported reliability
- Build an E(t) instrument that cannot manufacture its own correlation
- Write a resolution declaration naming conclusions not licensed
- Produce a measurement map a stranger can execute without clarification

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **executable** | runnable by a competent stranger with no clarification |
| **collinearity** | predictors covarying so closely their effects cannot be separated |
| **resolution declaration** | a statement matching claim scale to observation scale |
| **estimation feasibility** | whether a design can recover parameter values |
| **inter-rater reliability** | agreement between independent blind scorers |

**Not used at this grade:** Peer review to journal structure, referee reports, governance.

## How this grade is taught

Designs must be executable by a stranger without clarification. Every measurement choice carries an assumption that must be named.

## How students show they have learned it

Problem sets, an operationalisation examination, a design portfolio.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "Self-report can carry a timing hypothesis"

**Why it happens.** Report is the available instrument, so it gets used for everything.

**How to fix it.** Report of content is not report of mechanism, and report is slow.

**What to say.** "Change the hypothesis or change the instrument."

### "The weights can be estimated from any dataset"

**Why it happens.** The relation looks like a regression.

**How to fix it.** Memory and simulation are collinear by construction in most tasks.

**What to say.** "Collinear predictors make coefficients unidentifiable."

### "Declaring resolution is modesty"

**Why it happens.** Limitations read as hedging.

**How to fix it.** Show a design observing at minute resolution concluding in window language.

**What to say.** "It is what makes your result interpretable."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the seven-line map with two lines worked, and pair every run.

**Extension.** Ask them to cost the design in hours and participants. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
