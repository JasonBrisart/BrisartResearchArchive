**1.** **Lesson 1 — Two Side By Side.** Students set two expectations side by side and state one difference. *(4 points)*

**3.** **Lesson 3 — Comparing Three.** Students extend comparison beyond a pair. *(4 points)*



**4.** **Lesson 4 — Comparing Across The Day.** Students compare expectations from different points in one day. *(4 points)*



**5.** **Lesson 5 — Both Reasonable.** Students accept that two differing expectations can both be reasonable. *(4 points)*



**6.** **Lesson 6 — When One Is Wrong.** Students handle the case where one expectation was checkable and wrong. *(4 points)*



**7.** **Lesson 7 — Comparing With A Partner.** Students run a full comparison with a partner on a shared event. *(4 points)*
