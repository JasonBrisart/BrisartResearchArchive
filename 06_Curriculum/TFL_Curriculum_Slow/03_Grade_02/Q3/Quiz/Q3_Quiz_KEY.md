# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 2** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students record how expectations survive a single contradiction. Evidence: every student has a completed nine-card tally

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students count contradictions needed for a durable change. Evidence: the class has a pooled number greater than one

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students observe a broken expectation reappearing. Evidence: every student writes both explanations

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students frame a changed expectation as an update. Evidence: every student frames one change as an update

**5.** *(2 points)* put two things side by side and say how they differ.

**6.** *(2 points)* the thing you point at to show your answer is right.
