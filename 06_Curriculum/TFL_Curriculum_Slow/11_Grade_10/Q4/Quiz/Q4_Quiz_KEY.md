# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 10** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students find an explanation of their result that does not use TFL. Evidence: every student produces two genuine alternative explanations

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students discover their result fits alternatives equally well. Evidence: every student applies alternatives to at least two of their own results

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name an observation that separates two explanations. Evidence: every student names a separator for at least two

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students revise a design so it can distinguish explanations. Evidence: every student's redesign produces separating evidence

**5.** *(2 points)* E(t) governs persistence only and contributes to nothing else.

**6.** *(2 points)* E(t) is bounded and cannot escalate without limit.
