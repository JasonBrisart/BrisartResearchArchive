## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 21 — Identifiability Elsewhere.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 17:** every student marks at least one as unsharpenable
- **Lesson 18:** every student rejects at least two of their own
- **Lesson 19:** every student shows an intermediate step for at least six
- **Lesson 20:** every student sorts at least nine correctly
- **Lesson 21:** every student tests five outside predictions
- **Lesson 22:** every student lists five excluded categories
- **Lesson 23:** every student receives an audit marking at least one failure
- **Lesson 24:** the class marks at least eight correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | force every vague statement into a fake specific one | Praise the marked ones: they show where the model stops. |
| 18 | accept their own statements because they know what they meant | Have a partner apply the test instead. |
| 19 | jump straight to a specific statement unrelated to the original | Require the intermediate: show me the halfway version. |
| 20 | treat a strong feeling as evidence of a simulation | Ask for the content: what specifically did you expect? |
| 21 | accept vague public predictions | Ask what specifically would count as that prediction coming true. |
| 22 | treat exclusion as a weakness | Reframe: a model that applies to everything explains nothing. |
| 23 | pass everything | Require one per set. There always is one. |
| 24 | accept vague statements under time pressure | Re-run the third-party test on every disputed item. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
