> **Teacher only.**

**Master's Year 2** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students audit construct validity and prespecification. Evidence: every check carries a finding rather than a verdict

**4.** *(2 points)* a defect that makes the result uninterpretable.

**5.** *(2 points)* comparing your review against others of the same work.
