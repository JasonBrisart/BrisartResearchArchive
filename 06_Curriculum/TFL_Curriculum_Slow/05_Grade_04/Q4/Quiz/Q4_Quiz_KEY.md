# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 4** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students show the chain as a cycle rather than a line. Evidence: every student redraws as a loop with a justification

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students recognise that a trace samples a continuing process. Evidence: every student states that their trace sampled a longer chain

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students apply the principle to a case that feels like a fresh start. Evidence: every student defeats at least two apparent fresh starts

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the year's idea in a single sentence. Evidence: every student produces a one-sentence statement that is not a list

**5.** *(2 points)* the moment you just had becomes the start of the next.

**6.** *(2 points)* what the new moment receives from the one before it.
