# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 11** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the three rival accounts. Evidence: every student has four rows with genuinely distinct predictions

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state rivals as their proponents would. Evidence: every rival statement carries a recorded objection from a checker

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students discover their own results fit the rivals equally well. Evidence: every student applies all three to at least two of their own results

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify where the four accounts come apart. Evidence: every student states honestly whether their design enters it

**5.** *(2 points)* the framework applies only where all variables can be measured.

**6.** *(2 points)* specify how a variable will actually be measured.
