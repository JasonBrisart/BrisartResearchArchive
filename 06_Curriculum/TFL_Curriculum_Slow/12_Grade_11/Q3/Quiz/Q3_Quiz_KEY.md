# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 11** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify what must be controlled for a result to mean anything. Evidence: every student addresses all seven rows with reasons

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students separate the two variables that usually move together. Evidence: every student produces an independent manipulation

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify bias in which predictions get recorded. Evidence: every student names a direction for the bias in their own logs

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students attack a design using an assigned rival account. Evidence: every student receives three attacks, one per rival

**5.** *(2 points)* the framework applies only where all variables can be measured.

**6.** *(2 points)* specify how a variable will actually be measured.
