## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Measuring E(t).** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student marks at least three correctly with specific reasons
- **Lesson 2:** every student has a marked draft with unrunnable lines identified
- **Lesson 3:** every protocol includes an unscoreable worked example
- **Lesson 4:** every student identifies at least three failure modes correctly
- **Lesson 5:** every student produces a genuine alternative reason
- **Lesson 6:** every student identifies a vulnerability in their own criterion
- **Lesson 7:** at least one marker returns zero questions
- **Lesson 8:** the class identifies unrunnable lines in at least four maps

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | mark a proposal invalid without naming which variable is unmeasured | Require specificity: which variable? Name it. |
| 2 | write a line describing an intention rather than a procedure | Ask for the procedure: what does somebody physically do? |
| 3 | omit the unscoreable example | Require it: show me a response your rule cannot score. |
| 4 | conflate leading elicitation with retrospective collection | Separate them with examples of each. |
| 5 | claim their instrument is clean without checking | Run the three requirements line by line in front of them. |
| 6 | omit the partial-match rule | Supply a borderline case and ask how it scores. |
| 7 | assume one clean run is enough | Require two independent markers. |
| 8 | accept a vague Sim(t) line | Apply the third-party test to every Sim(t) line. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
