# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 2** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build an E(t) instrument that avoids circularity. Evidence: every student produces a genuine alternative explanation for a correlation

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students separate intensity from general activation. Evidence: every instrument has a separate activation index

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students ask what their instrument actually measures. Evidence: every student names three plausible alternatives per instrument

**4.** *(2 points)* constructing the expectation after the outcome.

**5.** *(2 points)* recording what will be measured before the data exist.
