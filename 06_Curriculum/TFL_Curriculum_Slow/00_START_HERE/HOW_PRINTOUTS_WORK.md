# How Printouts Work

## One printout per session

Every session has one matching student printout in that quarter's `Printouts/`
folder, named to match the session file.

## Distribute before the hook

The printout opens with *What I think before the session*. That section is only
meaningful if it is completed **before any explanation**. A printout handed out
after the teaching has lost the part that makes it worth printing.

## What the printout is for

It lets the student follow the session, record their initial position, take
structured notes, work the same examples the teacher works, do the independent
task, record results that did not fit, reach a limited conclusion, and complete
the exit ticket.

It is not a simplified copy of the lesson.

## What is never in a printout

- Answer keys
- The expected result
- Predicted student errors
- Teacher scripts or corrections
- Any wording that pressures a student toward the framework's predicted pattern

## Alignment

Every worked example, guided example, independent task, and exit-ticket item
appears in the printout **in the same order** as in the teacher lesson.

## Printing

Black and white. Nothing depends on colour. Response lines are sized for
handwriting — do not shrink them to save pages.

## Accessibility

Every written field may be completed orally with an adult scribing. Every drawn
field may be described aloud. Reduce the required number of independent examples
from three to one where needed. One example done properly meets the outcome.
