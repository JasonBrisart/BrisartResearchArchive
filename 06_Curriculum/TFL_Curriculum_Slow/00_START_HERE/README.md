# Read This First

## What TFL is

Experience updates in short repeating steps of roughly 100 to 300 milliseconds.
In each step, sensory input, retrieved memory, and a predictive simulation are
integrated into a current state, carried forward as the starting condition for
the next step. Affective intensity separately governs whether the simulation
persists into the next step.

## What is different about this edition

Each lesson file now contains, in order: the concept explained for the teacher,
two examples and a non-example, what the activity shows and what it does not,
prerequisite checks with a recovery activity, exact teacher scripts, a worked
example, two guided examples, executable independent practice, discussion
questions with expected responses, checks for understanding with a decision rule,
an exit ticket with a teacher-only key, expected student work at three levels,
at least four misconceptions with corrections, support and extension, scope and
safety notes, the homework connection, and reflection prompts.

Each lesson has a matching student printout aligned section by section.

## How to use it

1. Find your grade in `../README.md`.
2. Read its `00_GRADE_OVERVIEW.md`.
3. Open `Q1/00_QUARTER_GUIDE.md` and work down it.
4. Print the session's printout before the session.
5. Teach from the lesson file.
