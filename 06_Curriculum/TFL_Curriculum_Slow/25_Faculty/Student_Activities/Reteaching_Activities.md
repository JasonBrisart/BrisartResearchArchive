# Reteaching Activities — Faculty

> **Teacher-led.** Use these when a check for understanding fails.

**Supports:** recovery on any grade outcome · **Format:** small group or
individual · **Works without a teacher:** no

## When to use which

| If the student | Use |
|---|---|
| Cannot state the idea | Recovery 1 |
| States it but cannot give an example | Recovery 2 |
| Gives examples that all reuse yours | Recovery 3 |
| Reasons backwards from outcomes | Recovery 4 |
| Overstates what the activity shows | Recovery 5 |

## Recovery 1 — One sentence

Give the student three sentences, one correct. They pick and say why. Then they
write their own version. Five minutes.

## Recovery 2 — Example ladder

You give an example. They give one. You give a harder one. They give one. Stop
when they produce one unaided. Five minutes.

## Recovery 3 — Banned examples

List the teacher examples on a card. The student must find one not on the card.
Offer categories rather than answers: Provide each template with one section fully worked, and pair every test.

## Recovery 4 — Cover the ending

Give a case with the outcome covered. The student reasons forwards only. Reveal
the outcome afterwards. Repeat twice.

## Recovery 5 — Two columns

Head one column "what this shows" and one "what this does not show". The student
fills both. Most students can fill the first; the second is the actual repair.

## Complete when

The student produces one unaided example and one honest limit.
