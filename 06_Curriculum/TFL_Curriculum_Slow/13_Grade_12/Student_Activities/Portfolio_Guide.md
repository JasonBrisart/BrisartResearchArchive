# Portfolio Guide — Grade 12

The portfolio is **cumulative and selective**. You do not keep everything. You
keep enough to compare your early work with your later work.

## What to keep

| Item | How many | When |
|---|---|---|
| Lesson printouts | 1 per quarter | Your strongest, chosen by you |
| Homework | 1 per quarter | The one you learned most from |
| Quiz reflection | 1 per quarter | After each quiz |
| Quarter project | All 4 | At the end of each quarter |
| A corrected test item | 1 per quarter | The one you got wrong and fixed |
| Your strongest example | 1 for the year | Any time |
| A result that did not fit | 1 for the year | When it happens |
| A corrected misconception | 1 for the year | When you correct it |
| Reflection journal | 4 entries plus the year entry | End of each quarter |

## What not to keep

Everything else. A portfolio that keeps every page cannot be compared against
itself.

## The comparison that matters

At the end of the year, put your Quarter 1 printout next to your Quarter 4
printout and write what changed. That comparison is the portfolio's purpose.
