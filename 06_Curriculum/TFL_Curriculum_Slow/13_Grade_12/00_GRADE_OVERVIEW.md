**Course:** Falsification and Scope · **Stage:** Secondary

## Exit outcomes

- State the four requirements of a falsification condition
- Explain why absence of support is not falsification
- Reject an invalid application with its boundary and an alternative level
- Defend an adjudicating design against four adversarial roles

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **falsification condition** | a result that would be inconsistent with the claim |
| **circularity** | a claim built so that no result could contradict it |
| **invalid application** | a use of the framework outside what it models |
| **computational hypothesis** | what a simulation is; not an intention or wish |
| **gate requirement** | an item that must pass regardless of total score |

**Not used at this grade:** Publication-standard measurement critique, referee-quality review.

## How this grade is taught

Capstone standard, defended orally against assigned adversarial roles. Advocacy is not accepted before a falsification condition is on record.

## How students show they have learned it

A capstone design, quarter quizzes and tests, and a gated final.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "'We found no support' is a falsification condition"

**Why it happens.** Absence feels like disconfirmation.

**How to fix it.** Absence may come from poor measurement, low power, or a broken design.

**What to say.** "Name the observation that would actually occur."

### "Everything in my life is an instance of this framework"

**Why it happens.** New frameworks feel universal, and capable students over-apply most.

**How to fix it.** The scope test, applied to the student's own best idea.

**What to say.** "Years, identity, groups, and brain wiring are different questions."

### "A simulation is something the system wants"

**Why it happens.** Agency language is the default for anything that predicts.

**How to fix it.** State the exclusion: no agency, volition, goals, or selfhood.

**What to say.** "Computational hypotheses. Not intentions, not wishes, not choices."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the falsification template with one condition fully worked.

**Extension.** Ask for a falsification condition for a belief they hold outside this subject. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.

## This grade is gated

The final does not pass unless both gate items are satisfied regardless of total
score: a falsification condition naming a possible observation, and a correctly
identified invalid application with its boundary named. Announce both in session
one; never apply them retroactively.
