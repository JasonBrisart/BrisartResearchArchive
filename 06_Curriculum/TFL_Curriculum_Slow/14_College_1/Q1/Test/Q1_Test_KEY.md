> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 1** · **Total: 42 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every objection is specific rather than a statement of status

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: all three versions are accurate in both directions

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the pooled list contains at least four genuinely unchecked claims

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student has a reasoned position in either direction

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student marks at least one moment as unmappable

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student states the cross-cycle versus within-cycle distinction

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the class judges at least four correctly

**8.** *(2 points)* what is currently known about whether a framework is correct.

**9.** *(2 points)* confirmation by researchers unconnected to the author.

**10.** *(2 points)* presenting a framework as more established than it is.

**11.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the class judges at least four correctly
