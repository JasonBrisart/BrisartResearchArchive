## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 4 — Is This Worth Studying.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 1:** every objection is specific rather than a statement of status
- **Session 2:** all three versions are accurate in both directions
- **Session 3:** the pooled list contains at least four genuinely unchecked claims
- **Session 4:** every student has a reasoned position in either direction
- **Session 5:** every student marks at least one moment as unmappable
- **Session 6:** every student states the cross-cycle versus within-cycle distinction
- **Session 7:** the class judges at least four correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | write that it is unvalidated as their objection | Reject it as an objection: that is a status. What is wrong with the claim? |
| 2 | hedge the statement into dismissal | Mark it as an error with the same weight as overclaiming. |
| 3 | mark the whole specification as unchecked | Require the distinction: which claims have an observation attached? |
| 4 | assume the instructor's position and adopt it | Decline to reveal one: I am not telling you what I think. |
| 5 | map a mood as a simulation | Apply the third-party test: could somebody absent check that? |
| 6 | treat belief as a sixth variable in the integration function | Point at the relation: belief does not appear in it. |
| 7 | drift toward dismissal | Mark both directions equally, visibly. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
