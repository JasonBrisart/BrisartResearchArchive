# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 3** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students recognise that experience arrives combined. Evidence: every student produces one sentence that is not a list

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name the three inputs that fed one moment. Evidence: every final line is a single combined statement

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students explain why identical input yields different moments. Evidence: the class locates the differing line as the remembering

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build a reconstruction outside the classroom. Evidence: every student completes five lines in the new setting

**5.** *(2 points)* everything combined into the moment you are actually having.

**6.** *(2 points)* three things arriving together as one.
