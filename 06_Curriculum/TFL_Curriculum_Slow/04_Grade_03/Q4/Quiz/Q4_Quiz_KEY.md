# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 3** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students reconstruct a moment whose expectation failed. Evidence: every student identifies the noticing as the changed part

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students test what happens when each part is varied. Evidence: every student produces three varied moments

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students reconstruct two consecutive moments. Evidence: every student has two reconstructions from consecutive moments

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students confront the open question of what happens next. Evidence: every student has written and sealed a dated answer

**5.** *(2 points)* everything combined into the moment you are actually having.

**6.** *(2 points)* three things arriving together as one.
