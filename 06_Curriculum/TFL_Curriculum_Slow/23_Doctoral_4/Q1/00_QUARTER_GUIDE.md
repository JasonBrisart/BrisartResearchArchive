## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 3 — Reporting What Happened.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 1:** every participant has a non-empty contribution under both outcomes
- **Seminar 2:** every log includes at least one modification with its prompting result
- **Seminar 3:** every section is marked and cleaned
- **Seminar 4:** every analysis is checked against the plan, not against memory

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | assume a null is a recoverable disappointment | Require the statement now, while the design can still be repaired. |
| 2 | report no modifications | Ask for the history: reconstruct it from your notes. |
| 3 | defend interpretive phrasing as context | Cut it and show the section still reports. |
| 4 | label a post-hoc analysis as confirmatory | Check against the registered plan in front of them. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
