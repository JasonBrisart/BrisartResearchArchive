# Review Games — Grade 1

> **Teacher-led.** Whole-class review, typically end of quarter.

## Game 1 — In or out

You read a case. Students move left for "this grade's idea covers it" and right
for "outside". Discuss any split.

Prepare eight cases: five inside, three outside. The three outside cases are the
ones that do the teaching.

## Game 2 — Finish the sentence

Read the first half of a closing sentence from any lesson this quarter. Students
complete it. Fastest correct table scores.

## Game 3 — Spot the backwards reasoning

Read a worked case aloud. In half of them, reason backwards from the outcome.
Students call it when they hear it.

## Game 4 — Build the chain

Each table gets one lesson title from the quarter. They arrange themselves in
teaching order and say what each hands to the next.

## Game 5 — What it does not show

Give a result. Teams compete to list what it does **not** establish. Longest
defensible list wins. This one rewards the habit the curriculum most wants.

## Never

Do not award points for correct predictions in any game. You are never graded on whether a prediction turned out correct. You are graded on recording it and reasoning about it.
