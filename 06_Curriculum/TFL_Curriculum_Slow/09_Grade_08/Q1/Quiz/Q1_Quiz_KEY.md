# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 8** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students record the drop and recovery across a broken pattern. Evidence: every student has a plot showing drop and recovery

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students measure how many contradictions produce durable change. Evidence: the class has a pooled number and each student compared it

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students define recurrence and distinguish it from persistence. Evidence: every student marks at least one case as unclassifiable

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students discover that sampling rate sets what can be seen. Evidence: every student states a minimum detectable gap

**5.** *(2 points)* a simulation being modified after contradiction.

**6.** *(2 points)* a simulation being pushed aside rather than deleted.
