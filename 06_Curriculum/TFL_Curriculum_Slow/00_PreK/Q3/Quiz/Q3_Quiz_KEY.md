# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children notice some things stay with them and some do not. Evidence: each child names one thing that stuck and one that did not

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children recognise persistence in a familiar everyday form. Evidence: children can report a song continuing after it stopped

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children begin to say why something stayed with them. Evidence: every child offers a reason

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children find a correct guess they forgot immediately. Evidence: the group produces at least three right-and-forgotten examples

**5.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.
