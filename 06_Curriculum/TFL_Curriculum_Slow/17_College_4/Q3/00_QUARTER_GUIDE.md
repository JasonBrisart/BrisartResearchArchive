## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 18 — Capstone Scope Rejection.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 15:** at least one external run returns zero questions
- **Session 16:** all five prediction rows differ
- **Session 17:** every row has a control and an acknowledged residual risk
- **Session 18:** every student names why the invalid version appealed
- **Session 19:** every student passes a peer gate check on both items
- **Session 20:** every student records their unanswered questions
- **Session 21:** every unanswered question produces a revision

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | assume Year 2 standards are sufficient without re-checking | Require two fresh external runs on the capstone map. |
| 16 | reuse a prediction across accounts | Mark coinciding rows and require a redesign. |
| 17 | dismiss confounds without reasons | Require a written reason for each dismissal. |
| 18 | reject somebody else's design | Require their own: your design. The invalid version of it. |
| 19 | leave a gate for later | Run the gate check first. |
| 20 | defend by restating the design | Redirect: they asked what it cannot see. |
| 21 | dismiss the questions | Require a revision for each. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
