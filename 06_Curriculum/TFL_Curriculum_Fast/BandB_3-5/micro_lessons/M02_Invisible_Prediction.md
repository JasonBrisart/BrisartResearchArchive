# Micro-Lesson B2: The Invisible Prediction

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Band:** B (3-5)
**Materials:** A chair

---

## Purpose

Prove that predictions exist even when nothing is noticed.

## Script

**Ask a student to sit down.** They do.

**Say:**
> "Were you surprised?"

No.

**Say:**
> "So you had no prediction about that chair?"

They will agree.

**Say:**
> "Okay. What if it had collapsed?"

They will react.

**Say:**
> "You'd have been extremely surprised. And you can only be surprised if you expected something. So the prediction was there the whole time — you just never noticed it, because it was right."

## The Test

Write the rule:

```
If breaking it would surprise you,
the prediction was there.
```

**Ask students to find two more** in the room. Doors, light switches, the floor, their own footsteps.

## Use

Pairs with Day 5. Also works as a standalone warm-up any time.
