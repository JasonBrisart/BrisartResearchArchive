# Band D — Day 10: Falsification

**Duration:** 50 min

---

## Objective

Students state falsification conditions for specific TFL claims.

---

## 1. Hook — The Unfalsifiable Claim (7 min)

Write:

> "Predictions persist when they matter, and when they don't persist, they didn't matter enough."

**Ask:**
> "What result would contradict that?"

None. It's circular.

**Say:**
> "That's the trap. The framework avoids it with a threshold, measured intensity, and identifiable content. Today you make the escape explicit."

---

## 2. Name — What Falsification Requires (12 min)

Write the structure:

```
A FALSIFICATION CONDITION MUST:

1. Be stated before data collection
2. Be observable with the planned measurements
3. Be possible — the design could actually produce it
4. Be inconsistent with the framework's claim
```

Model one:

```
CLAIM: Simulations persist only when E(t) > θ_persistence.

FALSIFIED IF: Content-identifiable simulations persist
at the same rate across measured high and low E(t)
conditions, with ambiguity and repetition controlled.
```

**Say:**
> "Notice the controls in that statement. Without them, an alternative account explains the result and nothing has been falsified."

---

## 3. Work — Four Falsification Conditions (21 min)

Students write conditions for four claims.

```
CLAIM 1: E(t) governs persistence.
FALSIFIED IF: ____________________________

CLAIM 2: E(t) does not contribute to integration.
FALSIFIED IF: ____________________________

CLAIM 3: Contradiction produces revision or suppression.
FALSIFIED IF: ____________________________

CLAIM 4: Integration is conditionally deterministic.
FALSIFIED IF: ____________________________
```

**Standard:** each must name the observation, not merely the absence of support.

---

## 4. Compare — Strongest and Weakest (8 min)

Put two student conditions side by side — one specific, one vague.

**Ask:**
> "Which one could actually be checked next week?"

**Say:**
> "Specificity is the whole difference between a falsification condition and a hedge."

---

## 5. Carry (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Tomorrow: the other half. Where the framework doesn't apply at all."
