# Micro-Lesson D1: Equation Walk

**Duration:** 8 minutes
**Band:** D (9-12)

---

## Purpose

Fluent symbol-by-symbol reading of both relations.

## Script

Write both:

```
F(t) = w1·Input(t) + w2·Minfo(t) + w3·Sim(t)
Sim(t+1) = Sim(t),  if  E(t) > θ_persistence
```

**Say:**
> "Read them aloud as sentences. No symbol skipping."

Model the first:
> "The integrated state at time t equals weight one times input, plus weight two times retrieved memory, plus weight three times the simulation."

Model the second:
> "The simulation at the next window equals the simulation now, if and only if affective intensity exceeds the persistence threshold."

## The Diagnostic Question

> "Why does `E(t)` appear in the second and not the first?"

**Required answer:** Affective intensity functions exclusively as a persistence gate and does not contribute to integration.

A student who cannot answer this has not yet grasped the framework's most distinctive structural claim.

## Rapid Drill

Call a symbol, students give function:

`w2` · `θ_persistence` · `Sim(t+1)` · `F(t)` · `E(t)` · `w3`

## Use

Warm-up for any Band D session. Essential before midterm and final.
