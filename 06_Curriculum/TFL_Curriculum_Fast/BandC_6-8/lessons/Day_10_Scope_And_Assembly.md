# Band C — Day 10: Scope and Assembly

**Duration:** 45 min
**Materials:** Chart paper, unit test

---

## Objective

Students state what TFL does not model and complete the unit test.

---

## 1. Hook — The Over-Application Trap (6 min)

Read four claims. Students vote inside/outside the model.

```
1. "TFL explains why I expected the bell to ring."
2. "TFL explains why I'm the kind of person I am."
3. "TFL explains why that sound surprised me."
4. "TFL explains why my friendship changed over two years."
```

Answers: 1 inside, 2 outside, 3 inside, 4 outside.

**Say:**
> "Two of those were traps. Getting this right is part of understanding the model."

---

## 2. Name — The Boundaries (12 min)

Write the exclusions:

```
TFL is NOT defined to model:
  - long-term memory consolidation
  - identity formation
  - narrative construction
  - developmental trajectories
  - group-level or social-level dynamics

TFL does NOT:
  - specify neural circuits or anatomy
  - claim to explain what consciousness is
  - provide diagnostic or therapeutic content
```

**Say:**
> "These aren't disclaimers. They're part of the framework. A model that claims everything predicts nothing."

**The scope test:**

```
Is it short-timescale?        → maybe inside
Can you state the Sim(t)?     → maybe inside
Is it about years, identity,
   groups, or brain wiring?   → outside
```

---

## 3. Work — Unit Test (25 min)

Administer the **Band C Unit Test**.

---

## 4. Carry (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Band D takes the two equations behind all of this and makes you design a study that could prove the whole thing wrong."

---

## End of Band C

Students should be able to name and define all five variables, state the persistence condition, distinguish persistence from accuracy, describe contradiction and recurrence, identify the three dynamics, and correctly reject out-of-scope applications.
