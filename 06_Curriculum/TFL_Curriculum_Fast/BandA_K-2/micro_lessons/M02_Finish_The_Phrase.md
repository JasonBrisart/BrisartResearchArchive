# Micro-Lesson A2: Finish the Phrase

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Band:** A (K-2)
**Materials:** None
**Use as:** Warm-up or line-up activity

---

## Purpose

Show that expectation comes from memory, instantly and without effort.

## Script

Say the first part of familiar phrases and stop. Students complete them.

Run four or five:
- "Ready, set..."
- "Once upon a..."
- "Happy birthday to..."
- "One, two, buckle my..."
- "The more you know, the more you..."

After each, ask: **"Where did that come from?"**

**Close:**
> "You didn't think about it. It was just there. That's your remembering handing you the next part."

## The Twist (optional, 1 extra min)

Do one where you say the wrong ending.

> "Ready, set... banana."

Students will react.

**Say:**
> "That's surprise. It only happens because you were already expecting something."

## Watch For

This micro-lesson is the fastest route back into the unit after a weekend or break. It re-establishes the core idea in under two minutes.
