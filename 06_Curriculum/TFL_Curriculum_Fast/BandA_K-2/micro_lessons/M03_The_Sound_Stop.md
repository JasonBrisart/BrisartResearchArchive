# Micro-Lesson A3: The Sound Stop

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Band:** A (K-2)
**Materials:** Any audio source, or instructor voice
**Use as:** Standalone demonstration of engineered mismatch

---

## Purpose

Produce a surprise and convert it into a reported prediction.

## Script

Play or sing something familiar. Let it run about fifteen seconds so students settle in.

**Stop abruptly mid-phrase.**

**Immediately ask:**
> "What was supposed to happen?"

Students will finish the phrase for you.

**Say:**
> "You knew what came next. You were already playing it in your head."

**Then the frame:**

```
I was surprised because I expected ________.
```

Have two or three students complete it aloud.

**Close:**
> "When your guess is right, you never notice it. Breaking it is how we catch it."

## Variations

**Visual:** Clap a pattern three times, then hold still on the fourth.

**Movement:** Lead three identical arm motions, then a different one. Students will begin the expected motion before catching themselves. This version makes prediction physically visible and is the strongest option for most classes.

## Accommodation

Warn sound-sensitive students privately beforehand: "Something will stop suddenly. It's on purpose." They still predict; the startle is removed.
