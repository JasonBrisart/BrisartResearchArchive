# Level H — Module 2: Supervising Students on Unvalidated Work

**Duration:** 3.5 hours
**Artifact:** Supervision plan

## Objective

Participants build a supervision plan that protects students working on a framework that may not survive.

---

## 1. Frame — The Student Bears the Risk (25 min)

**Say:**
> "You have tenure, a position, or a trajectory that survives this framework being wrong. A doctoral student who builds four years on it may not. That asymmetry is the central ethical fact of supervising here."

---

## 2. Build — Six Supervisory Obligations (55 min)

**Obligation 1 — Informed entry.** The student knows the status before committing, in writing, with the risk named.

**Obligation 2 — Portable skills.** The work must build competences that transfer if the framework collapses — measurement, adjudication, review. A student whose only asset is TFL expertise has been failed.

**Obligation 3 — Hedged program design.** Dissertation structure should not require the framework to be correct. Adjudication designs satisfy this naturally: they produce a publishable result either way.

**Obligation 4 — Overclaiming interception.** Catch it in drafts, not at defense. Students under career pressure overclaim on their framework's behalf.

**Obligation 5 — Exit path.** A stated point at which the student can redirect without losing accumulated work.

**Obligation 6 — Licensing clarity.** Rights confirmed before derivative work begins. A student who writes a validation study without appropriate rights has a problem the supervisor should have prevented.

**Say:**
> "Obligation 3 is the one that does the most protective work. A well-built adjudication study is publishable whichever way the result falls, which means the student's degree does not depend on the framework being true."

---

## 3. Contest — Should Students Work on This at All? (50 min)

- **Participant A:** Doctoral students should not be exposed to a single-author unvalidated framework. The career risk is not theirs to take on a supervisor's interest.
- **Participant B:** Students routinely work on contested and emerging ideas. The obligation is informed consent and hedged design, not prohibition.

**Say:**
> "If A convinces you, that is a legitimate outcome and your supervision plan should say so — a plan that declines to supervise this work, with reasons, is a passing artifact."

---

## 4. Apply — Build the Plan (55 min)

    SUPERVISION PLAN

      Frequency and what triggers reassessment:

---

## 5. Propagate (15 min)

**Say:**
> "Next: scope enforcement. Your students will propose the clinical study. All of them, eventually."

---

## Instructor Notes

- Participants with current students find this module immediately usable and often revise real plans in session. Allow it.
- The declining-to-supervise option must be genuinely available or the Contest phase is theatre.
