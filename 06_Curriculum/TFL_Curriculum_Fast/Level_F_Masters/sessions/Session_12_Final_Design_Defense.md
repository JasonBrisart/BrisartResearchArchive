# Level F — Session 12: Final Design Defense

**Duration:** 100 min

## Objective

Students present and defend a complete, executable, adjudicating study design.

---

## 1. Orientation (10 min)

Restate the disclosure. Then the gate:

> "The gate is executability. If a competent researcher could not run your design without asking you a question, it does not pass, regardless of how elegant the reasoning is."

**Say:**
> "Nobody is being asked to have designed a study that vindicates the framework. Several of you have designed studies you expect to refute it. Those are equally valid and one of them is the strongest submission in the room."

---

## 2. Defenses (75 min)

Each student presents for 6 minutes, then takes 4 minutes of questions.

**Presentation must cover, in order:**

    1. The claim being tested
    2. The measurement map, in full
    3. The discriminating condition
    4. Predictions for TFL and all three rivals
    5. The falsification condition
    6. What this design cannot see

**Questioning rotates by assigned role.** Each presenter faces one question from each:

- The **uncertainty** challenger
- The **arousal** challenger
- The **habit** challenger
- The **scope** challenger

**Say:**
> "Item 6 is not modesty. A design whose author cannot state its blind spots will have them found by someone else later, under worse conditions."

---

## 3. Close (15 min)

Collect final designs.

**Say:**
> "You arrived able to critique a framework. You're leaving able to build the instrument that would settle a piece of it. Those are different skills and the second one is rarer."

**Say:**
> "You still don't know whether TFL is true. But there are now twelve designs in this room that would move the question, which is more than existed twelve weeks ago."

---

## End of Level F

Students completing Level F should be able to:

- Operationalize all five declared variables with executable instruments
- Prespecify persistence criteria and justify sampling intervals
- Match claim scale to observation resolution honestly
- Determine whether weights are estimable in a given design
- Build a design entering the discriminating condition
- Identify and control the seven standard confounds
- Write an analysis plan that fixes every consequential decision
- Reject invalid designs formally, including their own
- Conduct and receive a formal design review

Level G begins from a design that survives audit and adds original critique, adjudication against published literature, and referee-quality review.
