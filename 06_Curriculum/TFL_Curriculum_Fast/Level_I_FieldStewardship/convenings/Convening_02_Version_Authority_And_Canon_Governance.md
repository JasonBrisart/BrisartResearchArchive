# Level I — Convening 2: Version Authority and Canon Governance

**Duration:** Full day
**Artifact:** Version authority policy

## Objective

Participants produce a policy establishing which document governs, how
supersession is declared, and how the record remains interpretable across
versions.

---

## 1. Frame — The Silent Failure (45 min)

**Say:**

> "Version drift does not announce itself. A study cites the framework, a second
> study cites the framework, and years later nobody can determine whether they
> tested the same claim. By then the fix is unavailable."

---

## 2. Build — What a Version Authority Policy Must Fix (120 min)

    THE GOVERNING DOCUMENT
      Named explicitly. For this curriculum and for any body
      adopting this policy, that is the Institutional Edition.

    SUPERSESSION DECLARATION
      How a version is marked superseded, by whom, and where
      that declaration is published.

    CITATION REQUIREMENT
      What a publication must state so a reader can determine
      which architecture was tested.

    CONFLICT RULE
      What governs when a cited document and the governing
      document disagree.

    HISTORICAL PRESERVATION
      Superseded versions remain available as record and are
      not deleted, edited, or quietly revised.

**Say the load-bearing point:**

> "Supersession is not deletion. A governance body that erases superseded
> versions destroys the ability to interpret the work that cited them."

**Then the constraint that applies to this body:**

> "Your policy governs which document is authoritative. It does not distribute
> operational content. A version authority policy that circulates parameter
> values or calibration procedures has exceeded its function."

---

## 3. Contest — Who Holds Authority? (90 min)

- **Participant A:** Version authority properly rests with the framework's author.
  A governance body asserting it is overreach.
- **Participant B:** Authority over the specification rests with the author;
  authority over *which version a field treats as current for evaluation* is a
  field function and must be exercised by the field.

**Say:**

> "B's distinction is the one your policy needs to make explicit. Get it wrong in
> either direction and the policy is either useless or illegitimate."

---

citation dispute and reports whether it resolved without the author present.

---

## 5. Propagate (30 min)

**Say:**

> "Next: replication across institutions, where the record either accumulates or
> quietly does not."
