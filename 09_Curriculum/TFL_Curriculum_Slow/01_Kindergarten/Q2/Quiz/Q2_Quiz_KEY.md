# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children identify things that come back without effort. Evidence: children distinguish a notice answer from a remember answer

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children sort examples into noticing and remembering. Evidence: most pairs sort at least six of eight correctly

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children retrieve something from a longer interval. Evidence: the group recalls at least three items before checking

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children notice that remembering can be automatic and complete. Evidence: the group completes at least three fragments

**5.** *(2 points)* what I see, hear, or feel right now.

**6.** *(2 points)* what comes back from before.
