# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Kindergarten** · **Total: 44 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child names one thing noticed

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child has three items from three senses

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child names something noticeable only in the new place

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group finds at least two changes per round

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every pair finds one difference

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child finds something genuinely new to them

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group's tally is larger than they predicted

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group can define noticing in their own words

**9.** *(2 points)* what I see, hear, or feel right now.

**10.** *(2 points)* what comes back from before.

**11.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the group can define noticing in their own words
