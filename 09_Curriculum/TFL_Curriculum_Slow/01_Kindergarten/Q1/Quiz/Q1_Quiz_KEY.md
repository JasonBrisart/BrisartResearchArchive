# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children name things they can sense at this moment. Evidence: every child names one thing noticed

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children notice using three different senses deliberately. Evidence: every child has three items from three senses

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children notice in a different environment. Evidence: every child names something noticeable only in the new place

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children detect a change in a familiar scene. Evidence: the group finds at least two changes per round

**5.** *(2 points)* what I see, hear, or feel right now.

**6.** *(2 points)* what comes back from before.
