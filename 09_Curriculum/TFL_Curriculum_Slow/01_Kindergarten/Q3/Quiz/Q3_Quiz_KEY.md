# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children commit to a guess aloud before an outcome. Evidence: children volunteer guesses before the reveal

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children record a guess before finding out. Evidence: every child draws before each reveal

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children make a guess checked much later. Evidence: every child records a number or a drawing

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children check a guess made in a previous session. Evidence: every child checks their own and marks it

**5.** *(2 points)* what I see, hear, or feel right now.

**6.** *(2 points)* what comes back from before.
