# Human Review Queue

## 1. Coverage

**Status.** Complete. All 27 grades expanded.

No grade was stubbed. Every session carries a hand-authored concept block: a plain-language explanation, two concrete examples, a non-example, what the activity shows, and what it does not establish.

## 2. Quiz, test, and final items

**Files:** every `Quiz/`, `Test/`, and `Final/` paper.

**Issue.** Items are composed from authored objectives and success criteria
rather than being independently authored per question.

**Why automation could not resolve it.** Independent item authoring requires
decisions about difficulty calibration and discrimination that a generator
cannot make responsibly.

**Decision required.** Review each paper for item quality before first use.

## 3. Grade-specific example currency

**Files:** all lessons.

**Issue.** Examples use classroom-general contexts. Local contexts are usually
stronger.

**Decision required.** Substitute local examples where they would land better.
The structure does not depend on the specific example.

## 4. Institutional requirements

**Files:** all lessons from Master's Year 1 upward, if expanded.

**Issue.** Any design involving human participants is subject to institutional
approval that this package cannot grant.

**Decision required.** Confirm that all such work remains hypothetical unless
separately approved.
