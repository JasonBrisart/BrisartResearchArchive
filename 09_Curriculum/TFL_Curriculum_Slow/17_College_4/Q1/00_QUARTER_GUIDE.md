## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 4 — Designing The Capstone.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 1:** every student has three correctly ordered arguments
- **Session 2:** every claim is narrow enough for one project
- **Session 3:** every student demonstrates a pathway rather than asserting one
- **Session 4:** every student receives an executability check with marked lines
- **Session 5:** every student names the tempting rung
- **Session 6:** every student states honestly whether a null fires their condition
- **Session 7:** every planned analysis is marked in advance

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | place the condition as a closing caveat | Return it: that is a caveat. First is what makes it a test. |
| 2 | select the whole framework as the target | Narrow it with them until one claim remains. |
| 3 | assert the condition could fire | Require the result: write the actual numbers. |
| 4 | write a design requiring their presence | Hand it to a classmate and list every question asked. |
| 5 | place their ceiling at rung four | Ask what converging evidence exists. Rung four needs more than one design. |
| 6 | claim a null refutes the framework | Walk the controls: did they hold? |
| 7 | leave analyses unlabelled to decide later | Require labels now. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
