## Gate check — score this first

| Gate | Requirement | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Falsification item names a possible observation | Y / N |
| **B** | Scope item names a genuinely invalid application AND its boundary | Y / N |

**If either is N, the paper does not pass.** Record the numeric score, mark the
gate failure, and assign remediation before re-assessment.
