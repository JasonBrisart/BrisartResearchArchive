# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children say a guess out loud before an object is revealed. Evidence: every child has said at least one guess out loud

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children discover they had a guess without being asked for one. Evidence: at least half the group answers a do-not-look question

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children complete familiar phrases, showing guesses come from before. Evidence: the group can say because I heard it before in any words

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children make a guess about something they can check immediately. Evidence: every child commits before looking

**5.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.
