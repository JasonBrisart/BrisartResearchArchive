# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 46 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child has said at least one guess out loud

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: at least half the group answers a do-not-look question

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group can say because I heard it before in any words

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child commits before looking

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child has drawn a guess about something later

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child guesses from touch before seeing

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group notices that answers differ

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group can say guessing happened every time

**9.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**10.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.

**11.** *(2 points)* it happens over and over and does not stop.

**12.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the group can say guessing happened every time
