**1.** **Lesson 1 — What Is In The Bag.** Children say a guess out loud before an object is revealed. *(4 points)*



**2.** **Lesson 2 — You Knew Already.** Children discover they had a guess without being asked for one. *(4 points)*



**3.** **Lesson 3 — Finish It.** Children complete familiar phrases, showing guesses come from before. *(4 points)*



**4.** **Lesson 4 — Guessing the Weather.** Children make a guess about something they can check immediately. *(4 points)*



**5.** **Lesson 5 — The Snack Guess.** Children guess about a routine event and check it later in the day. *(4 points)*



**6.** **Lesson 6 — Guessing By Touch.** Children guess using one sense when another is removed. *(4 points)*



**7.** **Lesson 7 — Two Guesses.** Children hear that two people can guess differently about the same thing. *(4 points)*



**8.** **Lesson 8 — The Guessing Chart.** Children review the quarter and see that guessing happened every session. *(4 points)*



**9.** What does **guess** mean? *(2 points)*



**10.** What does **surprise** mean? *(2 points)*



**11.** What does **again** mean? *(2 points)*
