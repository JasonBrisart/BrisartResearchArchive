## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 21 — Wrong But Stuck.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 17:** each child names one thing that stuck and one that did not
- **Lesson 18:** children can report a song continuing after it stopped
- **Lesson 19:** every child offers a reason
- **Lesson 20:** the group produces at least three right-and-forgotten examples
- **Lesson 21:** the group produces at least three wrong-and-stuck examples
- **Lesson 22:** every child places at least two items
- **Lesson 23:** every child attempts an explanation
- **Lesson 24:** the group places at least six examples correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | say everything stuck | Prompt for the missing side: what did you do right after you came in? |
| 18 | think the song is literally still playing | Ask whether the speaker is on. It is not. So where is it playing? |
| 19 | say it stuck because it was right | Find a counter-example: who guessed right this week and forgot straight away? |
| 20 | struggle because correct guesses are invisible | Offer categories: doors, chairs, floors, light switches. |
| 21 | assume wrong guesses always disappear | Point at the surprise chart. Most of those were wrong and are remembered. |
| 22 | place items by preference rather than memory | Ask the test question: were you still thinking about it after? |
| 23 | recite words without meaning | Ask the listener to ask one question. The question is the check. |
| 24 | mix up surprise and sticking | Use the two test questions: did it shock you, versus did it stay? |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
