# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 46 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: each child names one thing that stuck and one that did not

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: children can report a song continuing after it stopped

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child offers a reason

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group produces at least three right-and-forgotten examples

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group produces at least three wrong-and-stuck examples

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child places at least two items

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child attempts an explanation

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group places at least six examples correctly

**9.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**10.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.

**11.** *(2 points)* it happens over and over and does not stop.

**12.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the group places at least six examples correctly
