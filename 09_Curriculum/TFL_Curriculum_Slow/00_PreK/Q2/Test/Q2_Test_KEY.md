# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 46 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: children predict before being prompted

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child can say what they thought was coming instead

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child has completed the surprise frame

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: children stop or react when the pattern breaks

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: children react to the skip without being told

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child connects the face to an expectation

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every child names the expectation, not only the surprise

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the group names the expectation for at least four surprises

**9.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**10.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.

**11.** *(2 points)* it happens over and over and does not stop.

**12.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the group names the expectation for at least four surprises
