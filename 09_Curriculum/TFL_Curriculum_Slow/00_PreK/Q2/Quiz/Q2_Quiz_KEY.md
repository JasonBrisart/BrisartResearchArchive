# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children predict what comes next in a familiar story. Evidence: children predict before being prompted

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children experience a deliberately broken guess. Evidence: every child can say what they thought was coming instead

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children report the guess an audio break revealed. Evidence: every child has completed the surprise frame

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children predict a rhythm and notice when it changes. Evidence: children stop or react when the pattern breaks

**5.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.
