# Lesson 12 Home Activity — The Clapping Pattern

**Pre-Kindergarten** · Guessing Games · **Quarter 2** · **Optional · Not marked**

> **For a grown-up to read aloud.** Under five minutes, spoken only. Nothing
> comes back to school.

## 1. Remember together

Ask: "What did we say at the end last time?" The answer was: "When your guess is right you never notice it."

## 2. Do this together

Today in class: four pattern breaks, children joining in and stopping when it changes.

At home, do the same thing once, using something you have at home.

## 3. Tell somebody

Ask your child to explain it to another grown-up. Then ask that grown-up to ask
**one question**.

## 4. Say it back

Ask: "What did we say at the end today?"

> "Your hands knew what was coming. That was a guess too."

---

**There are no right answers here.** A child who guesses wrong has done the task
correctly. Guessing wrong is the interesting part.
