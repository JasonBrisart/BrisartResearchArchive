## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — Tracing With The Relation.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student names a falsifiability cost for at least two proposals
- **Lesson 10:** every student marks at least one as partly and justifies it
- **Lesson 11:** every student names an absorbed result for at least two proposals
- **Lesson 12:** every student tests three excluded variables
- **Lesson 13:** every student has specific inheritance in cycles two to five
- **Lesson 14:** every student cites the propagation rule rather than noting a gap
- **Lesson 15:** every student repairs and classifies four traces
- **Lesson 16:** the class handles at least seven of ten correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | treat closure as stubbornness | Run the cost argument concretely with their own proposed variable. |
| 10 | force every variable into full absorption | Praise partial answers: partly is accurate for attention. |
| 11 | state the cost abstractly | Require the specific absorbed result, named. |
| 12 | accept either position without testing | Require the test: name the excluded variable that threatens the gating claim. |
| 13 | produce five well-written independent cycles | Cover all columns but the carried-in one and ask what remains. |
| 14 | say only that a row is missing something | Require the citation: which rule does that break? Name it. |
| 15 | rewrite rather than repair | Require the original to remain visible underneath. |
| 16 | state closure without its cost | Require the cost every time closure is mentioned. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
