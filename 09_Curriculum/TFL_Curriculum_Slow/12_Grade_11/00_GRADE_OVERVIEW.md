# Grade 11

**Course:** Measurement and Rivals · **Stage:** Secondary
**32 lessons of 50 minutes** · **Four quarters**

## What this year owns

Operationalisation, the three rival accounts, and confound control.

> A result consistent with everything discriminates nothing.

**Grade 11 assumes Grade 10.** The student designs a reproducible method and names design blind spots.

## Exit outcomes

- State the testability constraint and apply it to reject a proposal
- Build a seven-line measurement map another person could execute
- State the three rival accounts in their strongest form
- Identify the discriminating condition and whether a design enters it

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **testability constraint** | the framework applies only where all variables can be measured |
| **operationalise** | specify how a variable will actually be measured |
| **uncertainty-only** | persistence reflects unresolved ambiguity |
| **arousal-only** | persistence reflects general activation |
| **habit-based** | persistence reflects repetition |
| **discriminating condition** | the case where the accounts make different predictions |

**Not used at this grade:** Prespecified analysis plans as a formal requirement, gate compliance.

## How this grade is taught

Multi-source synthesis. Conflicting evidence reconciled explicitly, and rival explanations stated in their strongest form.

## How students show they have learned it

Measurement maps, differential tables, quarter quizzes and tests, projects.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "My data fits the framework, so it supports it"

**Why it happens.** Fitting feels like confirmation.

**How to fix it.** Apply the three rivals to the same data.

**What to say.** "Consistency is not evidence for."

### "Measuring intensity means asking how strongly somebody felt"

**Why it happens.** The obvious instrument conflates several things.

**How to fix it.** Show that rating both at once guarantees correlation by construction.

**What to say.** "If they are one judgement, the correlation is an artefact."

### "The rivals are easy to knock down"

**Why it happens.** Students build strawmen without noticing.

**How to fix it.** Have somebody who prefers the rival check the statement.

**What to say.** "If you operationalise the rival, you will operationalise it to lose."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the measurement map with one line worked as an example.

**Extension.** Ask for a published claim in any field that fails the constraint. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
