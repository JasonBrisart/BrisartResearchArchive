## What every expanded lesson contains

1. Lesson at a glance
2. Why this lesson exists
3. Teacher concept guide — plain explanation, two examples, a non-example,
   what the activity shows, what it does not show, scope boundary, epistemic
   status, and a doctrine-versus-inference table
4. What students should already know, with a five-minute recovery activity
5. Vocabulary with definitions, examples, non-examples, misuses, and corrections
6. Materials and preparation, including timing and room setup
7. Student printout — when to distribute, what is completed when
8. Opening retrieval with expected and incomplete responses
9. Hook with exact wording and a failure fallback
10. Teacher script for the explanation
11. Worked example with reasoning steps and invalidity conditions
12. Two guided examples, the second harder, with corrections
13. Executable independent practice with counts, time, and completion checklist
14. Discussion questions with expected responses and licensed conclusions
15. Checks for understanding with a decision rule
16. Exit ticket with a teacher-only key
17. Expected student work at strong, developing, and corrected levels
18. At least four misconceptions with detection and exact corrections
19. Support and extension that do not lower or overshoot the concept
20. Scope and safety notes
21. Homework connection
22. Teacher reflection prompts
23. Sequence with conceptual transitions both directions

## Framework accuracy checks applied

- E(t) never placed inside the integration relation
- E(t) never described as building Sim(t)
- E(t) never described as indicating accuracy
- Belief(t) never treated as a sixth dynamic signal
- Contradiction never described as guaranteed deletion
- No clinical, diagnostic, or therapeutic application
- No classroom activity presented as independent validation
- Epistemic status stated accurately in every lesson

## Terminology release

No lesson uses vocabulary from a later grade. Each lesson's extension section
explicitly re-states the grade's forbidden list to prevent early release.

## Honest data handling

Every lesson, every quarter guide, and every printout provides for results that
do not fit. "Cannot determine" is a valid answer at every grade. No file
instructs a student or teacher to alter a classification.
