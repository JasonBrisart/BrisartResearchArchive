## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — The Mediation Defence.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student writes both and states which variables appear where
- **Lesson 2:** every student states all three denials
- **Lesson 3:** every student names a violating observation
- **Lesson 4:** every student writes a specific deciding observation
- **Lesson 5:** every student states what would settle it
- **Lesson 6:** every student attempts the distinguishing line
- **Lesson 7:** every student marks a divergence for at least two accounts
- **Lesson 8:** the class marks at least four correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | treat the two relations as one undifferentiated system | Draw the separation: integration left, persistence right, no E(t) crossing. |
| 2 | state the gating role without the exclusivity | Ask for the three denials explicitly by name. |
| 3 | state the property without the reason | Ask the consequence: what could it then explain? Everything. Is that good? |
| 4 | resolve the argument in the framework's favour | Leave it unresolved: if the room cannot specify it, position three wins for now. |
| 5 | accept the defence without testing it | Pose the charge directly and require an answer. |
| 6 | omit the distinguishing line | Require an attempt even if weak. A blank is not work. |
| 7 | assume all accounts agree with TFL | Supply one that plainly disagrees. |
| 8 | state gating without exclusivity | Require all three denials every time. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
