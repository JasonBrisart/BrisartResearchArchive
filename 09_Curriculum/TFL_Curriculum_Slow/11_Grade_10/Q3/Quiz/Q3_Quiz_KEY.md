# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 10** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students design an investigation with a reproducible method. Evidence: every student has a checklist with no item requiring clarification

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students run somebody else's method and report where it failed. Evidence: every student receives a clarification list

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students revise a design against reproducibility feedback. Evidence: the second run produces fewer questions than the first

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students execute their repaired design and collect data. Evidence: every student has a dated, contemporaneous record

**5.** *(2 points)* E(t) governs persistence only and contributes to nothing else.

**6.** *(2 points)* E(t) is bounded and cannot escalate without limit.
