# Grade 10

**Course:** The Gate and the Guarantees · **Stage:** Secondary
**32 lessons of 50 minutes** · **Four quarters**

## What this year owns

Exclusive gating, the guarantee set, and reproducible investigation design.

> E(t) is a gate, not an ingredient — and the architecture does not promise everything resolves.

## Exit outcomes

- Write both relations and state which variables appear in each
- State the exclusive gating claim with all three of its denials
- Convert a guarantee into an observation that would violate it
- Design a method a peer can run without asking a question

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **exclusive gating** | E(t) governs persistence only and contributes to nothing else |
| **bounded persistence** | E(t) is bounded and cannot escalate without limit |
| **guarantee** | something the architecture commits to |
| **convergence** | a simulation resolving |
| **reproducible** | a method another person could run unaided |

**Not used at this grade:** The three rival accounts by name, operationalisation as a formal requirement.

## How this grade is taught

Investigation design. A method must be reproducible by a peer without asking the author a question.

## How students show they have learned it

Designed investigations, quarter quizzes and tests, four projects.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "E(t) colours everything"

**Why it happens.** Most intuitive accounts of emotion say exactly that.

**How to fix it.** State the exclusive claim, then require the refuting observation.

**What to say.** "The narrowness is what makes it testable."

### "The architecture promises everything resolves"

**Why it happens.** Models are assumed to be tidy.

**How to fix it.** Show that convergence is conditional with three stated conditions.

**What to say.** "A universal guarantee would be far less defensible."

### "A guarantee is a strength claim"

**Why it happens.** Students read guarantees as advertising.

**How to fix it.** Require the violating observation for every guarantee.

**What to say.** "A guarantee you cannot violate on paper is a slogan."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the guarantee set with the first violating observation worked.

**Extension.** Ask them to argue the mediation defence is an unfalsifiable rescue. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
