## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — The Wrong Part.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student decomposes at least four into connected parts
- **Lesson 10:** every student receives a forward-checked set with at least one marked
- **Lesson 11:** every student decomposes three character moments
- **Lesson 12:** every student produces three forward-checking decompositions
- **Lesson 13:** every student locates at least four planted errors
- **Lesson 14:** every student has three checked self-decompositions
- **Lesson 15:** every student marks at least one as resistant
- **Lesson 16:** the class checks at least six correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | give parts that would not produce the stated moment | Read their parts back: would those give you that moment? |
| 10 | mark everything as fine | Seed one deliberate error per set and say there is at least one. |
| 11 | describe the plot rather than the moment | Ask for the three parts explicitly, by name. |
| 12 | invent detail not visible in the image | Ask what in the picture supports each part. |
| 13 | mark the whole decomposition as wrong | Require them to name which of the three. |
| 14 | produce a narrative rather than three parts | Give the three-line template and refuse prose. |
| 15 | force parts onto a resistant moment | Praise the marked ones explicitly. |
| 16 | skip the forward check | Require the forward check on every item, every time. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
