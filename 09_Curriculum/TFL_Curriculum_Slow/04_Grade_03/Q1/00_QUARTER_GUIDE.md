## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Right Now, Just Now.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student produces one sentence that is not a list
- **Lesson 2:** every final line is a single combined statement
- **Lesson 3:** the class locates the differing line as the remembering
- **Lesson 4:** every student completes five lines in the new setting
- **Lesson 5:** every student completes three same-session reconstructions
- **Lesson 6:** every student names at least one differing line
- **Lesson 7:** every student has three routine reconstructions with genuine final lines
- **Lesson 8:** the class identifies at least four lists correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | join the three parts with commas | Refuse the list: that is three things with commas, give me the one thing. |
| 2 | make the final line a summary of the first three | Model one aloud: mine was — something is different today. |
| 3 | point at the expecting line | Walk it back one step: what made that differ? Look up one line. |
| 4 | reconstruct a classroom moment instead | Require something from the last two minutes, here. |
| 5 | reconstruct from much earlier | Time-box it: only the last five seconds. |
| 6 | produce an identical second reconstruction | Ask what the other person could not see or did not know. |
| 7 | say nothing happened so there is nothing to reconstruct | Offer categories: doors, chairs, stairs, footsteps. |
| 8 | revert to lists under time pressure | Re-run the one-thing test on every disputed final line. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
