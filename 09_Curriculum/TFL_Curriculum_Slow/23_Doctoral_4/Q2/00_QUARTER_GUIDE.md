## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 7 — Chapter Review By Strangers.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 5:** both discussions are checked against the ceiling
- **Seminar 6:** every statement names restrictions with reasons
- **Seminar 7:** every participant receives a question list from a stranger
- **Seminar 8:** the second read produces fewer questions

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | write only the discussion they expect to need | Require both before any result is examined. |
| 6 | state that everything is available without checking | Ask about consent terms, licensing, and institutional restrictions. |
| 7 | select a reader who knows the project | Assign readers across projects. |
| 8 | defend the original wording | Point at the evidence: somebody asked. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
