## Entry check — before session one

**Doctoral Year 4 assumes Doctoral Year 3.** The participant produces referee-quality review and states ceilings both ways.

- [ ] State the inference ceiling for a single study and justify the placement
- [ ] Write a reverse ladder and state whether a null fires the condition
- [ ] Produce a seven-section referee report with located findings
- [ ] Defend an adjudication proposal against four adversarial roles

If a box will not tick, return to `../22_Doctoral_3/` and reteach that outcome before starting. Advancement follows demonstrated readiness, never the calendar.

## Exit outcomes

- Design work that produces value under either outcome
- Maintain a change log detecting specification drift
- Write both discussions before the result is known
- Defend completed work against four roles inside the declared ceiling

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **hedged design** | a study producing value under either outcome |
| **change log** | documentation of every modification and what prompted it |
| **availability statement** | what others can obtain and under what terms |
| **specification drift** | quiet modification until a framework is unfalsifiable |
| **portable competence** | a skill that survives the framework's refutation |

**Not used at this grade:** Governance instruments, institutional stewardship artifacts.

## How this grade is taught

Defence against four assigned adversarial roles. Every claim must stay inside the declared inference ceiling.

## How students show they have learned it

The dissertation, a data and code statement, a defence.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "A null result is a failed dissertation"

**Why it happens.** The positive result is the one imagined during design.

**How to fix it.** A hedged design produces a publishable result either way.

**What to say.** "If your degree depends on the framework being true, the design failed first."

### "The discussion is written after the result"

**Why it happens.** It reports the result, so it feels downstream.

**How to fix it.** Write both discussions in advance.

**What to say.** "The honest version is the one written by somebody who did not yet know."

### "Modifications during the project are just refinements"

**Why it happens.** Small changes feel invisible.

**How to fix it.** The change log.

**What to say.** "Drift never announces itself. The log is the only reliable detection."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the structure with the both-discussions requirement worked.

**Extension.** Ask for the referee report they expect their dissertation to receive. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.

## This grade is gated

The final does not pass unless both gate items are satisfied regardless of total
score: a falsification condition naming a possible observation, and a correctly
identified invalid application with its boundary named. Announce both in session
one; never apply them retroactively.
