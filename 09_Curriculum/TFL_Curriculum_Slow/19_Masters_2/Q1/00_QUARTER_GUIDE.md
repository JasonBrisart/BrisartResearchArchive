## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 4 — The Adjudication Section.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 1:** every outline covers all six sections
- **Session 2:** every list is specific and line-by-line
- **Session 3:** every check carries a finding rather than a verdict
- **Session 4:** every audit names the best rival explanation
- **Session 5:** every pathway is traced rather than accepted
- **Session 6:** every negative verdict names a specific blocking element

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | review by listing what they liked | Require the sections: which section is that in? |
| 2 | write that the design was clear | Require evidence: show me the list, even if empty. |
| 3 | write that measurement is adequate | Require findings: adequate is a verdict. What did you find? |
| 4 | assert the design adjudicates without naming a rival | Require the name: which account also predicts that? |
| 5 | accept the author's claim | Require the trace: show me the result that triggers it. |
| 6 | give unranked revisions | Require ranking: if they fix one thing, which? |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
