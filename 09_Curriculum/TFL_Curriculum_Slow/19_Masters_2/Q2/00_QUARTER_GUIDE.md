## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 10 — The Revised Design.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 7:** every divergence is accounted for rather than defended
- **Session 8:** every student identifies one instance in their own work
- **Session 9:** every point receives a revision or a reasoned refusal
- **Session 10:** both counts are reported and the second is lower
- **Session 11:** every student classifies at least nine correctly
- **Session 12:** the cohort identifies its own weakest section

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | attribute divergence to differing standards without examining it | Ask the source: fact or standard? Which? |
| 8 | claim they have committed neither | Re-read a specific review with them and locate the reasoning. |
| 9 | ignore points they disagree with | Require coverage: every point gets a line. |
| 10 | report improvement without counts | Require both numbers. |
| 11 | classify every weakness as fatal | Apply the test: could any result from this be interpreted? |
| 12 | review only recent sessions | Sample from every section. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
