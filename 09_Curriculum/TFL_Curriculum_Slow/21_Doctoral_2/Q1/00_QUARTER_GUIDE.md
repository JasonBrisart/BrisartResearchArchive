## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 3 — Evidence For A Critique.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 1:** every participant commits to one form as primary
- **Seminar 2:** every target passes an external fairness check
- **Seminar 3:** every claim is evidenced or deleted
- **Seminar 4:** every participant attempts the distinguishing line

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | submit a known limitation as original | Check it against the acknowledged-open list. |
| 2 | weaken the target to make the critique easier | Have the assigned defender rewrite it and compare. |
| 3 | retain an unsupported claim as background | Remove the category. |
| 4 | resolve the charge in the framework's favour | Refuse to rescue it. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
