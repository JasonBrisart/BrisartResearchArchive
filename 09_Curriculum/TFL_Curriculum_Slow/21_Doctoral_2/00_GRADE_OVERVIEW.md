# Doctoral Year 2

**Course:** Original Critique · **Stage:** Doctoral
**16 seminars of 3 hours** · **Four quarters**

## What this year owns

Original critique to publishable standard, with a fairly stated defence.

> Restating that the framework is unvalidated is not a critique.

## Entry check — before session one

**Doctoral Year 2 assumes Doctoral Year 1.** The participant produces a located audit and ranks gaps by dependency.

- [ ] Produce an audit document with textual locations for every finding
- [ ] Classify absences as deliberate, acknowledged, or undocumented
- [ ] Rank gaps by what rests on them and justify the ranking
- [ ] Situate the framework in a literature with evidence for the placement

If a box will not tick, return to `../20_Doctoral_1/` and reteach that outcome before starting. Advancement follows demonstrated readiness, never the calendar.

## Exit outcomes

- Distinguish original critique from status objection and theory preference
- State a target so fairly that a proponent accepts the rendering
- State the framework's strongest available defence, externally checked
- Specify a closure path and give a proportionate assessment

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **specification critique** | a gap that prevents testing, documented |
| **structural critique** | an internal tension between two commitments |
| **adjudication critique** | a rival the framework cannot be separated from |
| **anticipated defence** | the strongest response available to the framework |
| **concession** | narrowing a critique in the face of a good argument |

**Not used at this grade:** Referee reports on manuscripts, adjudication proposals, governance.

## How this grade is taught

Critique must be publishable in form, not merely correct. The anticipated defence must be checked by somebody sympathetic to the framework.

## How students show they have learned it

A critique paper, structured attack contributions, measurement audits.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "A critique is a list of things I dislike"

**Why it happens.** Criticism is the everyday meaning of the word.

**How to fix it.** Apply the originality test.

**What to say.** "What does this stop somebody from testing?"

### "The anticipated defence should be easy to beat"

**Why it happens.** Authors construct the defence they can answer.

**How to fix it.** Require external checking by somebody sympathetic.

**What to say.** "Constructing a weak defence is the commonest failure in critique writing."

### "Conceding means I failed"

**Why it happens.** Concession reads as losing.

**How to fix it.** Score it identically to rebuttal and say so publicly.

**What to say.** "A narrowed surviving critique beats an overreaching one."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide a worked critique in each of the three forms from another framework.

**Extension.** Ask them to submit the critique for comment outside the seminar. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
