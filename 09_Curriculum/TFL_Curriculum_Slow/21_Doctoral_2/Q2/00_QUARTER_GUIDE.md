## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 7 — Responding Or Conceding.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 5:** no participant answers no to all five
- **Seminar 6:** every defence is checked with the objection recorded
- **Seminar 7:** every participant answers or concedes with narrowing
- **Seminar 8:** every author records the gap between intended and received claim

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | dismiss all five | Return it: at least one lands on every design I have seen. |
| 6 | construct a defence designed to lose | Have the defender rewrite it and score against the rewrite. |
| 7 | refuse to concede | Score concession publicly at full value. |
| 8 | explain during the reading round | Enforce silence. The explanation is itself the evidence. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
