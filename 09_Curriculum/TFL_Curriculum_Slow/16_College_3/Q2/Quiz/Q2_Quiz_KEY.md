# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 3** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build a design that separates all accounts. Evidence: the prediction lines differ from one another

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify what would invalidate their result. Evidence: every student addresses all seven rows with reasons

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students attack a design using an assigned rival. Evidence: every design is attacked from every rival position

**4.** *(2 points)* designing so competing accounts make different predictions.

**5.** *(2 points)* stating a rival as its proponents would.
