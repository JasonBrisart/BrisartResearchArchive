# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 3** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students discover their own results fit the rivals equally well. Evidence: every student finds most results discriminate nothing

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state rivals as their proponents would. Evidence: every rival carries a recorded objection from its assigned defender

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build a table in which the accounts differ. Evidence: every student has four distinct rows or an honestly marked coincidence

**4.** *(2 points)* designing so competing accounts make different predictions.

**5.** *(2 points)* stating a rival as its proponents would.
