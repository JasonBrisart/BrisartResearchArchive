## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 21 — Closing The Log.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 17:** every student has a plot showing drop and recovery
- **Lesson 18:** every student compares the pooled number with their prediction
- **Lesson 19:** every student describes a poorly fitting case precisely
- **Lesson 20:** every example is low-stakes and non-clinical
- **Lesson 21:** every student's final matrix uses at least sixteen entries
- **Lesson 22:** every student presents all three elements
- **Lesson 23:** every student sorts at least eight correctly
- **Lesson 24:** the class handles at least nine of twelve correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | describe the recovery as stubbornness | Reframe mechanically: suppressed, then re-entered. Not a personality. |
| 18 | adjust their prediction after seeing the result | Collect predictions in writing before trials begin. |
| 19 | invent a reason the case does not count | Remove the option: you may describe it, you may not dismiss it. |
| 20 | offer a personally distressing example | Acknowledge briefly, do not analyse, redirect to a school or game example. |
| 21 | discover gaps too late to fill | Audit the log at the end of every quarter, not only here. |
| 22 | present the matrix without the disconfirmer | Ask for it in front of the room, every time. |
| 23 | put everything inside because the framework feels universal | Offer the reliable outside items: years, identity, groups, brain wiring. |
| 24 | reintroduce instant deletion | Point at the recovery data every time it appears. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
