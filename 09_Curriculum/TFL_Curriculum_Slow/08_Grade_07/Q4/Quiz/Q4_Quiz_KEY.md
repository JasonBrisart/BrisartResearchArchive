# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 7** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students present a data-backed argument for the central claim. Evidence: every student produces all five parts with genuine content

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students defend their argument against a classmate's challenge. Evidence: every student answers three challenges specifically

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students revise an argument after it was challenged. Evidence: every student revises at least one part and says why

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students confront what sets the threshold. Evidence: every student states what would have to be measured

**5.** *(2 points)* the persistence threshold required for carry-over.

**6.** *(2 points)* whether Sim(t) matched what actually happened.
