# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 7** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students begin a prospective prediction log. Evidence: every student has two entries with intensity rated at writing

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students read and decode the persistence relation. Evidence: every student can read the relation aloud as a sentence

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students reason correctly about threshold direction. Evidence: every student has the direction correct in both threshold rows

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name the three outcomes when intensity fails to clear the bar. Evidence: every student classifies at least six correctly

**5.** *(2 points)* the persistence threshold required for carry-over.

**6.** *(2 points)* whether Sim(t) matched what actually happened.
