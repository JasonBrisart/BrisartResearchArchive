# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 1** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students join an expectation to its source. Evidence: every student has five complete because-sentences

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students apply because to expectations from other lessons. Evidence: every student completes four from other subjects

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify what a stated expectation came from. Evidence: each student sources at least four of six

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students formalise the backward trace. Evidence: every student traces six of their own

**5.** *(2 points)* the proper word for a guess about what happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* the word that joins a guess to where it came from.
