## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — Two Sources.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student has five complete because-sentences
- **Lesson 10:** every student completes four from other subjects
- **Lesson 11:** each student sources at least four of six
- **Lesson 12:** every student traces six of their own
- **Lesson 13:** every student produces three two-source traces
- **Lesson 14:** every student receives a marked trace
- **Lesson 15:** every student repairs at least one trace
- **Lesson 16:** the class catches all three

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | write because I just knew | Push once, every time: knew from what? |
| 10 | think the framework only applies here | Do the first one yourself, out loud, from another subject. |
| 11 | insist there is one correct source | Accept several. Three students traced it differently and all three work. |
| 12 | trace to an event rather than a memory | Ask whether they remembered that, or whether it just happened. |
| 13 | force a second source that is not real | Accept one source where that is honest. Say so. |
| 14 | mark everything as fine | Require one weakest per set. There always is one. |
| 15 | defend rather than repair | Allow one defence per set if they can point at the source now. |
| 16 | accept I just knew under time pressure | Make catching it a game. Plant them deliberately. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
