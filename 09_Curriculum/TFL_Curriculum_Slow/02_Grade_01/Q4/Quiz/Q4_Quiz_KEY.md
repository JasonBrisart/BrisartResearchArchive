# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 1** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students produce a complete traced expectation unaided. Evidence: every student has two complete unaided traces

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students audit their own complete trace. Evidence: every student finds at least one thing to repair

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students show the three parts repeating as a cycle. Evidence: every student's second cycle starts from the first cycle's ending

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students extend the chain to three cycles. Evidence: every student has three connected cycles

**5.** *(2 points)* the proper word for a guess about what happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* the word that joins a guess to where it came from.
