The audit document, and situating the framework in a real literature.

## Exit outcomes

- Produce an audit document with textual locations for every finding
- Classify absences as deliberate, acknowledged, or undocumented
- Rank gaps by what rests on them and justify the ranking
- Situate the framework in a literature with evidence for the placement

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **specification audit** | a documented catalogue of what has not been said |
| **deliberate exclusion** | a scoped absence the specification states |
| **acknowledged open** | something the specification flags as undetermined |
| **undocumented gap** | silence where silence prevents testing |
| **load-bearing** | a claim on which much of the framework rests |

**Not used at this grade:** Referee reports on manuscripts, governance instruments.

## How this grade is taught

Every gap claim requires a textual location. An impression without a reference is not an audit finding.

## How students show they have learned it

An audit document, field-rival comparisons, a standing objection.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "Every gap is fatal"

**Why it happens.** Finding an absence feels like finding an error.

**How to fix it.** Sort into three categories.

**What to say.** "Most of what you found is scoped exclusion."

### "An impression counts as an audit finding"

**Why it happens.** The gap is real, so the reference feels unnecessary.

**How to fix it.** Require the textual location for every claim.

**What to say.** "Without it the finding cannot be checked by anybody else."

### "My field has no account in this territory"

**Why it happens.** The student's literature uses different vocabulary.

**How to fix it.** Require two readings.

**What to say.** "If your field genuinely has nothing, that is itself a finding."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide one worked audit entry with its textual location.

**Extension.** Ask whether closing their strongest gap needs theory, data, or both. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
