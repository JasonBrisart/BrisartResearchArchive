## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 15 — Audit Review.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 13:** every participant records unanswered questions
- **Seminar 14:** every unanswered question produces a logged revision
- **Seminar 15:** every participant defends the top-ranked gap and the placement
- **Seminar 16:** the cohort produces one consolidated register

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | defend by restating the audit | Redirect to the specific challenge. |
| 14 | revise without logging | Require the log. |
| 15 | report an unchanged objection without examining it | Ask: was that about the claim or the status? |
| 16 | treat the register as a course artifact only | Discuss circulation: who outside this room should see it. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
