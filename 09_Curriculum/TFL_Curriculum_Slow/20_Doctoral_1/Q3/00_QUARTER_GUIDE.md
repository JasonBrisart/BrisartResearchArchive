## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 11 — Defending The Placement.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 9:** every account has a favouring observation
- **Seminar 10:** every placement is supported by a divergence or coverage argument
- **Seminar 11:** every participant answers both challenges specifically
- **Seminar 12:** every selection carries a preliminary closure path

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | build only the framework-favouring direction | Require the reverse for every row. |
| 10 | assert novelty without placement | Require one of the three placements explicitly. |
| 11 | defend by restating the placement | Require engagement with the challenge. |
| 12 | select a gap with no closure path | Require a preliminary path before accepting the selection. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
