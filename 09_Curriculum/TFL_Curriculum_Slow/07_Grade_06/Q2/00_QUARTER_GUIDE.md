## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — F(t) Carries Forward.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student has six reasonable estimates
- **Lesson 10:** every student identifies at least five correctly
- **Lesson 11:** every student shows the arithmetic for five estimates
- **Lesson 12:** every student states the order without the chart
- **Lesson 13:** every student's rewritten trace shows F(t) propagating
- **Lesson 14:** every student produces five cross-subject estimates
- **Lesson 15:** every student routes at least four claims correctly
- **Lesson 16:** the class marks at least six correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | treat the window as a precise measured constant | Flag it honestly: that range is a parameter. You will criticise it properly later. |
| 10 | accept the confusion because five seconds feels intuitive | Use the film analogy and ask for the frame rate of a film they know. |
| 11 | guess without calculating | Require the division, written out. |
| 12 | treat the order as arbitrary | Ask the consequence: if Sim came before Minfo, where would it come from? |
| 13 | treat the notation as new content | Show the equivalence line by line with their own old work. |
| 14 | think it only applies here | Do the first one from another subject, aloud. |
| 15 | defend the number by inventing support | Concede: better to admit underspecification than invent support. |
| 16 | revert to treating the window as content duration | Re-run the film analogy on every confused statement. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
