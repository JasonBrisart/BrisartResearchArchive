## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Mapping Across A Lesson.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student has marked their list against the syllabus
- **Lesson 2:** every student has three moments with a specific Sim(t) in each
- **Lesson 3:** every student has written the constraint with an example
- **Lesson 4:** every student maps four with specific Sim(t) lines
- **Lesson 5:** every student maps four consecutive moments
- **Lesson 6:** every student marks at least one as unmappable
- **Lesson 7:** every student names at least two differing lines
- **Lesson 8:** the class sorts at least nine correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | assume every question has a known answer | Name the genuinely open one and say who is working on it. |
| 2 | write a mood in the Sim(t) line | Require content: what specifically did you expect? |
| 3 | describe E(t) as making the expectation stronger or more accurate | Separate the ideas: stronger how? It lasts longer. Not more likely right. |
| 4 | invent detail the account does not support | Ask what in the account supports each variable. |
| 5 | map widely separated moments | Require all four from the current session. |
| 6 | force a Sim(t) onto a vague moment | Praise the marked ones. They map the boundary. |
| 7 | assume one map must be wrong | Remove correctness: both were there, both mapped honestly. |
| 8 | confuse Minfo(t) and Sim(t) | Use the timing test: did that come back, or is it about what comes next? |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
