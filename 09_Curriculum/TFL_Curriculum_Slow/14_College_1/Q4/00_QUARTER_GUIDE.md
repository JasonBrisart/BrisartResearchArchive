## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 25 — The Cards Come Back.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 22:** every condition names a specific observation with controls
- **Session 23:** every student ranks three requirements by feasibility
- **Session 24:** every student compares on all four dimensions
- **Session 25:** every standing objection is converted into a falsification condition
- **Session 26:** the class produces a sorted objection ledger
- **Session 27:** every student knows whether they would pass the gates
- **Session 28:** both gates are satisfied

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | write if we found no support | Reject it firmly every time it appears. |
| 23 | answer with a verdict on the framework | Redirect: not whether it is true. What would settle it. |
| 24 | choose a fully settled framework | Require one that is contested or recent. |
| 25 | dismiss their earlier objection as naive | Defend it: you had no way to know. You checked it. |
| 26 | treat abandoned objections as failures | Score them identically to answered ones, visibly. |
| 27 | ignore the gate items | Mark the gates first, as the real paper will be. |
| 28 | want a verdict from the instructor | Decline to supply one. The absence is the point. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
