# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 1** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the rivals in forms their proponents would accept. Evidence: every rival statement carries a recorded objection from a checker

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students find their own results fit the rivals equally well. Evidence: every student finds that most results discriminate nothing

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students locate where the four accounts come apart. Evidence: every student states honestly whether their design enters it

**4.** *(2 points)* what is currently known about whether a framework is correct.

**5.** *(2 points)* confirmation by researchers unconnected to the author.
