## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 11 — Closure And Its Cost.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 8:** every student routes at least four claims correctly
- **Session 9:** every student writes a specific deciding observation
- **Session 10:** every student produces three genuine reasons
- **Session 11:** every student tests the challenge against specific excluded variables
- **Session 12:** every student identifies fixed parameters as the condition
- **Session 13:** every student produces two genuine violating observations
- **Session 14:** the class identifies its own weakest area

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | defend the number by manufacturing justification | Concede: better served by admitting underspecification than inventing support. |
| 9 | leave the argument certain in either direction | End it unresolved. A certain student has been over-steered. |
| 10 | read a fitting result as support | Apply the rivals immediately and let the disappointment land. |
| 11 | accept either position without testing it | Require the test: name the excluded variable that threatens the gating claim. |
| 12 | state it without the parameter condition | Supply the counter-case: same input, different weights. |
| 13 | recall the list without meaning | Require a consequence for each guarantee named. |
| 14 | review only the most recent sessions | Sample from every session, visibly. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
