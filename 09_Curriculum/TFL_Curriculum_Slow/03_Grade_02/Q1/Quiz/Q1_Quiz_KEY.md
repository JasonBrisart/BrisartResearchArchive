# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 2** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students set two expectations side by side and state one difference. Evidence: every pair states a difference, not just two expectations

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name the memory behind each of two expectations. Evidence: every student names two different memories

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students extend comparison beyond a pair. Evidence: every student names at least two differences

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students compare expectations from different points in one day. Evidence: every student compares two pairs

**5.** *(2 points)* put two things side by side and say how they differ.

**6.** *(2 points)* the thing you point at to show your answer is right.
