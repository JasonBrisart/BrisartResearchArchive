# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 2** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students support a claim by pointing at something. Evidence: every student has four evidenced claims or a justified crossing-out

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students distinguish stronger from weaker evidence. Evidence: every student ranks at least four correctly

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students support a claim about what they remembered. Evidence: every student marks at least one with a question mark

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students gather evidence deliberately before making a claim. Evidence: every student's claim follows from their three observations

**5.** *(2 points)* put two things side by side and say how they differ.

**6.** *(2 points)* the thing you point at to show your answer is right.
