# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 12** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students order their own argument correctly. Evidence: every student has three correctly ordered arguments

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students select one claim narrow enough to be settled. Evidence: every claim is narrow enough for one project

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students write the condition their capstone could trigger. Evidence: every student demonstrates a pathway rather than asserting one

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build the design around the condition. Evidence: every student receives an executability check with unrunnable lines marked

**5.** *(2 points)* a result that would be inconsistent with the claim.

**6.** *(2 points)* a claim built so that no result could contradict it.
