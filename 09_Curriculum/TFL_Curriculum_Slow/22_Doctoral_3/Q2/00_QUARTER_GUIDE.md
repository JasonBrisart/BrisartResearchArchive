## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 7 — Calibration.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 5:** every audit carries findings rather than verdicts
- **Seminar 6:** every audit names licensed rung, claimed rung, and the gap
- **Seminar 7:** every divergence is classified as fact or standard
- **Seminar 8:** every report carries located findings in every section

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | accept a claim of prespecification | Ask for the evidence: where is the registration or timestamp? |
| 6 | only look for overclaiming | Supply an underclaiming manuscript. |
| 7 | attribute divergence to taste | Remove the option. |
| 8 | let a verdict rest on the framework's status | Automatic deduction wherever it appears, and say so in advance. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
