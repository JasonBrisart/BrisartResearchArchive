## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 11 — Proposal Triage.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 9:** every question is narrow enough for one design
- **Seminar 10:** every proposal contains all eight items
- **Seminar 11:** every verdict names the specific failing item
- **Seminar 12:** the second verdict improves, or the reason is stated

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | select the framework as the target | Narrow it until one claim remains. |
| 10 | check their own rival operationalisation | Assign somebody who prefers the rival. |
| 11 | give a verdict without naming the failing item | Require it every time. |
| 12 | rebuild items that were not the problem | Require the named item to be the one rebuilt. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
