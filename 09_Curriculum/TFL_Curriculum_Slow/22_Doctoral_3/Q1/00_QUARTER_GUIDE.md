## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 3 — The Reverse Ladder.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 1:** every participant locates the gap in all three
- **Seminar 2:** every participant argues a ceiling position
- **Seminar 3:** every participant states honestly whether a null fires their condition
- **Seminar 4:** every report covers all seven sections including disclosure

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | assume the authors were careless | Reframe: they were invested. Structural problem, not character. |
| 2 | place the ceiling by preference | Ask for the converging evidence. |
| 3 | neglect the reverse ladder | Require it in writing, every time. |
| 4 | omit the disclosure section | Require it: does the manuscript represent the status accurately? |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
