# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 5** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students recognise routine moments contained many correct expectations. Evidence: every student lists at least eight unnoticed expectations

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students prove an unnoticed expectation existed. Evidence: every student applies the test to at least eight items

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students rank expectations by how strongly breaking them would surprise. Evidence: every student ranks ten and justifies three

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students find unnoticed expectations outside school. Evidence: every student produces ten outside-school items

**5.** *(2 points)* a correct expectation that leaves no trace.

**6.** *(2 points)* when what happened does not match what was expected.
