# Assessment Overview

## Three rules

**1. Prediction accuracy is never graded.** Credit is for recording, reasoning,
and analysing. Say so on day one of every grade — it is what makes honest
reporting possible.

**2. Scope is assessed.** From Grade 7 upward students are tested on what the
framework does *not* cover. Over-application is a scored error.

**3. The loop outranks the vocabulary.** A student who explains the idea in plain
language but forgets a term scores higher than one who lists terms without the
idea.

## Weighting, every grade

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Homework (whichever you set) | 20% |
| Projects (four) | 20% |
| Quizzes (four) | 15% |
| Quarter tests (four) | 20% |
| Final examination | 25% |

## Gates

From Grade 12 upward the final carries two gate items that must pass regardless
of total score: a falsification condition naming a possible observation, and a
correctly identified invalid application with its boundary named. Announced in
advance, never applied retroactively.

Gated: Grade 12, College Year 4, Master's Year 2, Doctoral Years 3 and 4,
Faculty, Field Stewardship.

## Never scored

- Whether a prediction turned out correct
- Handwriting, spelling, neatness, unless writing is the outcome
- Which conclusion the student reaches about the framework
