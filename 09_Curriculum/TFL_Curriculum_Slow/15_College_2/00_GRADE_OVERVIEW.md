## Exit outcomes

- State the confabulation objection and answer it with prespecification
- Write a Sim(t) protocol resistant to all four failure modes
- Build an E(t) instrument independent of the outcome
- Produce a protocol that survives an external run

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **confabulation** | constructing the expectation after the outcome |
| **prespecification** | recording what will be measured before the data exist |
| **blind coding** | scoring by somebody who does not know the condition |
| **construct validity** | whether the instrument measures what it claims to |
| **elicitation** | the exact procedure by which a response is obtained |

**Not used at this grade:** Adjudicating design as a requirement, prespecified analysis plans.

## How this grade is taught

Measurement literacy required. Vague constructs are rejected outright, and a protocol is judged by whether somebody else could run it.

## How students show they have learned it

Protocols, problem sets, a measurement critique, midterm and final.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "Self-report cannot access a 100 ms process, so it is worthless"

**Why it happens.** A serious objection, not an error.

**How to fix it.** Report of content is not report of mechanism — but report is slow.

**What to say.** "If your hypothesis is about timing, self-report cannot carry it."

### "A rating scale measures intensity"

**Why it happens.** It is the obvious instrument and it conflates several things.

**How to fix it.** Show that intensity rated alongside persistence guarantees correlation.

**What to say.** "Which is it measuring — intensity, engagement, or social desirability?"

### "My protocol is clear because I understand it"

**Why it happens.** Authors cannot see their own ambiguities.

**How to fix it.** Hand it to somebody and count the questions.

**What to say.** "You are not the test. The person who runs it is."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the Sim(t) protocol with the verbatim prompt worked.

**Extension.** Ask for a complementary non-report measure and its assumptions. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
