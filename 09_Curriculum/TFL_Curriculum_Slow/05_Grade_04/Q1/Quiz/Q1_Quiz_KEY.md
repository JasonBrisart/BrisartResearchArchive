# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 4** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students compare their sealed Grade 3 prediction with the answer. Evidence: every student has compared their sealed answer with the stated one

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students demonstrate they cannot start a moment fresh. Evidence: every student has attempted a reset and reported the result

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students trace one episode across four moments showing inheritance. Evidence: rows two to four inherit specific content from the row above

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students physically demonstrate inheritance and feel its cost. Evidence: the class can say even a wrong expectation came from somewhere

**5.** *(2 points)* the moment you just had becomes the start of the next.

**6.** *(2 points)* what the new moment receives from the one before it.
