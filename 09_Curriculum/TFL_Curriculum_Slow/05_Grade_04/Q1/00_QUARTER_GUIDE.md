## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Inheritance In A Story.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student has compared their sealed answer with the stated one
- **Lesson 2:** every student has attempted a reset and reported the result
- **Lesson 3:** rows two to four inherit specific content from the row above
- **Lesson 4:** the class can say even a wrong expectation came from somewhere
- **Lesson 5:** every student's trace has specific inheritance in three rows
- **Lesson 6:** every student names specific inheritance at three steps
- **Lesson 7:** every student locates the broken row in at least three of four
- **Lesson 8:** the class audits at least four correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | treat a wrong sealed answer as failure | Reframe publicly: a year ago you had no way to know. You checked it. |
| 2 | claim they cleared it completely | Ask for the mechanism: what replaced it? Where did that come from? |
| 3 | write the last moment in the inheritance column | Refuse the generic answer: which part? Name the actual thing. |
| 4 | rescue a breaking chain too early | Let it break. The difficulty is the lesson. |
| 5 | summarise the plot | Only the inheritance column is read. Say so before they start. |
| 6 | list events rather than inheritance | Ask what each moment handed to the next. |
| 7 | mark the row with the wrong expectation as broken | Separate the two: wrong is fine. Broken means it came from nowhere. |
| 8 | accept generic inheritance | Re-run the naming test on every generic entry. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
