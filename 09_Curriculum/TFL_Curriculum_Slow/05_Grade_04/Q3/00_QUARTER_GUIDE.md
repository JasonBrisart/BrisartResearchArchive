## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 21 — The Chain With A Gap.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 17:** every student has eight rows with inheritance in rows two to eight
- **Lesson 18:** every student receives an audited eight-row trace
- **Lesson 19:** every student repairs from the first break downward
- **Lesson 20:** every student produces six rows on outside material
- **Lesson 21:** every student handles the gap rather than ignoring it
- **Lesson 22:** every student records an unanswered question
- **Lesson 23:** every student has an audited and repaired eight-row trace
- **Lesson 24:** the class classifies at least six breaks correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | split four moments in half to reach eight | Check the content: rows three and four say the same thing. |
| 18 | audit the whole thing rather than finding the first break | One break. After that the trace is unreliable anyway. |
| 19 | repair only the marked row | Ask them to re-read from the repair down. |
| 20 | think it only applies here | Build the first two rows together from the other subject. |
| 21 | treat the gap as a reset | Ask what they woke up already thinking about. |
| 22 | report no question | Brief the listener to ask one. |
| 23 | run out of time before repairing | Time-box the produce step hard. The repair is the assessed part. |
| 24 | confuse wrong with broken | Re-run the distinction on every disputed row. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
