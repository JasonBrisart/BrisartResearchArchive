# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 8** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students write the assumptions underneath their own claims. Evidence: every claim has two genuine assumptions

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify which assumption is weakest. Evidence: every student justifies a weakest assumption for three claims

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students audit somebody else's claim for unstated assumptions. Evidence: every student receives an audit naming at least one per claim

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students connect assumptions to their own measurement choices. Evidence: every student names at least three method assumptions

**5.** *(2 points)* a simulation being modified after contradiction.

**6.** *(2 points)* a simulation being pushed aside rather than deleted.
