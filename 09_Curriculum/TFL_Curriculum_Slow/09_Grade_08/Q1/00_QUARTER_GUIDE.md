## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Running The Sampling.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student has a plot showing drop and recovery
- **Lesson 2:** the class has a pooled number and each student compared it
- **Lesson 3:** every student marks at least one case as unclassifiable
- **Lesson 4:** every student states a minimum detectable gap
- **Lesson 5:** every student has a completed five-checkpoint log
- **Lesson 6:** every student states the limit alongside the classification
- **Lesson 7:** every student states both the rate and the cost
- **Lesson 8:** the class identifies at least four overreaching claims

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | describe the recovery as the class being stubborn | Reframe mechanically: suppressed and re-entered. That is a mechanism. |
| 2 | adjust their prediction after seeing the result | Collect predictions in writing before trials begin. |
| 3 | force a classification on an ambiguous case | Praise the marks: you cannot tell from that description. |
| 4 | claim their schedule could detect any gap | Do the arithmetic with them: you check hourly. A ten-minute absence? |
| 5 | backfill checkpoints | Timestamp each checkpoint. Unmarked entries do not count. |
| 6 | claim recurrence from a rate that could not detect it | Ask for the arithmetic: your gap was shorter than your interval. |
| 7 | propose an impractical rate | Ask who runs it and for how long. |
| 8 | report a result without its rate | Refuse any result that does not state its rate. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
