# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 8** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the exclusions and why they exist. Evidence: every rejection names a specific boundary

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students learn why the clinical application is a misuse, not a stretch. Evidence: every student has a written verbatim refusal

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students resolve genuinely ambiguous scope questions. Evidence: every student resolves at least two by identifiability

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students defend a scope rejection against a challenge. Evidence: every student answers both challenges specifically

**5.** *(2 points)* a simulation being modified after contradiction.

**6.** *(2 points)* a simulation being pushed aside rather than deleted.
