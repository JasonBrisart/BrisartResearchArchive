# Level H — Portfolio Rubric

**Total: 100 points**

---

## GATE CHECK — Applied First

| Gate | Requirement | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Disclosure protocol executable by someone other than the author | Y / N |
| **B** | Scope-enforcement procedure executable by someone other than the author | Y / N |

Both are tested by blind execution in Module 8, not by reading. **If either is N, the portfolio does not pass** regardless of total score.

**Gate A fails if:** the standard statement is not verbatim, contexts are unassigned, or the blind performer could not deliver it.
**Gate B fails if:** the checker has no authority to stop, categories lack worked examples, or the blind performer could not render a verdict.

---

## 1. Disclosure Protocol (10 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Verbatim statement, accurate in both directions; all contexts assigned with named responsibility; omission consequence stated; blind-executable |
| 7-8 | Complete; one context underspecified |
| 4-6 | Statement present but contexts vague, or wording overclaims or underclaims |
| 0-3 | Not executable |

Accuracy means: original, formally specified, falsifiable in principle, not independently validated. **Deduct equally for "established" and for dismissive framing.**

---

## 2. Supervision Plan (20 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | All six obligations addressed; hedged design produces value under refutation; exit path preserves accumulated work; licensing confirmed before derivative work |
| 14-17 | Obligations addressed; hedging or exit path thin |
| 9-13 | Informed entry present but student risk not structurally mitigated |
| 4-8 | Obligations listed without mechanism |
| 0-3 | Absent |

A plan that **declines to supervise**, with reasons and stated conditions for revisiting, is scored on the same scale and can reach full marks.

---

## 3. Scope-Enforcement Procedure (15 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | All three tiers; checker has stopping authority; five categories with worked examples; verbatim standing response for clinical proposals; blind-executable |
| 10-12 | Three tiers; one element underspecified |
| 6-9 | Tier 2 checker lacks authority, or clinical response left to improvisation |
| 2-5 | Categories listed without procedure |
| 0-1 | Absent |

---

## 4. Syllabus (20 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | Disclosure in writing; outcomes phrased as competences surviving refutation; opposition scheduled on the calendar; falsification-before-advocacy as policy; gates stated; scope in the grading scheme; standards alignment in institutional language |
| 14-17 | Six of seven elements strong |
| 9-13 | Outcomes phrased as framework knowledge — the standard first-draft failure |
| 4-8 | Disclosure verbal only, or opposition unscheduled |
| 0-3 | Not defensible to a curriculum committee |

---

## 5. Review Protocol and Brief (15 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | Brief names both failure directions; structure covers all seven sections; conflict declaration addressed; blind test produced a usable review |
| 10-12 | Complete; conflict handling thin |
| 6-9 | Structure present, brief does not instruct against the two failures |
| 2-5 | Requires the author to be present |
| 0-1 | Absent |

---

## 6. Program Memo (10 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Recommendation justified; all five failure modes structurally addressed; **stop conditions name observations** |
| 7-8 | Recommendation sound; stop conditions vague |
| 4-6 | Failure modes acknowledged without structural mitigation |
| 0-3 | No stop conditions — this is single-point dependency with extra steps |

"Single study first" and "decline" are scored on the same scale as "proceed."

---

## 7. Compliance Checklist (included in defense score)

Must be present and complete. An absent or materially inaccurate checklist caps the portfolio at 70 regardless of other scores.

---

## 8. Portfolio Defense (10 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | All seven presentation items covered; withstands all four challenge roles; answers the student-advocate challenge with mechanism rather than intention |
| 7-8 | Strong on most; one challenge role lands |
| 4-6 | Presents the artifacts without defending their logic |
| 0-3 | Cannot account for the portfolio's structure |

---

## Grade Bands

*Applies only if both gates are met.*

| Points | Level |
|---|---|
| 87-100 | Secure — ready to steward this work institutionally |
| 73-86 | Proficient |
| 60-72 | Developing |
| Below 60 | Not yet proficient |

## Re-Assessment

| Gate failed | Required before retake |
|---|---|
| A | Rewrite protocol; pass a second blind execution with a different performer |
| B | Rewrite procedure; pass a second blind execution |
| Both | Both, plus review of Modules 1 and 3 |

## Never Scored

- Whether the participant endorses the framework
- Whether the program memo recommends proceeding
- Whether the supervision plan accepts students
- Institutional prestige, discipline, or resource level
- Writing polish beyond intelligibility

**A portfolio recommending limited institutional engagement, or none, is a complete and passing artifact when its reasoning and procedures are sound.**
