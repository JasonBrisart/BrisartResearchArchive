# Band B — Day 2: Right Now

**Duration:** 30-40 min
**Materials:** Chart paper, notebooks

---

## Objective

Students understand that noticing, remembering, and expecting combine into a single experienced moment.

---

## 1. Hook — The Ambiguous Thing (6 min)

Show something partially obscured, or describe an ambiguous sound.

Example: cover most of an object, leaving a small portion visible.

**Say:**
> "Write down what this is. Don't say it out loud."

Collect written guesses. Read several aloud.

**Say:**
> "Same object. Same amount showing. Different answers."

**Ask:**
> "You all got identical information. Why were the answers different?"

---

## 2. Name — Right Now (8 min)

Write **RIGHT NOW** on the chart, below the three parts.

**Say:**
> "Right now isn't just what you're seeing. It's what you notice, plus what you remember, plus what you expect — all combined into one thing."

Draw it:

```
   NOTICE   ─┐
   REMEMBER ─┼──→  RIGHT NOW
   EXPECT   ─┘
```

**Say:**
> "You don't experience these three separately. You experience one moment. But three things went into building it."

Return to the hook:
> "That's why your answers were different. Same noticing. Different remembering. So a different right now."

---

## 3. Work — Build a Right Now (16 min)

Students pick one specific moment from earlier today and reconstruct it.

**Template:**

```
The moment:        ________________________
What I noticed:    ________________________
What I remembered: ________________________
What I expected:   ________________________
My "right now" was:________________________
```

The last line is the hard one. Model it first:

> "My moment: walking in this morning. Noticed: lights were off. Remembered: the custodian usually turns them on. Expected: someone would be in here already. My right now was — *something is different today.*"

**That final line is a combination, not a list.** Push students toward a single statement.

---

## 4. Compare — Two Students, Same Moment (7 min)

Find two students who chose the same moment (lunch, an assembly, the morning bell).

Put both reconstructions side by side.

**Ask:**
> "Same event. Were their right-nows the same?"

**Say:**
> "The event was shared. The moment wasn't."

---

## 5. Carry — Forward (3 min)

**Say:**
> "So we have right now. Tomorrow: what happens to it when the next moment arrives. Most people guess wrong about this one."
