# Band B — Unit Task Rubric

**Total: 40 points**

---

## Part 1 — The Trace (20 points)

### Component accuracy (10 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | All three moments complete. All five lines filled each time. Parts correctly sorted — memories in the memory line, predictions in the expect line. |
| 6-8 | All three moments present. One or two lines blank or misplaced. |
| 3-5 | Only two moments, or frequent confusion between parts. |
| 0-2 | One moment, or parts not distinguished. |

### Loop reasoning (10 points)

**This is the section that matters most.**

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Moments 2 and 3 clearly inherit from the moment before. The "carried in" line contains real content from the previous moment. |
| 6-8 | Inheritance present but weak in one moment. |
| 3-5 | Inheritance stated but generic ("the last moment"). No specific content carried. |
| 0-2 | Each moment stands alone. No carry-forward shown. |

**Note:** A trace with three independent moments scores 0-2 here even if every line is filled. Carry-forward is the unit.

---

## Part 2 — The Break (10 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Identifies a real mismatch, states both the expectation and the outcome, and shows what was expected afterward. |
| 6-8 | Mismatch and outcome present; the "after" line is vague. |
| 3-5 | Describes something unexpected without stating the original expectation. |
| 0-2 | Blank or unrelated. |

---

## Part 3 — Sticking (6 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5-6 | Identifies what stuck, marks right/wrong, and correctly states that correctness did **not** decide sticking. |
| 3-4 | Complete but the final answer is unclear or hedged. |
| 1-2 | States that being right caused it to stick. |
| 0 | Blank. |

The final line is the load-bearing item. A student who says "yes, because I was right" has the central misconception and should do Micro-Lesson B4.

---

## Part 4 — Scope (4 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 4 | Names something genuinely outside the model — growing up, a long friendship changing, learning something over years, why people are different. |
| 2-3 | Names something outside but explains it weakly. |
| 1 | Names something the loop actually does cover. |
| 0 | Blank, or asserts the loop explains everything. |

---

## Grade Bands

| Points | Level |
|---|---|
| 34-40 | Secure |
| 26-33 | Developing |
| 18-25 | Emerging |
| Below 18 | Revisit Days 3-4 and 7 |

---

## What Is Never Scored

- Whether any prediction was correct
- Spelling, handwriting, neatness
- Length beyond what the lines require
- How interesting the chosen event was
