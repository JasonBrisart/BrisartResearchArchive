# Band B — Quiz 2 ANSWER KEY

**Total: 12 points**

---

**1.** (3 points)

Full credit for any answer stating that being wrong **does not instantly erase** a prediction, and that it can return.

Sample full-credit answers:
- "It doesn't get deleted right away. It came back even after the square."
- "One wrong time isn't enough to change it."

**2 points:** Says the prediction came back, without the point about deletion.

**1 point:** Describes the activity without drawing a conclusion.

---

**2.** (3 points — 1 each for a, c, d; **subtract 1 if b is circled**, minimum 0)

```
Correct: a, c, d
Incorrect: b
```

---

**3.** (3 points)

Full credit requires **yes** plus the surprise test.

Sample full-credit answer:
- "Yes. If the chair had broken I would have been really surprised, and you can only be surprised if you expected something."

**1 point:** Says yes with no reasoning.

**0 points:** Says no.

---

**4.** (1 point)

```
False
```

---

**5.** (2 points)

Full credit: **how much it mattered.**

Accept: how important it was, how strongly it hit me, how much I cared about it.

**Do not accept:** whether it was right, how true it was.

## Re-Teach Triggers

| If many students miss | Re-teach with |
|---|---|
| Q1 or Q2 | Micro-Lesson B3 (Pattern Break) |
| Q3 | Micro-Lesson B2 (Invisible Prediction) |
| Q4 or Q5 | Micro-Lesson B4 (Stuck Versus Right) — highest priority |

Q4 and Q5 test the central distinction of the curriculum. If more than a third of the class misses either, re-teach before the unit task.
