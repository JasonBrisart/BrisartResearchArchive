# Micro-Lesson B1: The Purple Elephant

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Band:** B (3-5)
**Materials:** None

---

## Purpose

Demonstrate carry-forward in under two minutes.

## Script

**Say:**
> "Think about a purple elephant."

Wait three seconds.

**Say:**
> "Now clear your mind completely and think about anything you want."

Wait three seconds.

**Ask:**
> "Hands up if the elephant is still in there somewhere."

Most hands go up.

**Say:**
> "I told you to start fresh. You couldn't. The last moment came with you into this one."

**Close:**
> "That's carry-forward. Nothing starts from zero."

## Follow-Up Question

> "Can you think of a time today when you tried to stop thinking about something and it stayed?"

Keep answers light and school-based. Do not explore anything personal.

## Use

Best as the opening of Day 3, or as unit re-entry after a break.
