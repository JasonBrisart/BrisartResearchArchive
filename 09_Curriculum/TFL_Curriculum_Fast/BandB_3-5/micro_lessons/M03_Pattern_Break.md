# Micro-Lesson B3: Pattern Break

**Duration:** 7 minutes
**Band:** B (3-5)
**Materials:** Index cards, or clapping

---

## Purpose

Show that a broken prediction often returns.

## Script

Establish a pattern clearly — at least four repetitions.

```
circle, circle, circle, circle
```

**Before the fifth, ask:** "What's next?"

Everyone says circle. **Show a square.**

**Before the sixth, ask again:** "What's next?"

Most will say circle again.

**Say:**
> "You were just proven wrong one second ago. And you went straight back to it."

**Say:**
> "Being wrong once doesn't delete a prediction. It starts changing it. It usually doesn't finish."

## Clapping Version

No materials needed. Clap a steady pattern four times, then break the rhythm on the fifth. Students who clap along will do the expected clap before catching themselves.

## Extension

Run the break three times in a row. Ask when students stopped predicting circle. Most need two or three breaks.

**Say:**
> "That's how many times it took to actually change it."
