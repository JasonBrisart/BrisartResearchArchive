# Band D — Quiz 3 ANSWER KEY

**Total: 20 points**

---

**1.** (4 points — 1 each)

1. Stated before data collection
2. Observable with the planned measurements
3. Possible — the design could actually produce it
4. Inconsistent with the framework's claim

---

**2.** (3 points)

Full credit: absence of support is not the same as a contradicting observation. A falsification condition must name a result that could actually occur and that would be inconsistent with the claim. "No support" could result from poor measurement, insufficient power, or design failure.

---

**3.** (4 points)

Full credit requires a **nameable observation with controls**.

Sample: "Stated expectations remain unchanged in content and confidence after repeated, unambiguous contradiction, across multiple trials, with the contradiction verified as detected."

**2 points:** Names the observation without controls or detection verification.
**0-1 point:** "If contradiction doesn't work."

---

**4.** (6 points — 2 each)

```
a) VALID
b) INVALID — developmental trajectory; outside the
   short-timescale scope
c) INVALID — not a clinical model; the framework
   provides no diagnostic criteria or therapeutic
   mechanisms
```

For (b) and (c), 1 point for correct marking, 1 point for naming the boundary.

---

**5.** (3 points)

```
Computational hypotheses.
```

Full credit requires this phrasing or a clear equivalent. Accept "hypotheses about near-future or counterfactual states."

**Do not accept:** "guesses the mind wants," "intentions," "goals."
