# Band D — Day 12: Final Assessment

**Duration:** 50 min

---

## Objective

Students demonstrate integrated command of the formal system.

---

## 1. Orientation (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Two requirements before anything else. You must state a falsification condition, and you must correctly identify an invalid application. Missing either one means the final doesn't pass, regardless of the rest."

**Say:**
> "That's deliberate. Being able to argue against the framework is the outcome."

---

## 2. Final Examination (42 min)

Administer the **Band D Final Examination**.

Students work individually.

**Permitted:** the two relations written on the board.

```
F(t) = w1·Input(t) + w2·Minfo(t) + w3·Sim(t)
Sim(t+1) = Sim(t), if E(t) > θ_persistence
```

**Not permitted:** notes, prior assignments, peer consultation.

---

## 3. Close (3 min)

**Say:**
> "You started this band being able to describe the loop. You're finishing it able to specify it, measure it, test it, and say what would prove it wrong."

**Say:**
> "That last part is the difference between knowing a framework and being able to use one."

---

## End of Band D

Students completing Band D should be able to:

- State and explain both core relations
- Explain why `E(t)` is structurally excluded from integration
- Hand-trace the system across cycles with correct propagation
- State the architectural guarantees and their limits
- Operationalize every variable and parameter
- Articulate uncertainty-only, arousal-only, and habit-based alternatives
- Design a study capable of adjudicating between them
- State falsification conditions
- Correctly reject invalid applications
