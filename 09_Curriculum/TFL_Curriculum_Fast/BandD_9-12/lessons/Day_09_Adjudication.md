# Band D — Day 9: Adjudication

**Duration:** 50 min

---

## Objective

Students design a study capable of distinguishing TFL from alternatives.

---

## 1. Quiz 2 (10 min)

---

## 2. Name — Design Requirements (10 min)

Write what an adjudicating design requires:

```
1. Identifiable Sim(t) content stated in advance
2. E(t) measured, not assumed
3. Ambiguity manipulated independently of intensity
4. Repetition controlled or varied deliberately
5. A prespecified persistence criterion
6. Predictions stated for TFL AND each alternative
```

**Say:**
> "Requirement 3 is what most designs miss. If intensity and ambiguity move together, you cannot separate TFL from an uncertainty-only account."

---

## 3. Work — Study Design (23 min)

Students draft a complete design.

```
RESEARCH QUESTION: ________________________

CONDITIONS
  Condition 1: _____________________________
  Condition 2: _____________________________
  Condition 3: _____________________________

MANIPULATED:  ______________________________
CONTROLLED:   ______________________________

MEASUREMENT
  Sim(t) captured by:  ______________________
  E(t) captured by:    ______________________
  Persistence defined as: ___________________
  Window defined as:   ______________________

PREDICTIONS
  TFL predicts:            _________________
  Uncertainty-only predicts: _______________
  Arousal-only predicts:   _________________
  Habit-based predicts:    _________________
```

The four prediction lines must **differ from each other**. Identical predictions mean the design does not adjudicate.

---

## 4. Compare — Design Critique (5 min)

Take one design and critique it as a class against the six requirements.

---

## 5. Carry (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Tomorrow: the requirement most people skip. What result would make you abandon the model."
