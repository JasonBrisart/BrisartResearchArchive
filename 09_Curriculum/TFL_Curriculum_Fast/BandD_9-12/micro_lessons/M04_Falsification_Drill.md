# Micro-Lesson D4: Falsification Drill

**Duration:** 8 minutes
**Band:** D (9-12)

---

## Purpose

Convert claims into checkable falsification conditions.

## Script

**Say:**
> "I give a claim. You give the observation that would kill it. Ten seconds each."

| Claim | Target falsification |
|---|---|
| `E(t)` governs persistence | Equal persistence rates across measured high/low `E(t)`, ambiguity and repetition controlled |
| Contradiction drives revision | Stated expectations unchanged after repeated, unambiguous contradiction |
| `E(t)` doesn't affect integration | Integration weights shift systematically with measured `E(t)`, inputs held constant |
| Integration is conditionally deterministic | Identical inputs and parameters yield systematically different `F(t)` |

## The Rejection Standard

Reject any answer of the form "if we found no support."

**Say:**
> "Absence of support isn't falsification. Name the observation that would actually occur."

## Quality Test

Ask for each answer:
> "Could someone check that next week with real measurements?"

If no, it's a hedge, not a condition.

## Use

Before Day 10 work and immediately before the final, where falsification is a gate requirement.
