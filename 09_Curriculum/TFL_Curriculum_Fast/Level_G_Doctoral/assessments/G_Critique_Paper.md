# Level G — Critique Paper

**Due:** Seminar 7 · **Points:** 100 · **Weight:** 30%
**Length:** 4000-6000 words

---

## Task

Produce an original critique of one aspect of the TFL specification, to a standard suitable for submission.

**Restating the framework's unvalidated status is not a critique.** Neither is preferring a different theory. An original critique identifies something true about this specification that its author has not addressed and that consequentially affects testability.

## Qualifying Forms

| Form | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| **Specification critique** | A gap that prevents testing, documented with textual evidence |
| **Structural critique** | An internal tension between two of the framework's commitments |
| **Adjudication critique** | A rival from which the framework cannot currently be separated |

A paper may combine forms. It must commit to one as primary.

---

## Required Sections

1. **The target, stated fairly** — the framework's position in its strongest form
2. **The critique** — what is wrong, missing, or inseparable, with evidence
3. **What rests on it** — consequences for the framework's testability
4. **Anticipated defence** — the best response available to the framework, stated fairly
5. **Response or concession** — both acceptable
6. **What would close it** — theoretical work, empirical work, or both
7. **Assessment** — what the framework's standing is if this critique holds

---

## Rubric (100 points)

### Fair statement of target (12)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 10-12 | Strongest form; a proponent would accept the rendering |
| 7-9 | Accurate, slightly weakened |
| 4-6 | Strawmanned or conflates claims |
| 0-3 | Misstates the framework's position |

### Originality and substance of critique (25)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 22-25 | Identifies a genuine, previously undocumented issue with consequences for testability |
| 16-21 | Real issue, consequences thinly developed |
| 10-15 | Restates a known limitation or the framework's own acknowledged openings |
| 4-9 | Status objection, or theory preference |
| 0-3 | No critique advanced |

### Evidence (15)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | Textual location for every specification claim; literature cited where empirical |
| 9-12 | Mostly evidenced; some assertions unsupported |
| 4-8 | Impressionistic |
| 0-3 | Unevidenced |

### Anticipated defence (15)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | Strongest available defence, fairly stated; engages the framework's actual commitments |
| 9-12 | Plausible defence, mildly weakened |
| 4-8 | Weak defence constructed to be defeated |
| 0-3 | No defence considered |

### Response or concession (13)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 11-13 | Either rebuts the defence on stated grounds or concedes explicitly, with the critique narrowed accordingly |
| 7-10 | Response present, incompletely reasoned |
| 3-6 | Asserts without engaging |
| 0-2 | Absent |

**Concession is scored identically to successful rebuttal.** A narrowed, surviving critique is worth more than an overreaching one.

### Closure path (10)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Specifies what would resolve the issue; distinguishes theoretical from empirical work |
| 6-8 | Names a direction |
| 0-5 | Absent or not actionable |

### Assessment (10)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Proportionate — does not convert a local gap into a global verdict, in either direction |
| 6-8 | Reasonable, slightly overreaching |
| 0-5 | Disproportionate to the critique advanced |

---

## Never Scored

Whether the critique favours or damages the framework · which qualifying form is chosen · length within range · writing polish beyond intelligibility
