# Level G — Adjudication Proposal and Defense

**Due and defended:** Seminar 10 · **Points:** 100 · **Weight:** 40%
**Length:** 5000-8000 words

---

## GATE REQUIREMENTS

**The proposal does not pass unless both are satisfied:**

- **An executable adjudicating design** — a competent researcher could run it, and it enters a condition where the accounts make different predictions.
- **A correctly identified invalid application** with the boundary named.

Both required regardless of total score. Checked before any other scoring.

---

## Required Architecture

### 1. The Open Question (10 points)
Stated as a question. Why it is currently open. What is at stake in resolving it.

### 2. Claim and Least-Separable Rival (15 points)
Both in strongest form. Where they agree. Where they must diverge. Why this rival rather than another.

### 3. The Discriminating Condition (15 points)
The cell where predictions differ, specified. Evidence the cell is reachable — not asserted.

### 4. Measurement (20 points)
Full map at publication standard. **Fair operationalization of the rival, stated as its proponents would state it.**

### 5. Predictions (10 points)
For the claim, the rival, and remaining accounts. All must differ.

### 6. Falsification (15 points)
Prespecified, controlled, possible, inconsistent with the claim. Firing pathway demonstrated under the analysis plan.

### 7. Inference Ceiling (10 points)
Highest rung licensed, for both positive and null results. What is explicitly not licensed.

### 8. Scope (5 points)
One invalid application, formally rejected, boundary named, alternative level identified.

## Selected Scoring Detail

### Measurement (20)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | Executable throughout; rival operationalized fairly; construct validity argued rather than assumed |
| 13-17 | Executable; rival operationalization defensible but unargued |
| 8-12 | Rival operationalized in a form its proponents would contest |
| 3-7 | Multiple variables underspecified |
| 0-2 | Not operationalized |

**Automatic ceiling of 10** if `Sim(t)` is not prespecified and checkable.
**Automatic ceiling of 12** if the rival is operationalized in a weakened form.

### Falsification (15)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | Specific, controlled, possible, inconsistent; firing pathway demonstrated against the stated analysis |
| 9-12 | Condition sound; firing pathway asserted |
| 5-8 | Vague but observable |
| 0-4 | Nothing observable, or "no support found" — **GATE A FAILED** |

### Inference Ceiling (10)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Both directions laddered; null result correctly characterized; no global verdict claimed from one design |
| 6-8 | Positive direction handled; null direction thin |
| 3-5 | Ceiling stated but exceeded elsewhere in the proposal |
| 0-2 | No ceiling stated |

---

## Defense (scored within the above)

Eight minutes presenting, eight defending, against four assigned challengers: the rival's advocate, the measurement referee, the inference referee, the scope referee.

A proposal whose author cannot defend the fair-operationalization choice in item 4 loses points on measurement regardless of the written text.

## Never Scored

Whether the proposal is expected to support or refute the framework · choice of claim, rival, or field · funding feasibility · institutional approvability of a hypothetical design

**A proposal designed to refute TFL is scored identically to one designed to support it. The distribution of intentions in a well-run seminar is roughly even.**
