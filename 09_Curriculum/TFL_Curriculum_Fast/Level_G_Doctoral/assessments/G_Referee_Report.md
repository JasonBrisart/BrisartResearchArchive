# Level G — Referee Report

**Due:** Seminar 9 · **Points:** 100 · **Weight:** 20%

---

## Task

Produce a formal referee report on two assigned manuscripts, written to journal standard.

**The governing rule:**

> Review the inference, not the framework. Ask whether the manuscript supports what it claims — not whether TFL is true.

A report that recommends rejection on the grounds that the framework is unvalidated has committed the error this assessment exists to detect.

---

## Required Structure, Per Manuscript

    SUMMARY
      The manuscript's claim, in your words.

    SIGNIFICANCE
      If the result holds, what question does it move?
      Is that question currently open?

    MEASUREMENT
      Construct validity of each variable.
      Prespecification evidence.
      Resolution matched to claim scale.

    ADJUDICATION
      Are rivals stated fairly?
      Is the discriminating condition entered?
      Would the rival's proponents accept its operationalization?

    INFERENCE
      Highest rung the design licenses.
      Highest rung the manuscript claims.
      The gap.

    DISCLOSURE
      Is the framework's status represented accurately?
      Overclaimed / underclaimed / accurate.

    VERDICT
      Accept / minor revision / major revision / reject
      Required revisions, ranked and actionable.

---

## Rubric (100 points, 50 per manuscript)

### Summary accuracy (8)
Demonstrates the claim was correctly understood before it was evaluated.

### Significance judgment (7)
Determines whether the question is open, rather than assuming it is or isn't.

### Measurement audit (12)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 10-12 | All three checks applied specifically; construct validity examined rather than assumed |
| 7-9 | Applied; one area superficial |
| 3-6 | Named but not applied to this manuscript |
| 0-2 | Not performed |

### Adjudication audit (10)
Full credit requires assessing whether the rival's operationalization is one its proponents would accept — the check most reviewers omit.

### Inference audit (8)
Identifies the gap between licensed and claimed inference, in whichever direction it runs.

### Disclosure audit (5)
Assesses the manuscript's representation of the framework's status.

**Automatic deduction of 10 points** anywhere in the report for a verdict resting on the framework's unvalidated status rather than on the manuscript.

---

## Calibration Standard

Your two reports are compared against the instructor's independent review and against peer reports on the same manuscripts. Divergence is expected and is not itself penalized. Divergence traceable to reviewing the framework rather than the work is penalized.

## Never Scored

Whether your verdict matches the instructor's · tone, provided criticism is specific and located · whether you would personally pursue this research
