# Level G Overview — Doctoral

**Seminar title:** Adjudicating an Unvalidated Framework
**Meetings:** 10
**Length:** 3 hours
**Typical placement:** 600/700-level doctoral seminar
**Enrollment:** 8-15. Above fifteen this stops functioning as a seminar; split the section.

---

## What Students Learn

Level F produced a researcher who could test a claim. Level G produces one who can **situate, critique, and contest** the framework at the level of a literature — and who can referee others' work on it.

The level has two deliverables: a critique paper that identifies genuine underspecification, and an adjudication proposal that would move the question.

The organizing premise, stated plainly on day one:

> This framework has not been independently validated. You are not here to learn it. You are here to decide what it would take to settle it, and to do the part of that work that can be done in a seminar.

## Prerequisites

Level F or placement. Graduate standing. Statistics through multiple regression. Familiarity with the student's own field's literature on expectation, prediction, or affect is assumed and used from Seminar 3 onward.

## Readings

Comparison readings are **not supplied by this package**. Instructors select from their own discipline's literature. This is deliberate: adjudication against a package-supplied strawman teaches nothing, and the relevant rivals differ by field.

Minimum: two substantial readings per seminar from Seminar 3 onward, selected by the instructor or proposed by students and approved.

## Assessment Summary

| Instrument | Weight | When |
|---|---|---|
| Seminar contribution | 10% | Continuous |
| Critique paper | 30% | Seminar 7 |
| Referee report | 20% | Seminar 9 |
| Adjudication proposal and defense | 40% | Seminar 10 |

**Gate requirement:** The adjudication proposal does not pass without both an executable adjudicating design and a correctly identified invalid application with the boundary named.

Raise this before work begins, not after.

## Standing Rules

1. Conclusions are never graded — only arguments.
2. Falsification precedes advocacy, without exception.
3. A critique that finds nothing underspecified has not been done carefully.
4. Clinical, diagnostic, and individual-assessment applications are rejected outright.
5. The instructor is not the framework's defender. Assign that role or leave it empty.

## What Carries Into Level H

Students finishing Level G should be able to:

- Conduct a specification audit and document genuine gaps
- Adjudicate against the actual rivals in their own field
- Evaluate measurement at publication standard
- Distinguish what a design supports from what its author claims
- Produce referee-quality review
- Build a proposal that would move an open question
