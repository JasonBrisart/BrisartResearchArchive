# Micro-Lesson C1: Sharpen the Sim

**Duration:** 8 minutes
**Band:** C (6-8)
**Materials:** None

---

## Purpose

Train the identifiability rule for `Sim(t)`.

## Script

Put a vague statement on the board.

```
"I had a weird feeling about today."
```

**Ask:**
> "Is that a `Sim(t)`?"

No. There's no content.

**Say:**
> "Simulation content must be identifiable. If you can't state what you expected, the model doesn't apply."

## Rapid Round

Read statements. Students call out SIM or NOT SIM.

| Statement | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I thought it'd rain by lunch." | SIM |
| "Something felt off." | NOT SIM |
| "I expected her to say no." | SIM |
| "I was nervous." | NOT SIM |
| "I figured the door would be locked." | SIM |
| "I had a bad vibe." | NOT SIM |

## Close

> "The vague ones aren't failures. They're just outside what this model covers. Knowing that is the skill."

## Use

Before any study-design work. Prevents the most common Band D error.
