# Band C — Day 6: Contradiction

**Duration:** 45 min
**Materials:** Index cards with patterns

---

## Objective

Students observe that contradiction drives revision or suppression rather than instant deletion.

---

## 1. Hook — The Pattern Trial (8 min)

Run a clean pattern-break trial.

```
Cards 1-5:  circle × 5
Card 6:     square
Cards 7-9:  circle × 3
```

Poll predictions before cards 6, 7, and 8. Record class percentages on the board.

Typical result: prediction of "circle" drops after the square, then climbs back.

---

## 2. Name — What Contradiction Does (12 min)

Write the definition:

```
CONTRADICTION
Mismatch between Sim(t) and Input(t)
that produces measurable update effects,
including revision or suppression.
```

**Emphasize the verbs:**
> "Revision. Suppression. Not deletion. Not erasure."

Write the three outcomes:

```
After contradiction, a simulation may:
  - be revised (modified)
  - be suppressed (pushed aside)
  - be replaced by an alternative
```

**Point at the board data:**
> "Your numbers dropped and then recovered. That's suppression followed by return, not deletion."

---

## 3. Work — Multi-Break Trials (15 min)

In pairs, students run a trial with **three** breaks and track how many it takes to change the prediction durably.

```
Break 1 → did prediction change?  Y / N
Break 2 → did prediction change?  Y / N
Break 3 → did prediction change?  Y / N

Number of breaks before durable change: ____
```

Pool the class numbers.

---

## 4. Compare — The Count (8 min)

**Ask:**
> "How many contradictions did it take?"

Answers will cluster above one.

**Say:**
> "One contradiction starts the update. It usually doesn't complete it. That's a testable claim and you just tested it."

---

## 5. Carry (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Tomorrow: why the old prediction came back at all. It has a name."

---

## Assessment

Administer **Quiz 2** at the start of Day 7.

## Instructor Notes

- Recording percentages before each card makes the recovery visible as data rather than impression.
- If a class shows durable change after one break, note it honestly. It is a legitimate result and Band D students would call it evidence requiring adjudication.
