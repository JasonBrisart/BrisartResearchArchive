# Band C — Day 9: System Dynamics

**Duration:** 45 min
**Materials:** Chart paper

---

## Objective

Students identify three characteristic system behaviors over time.

---

## 1. Hook — Sort Yesterday's Data (6 min)

**Say:**
> "Day 7 you found three patterns — persistent, recurrent, resolved. Today those get proper names and causes."

---

## 2. Name — Three Dynamics (14 min)

Write all three:

```
RAPID RESOLUTION
  low persistence, high turnover
  → E(t) below threshold, Sim(t) matches Input(t)

SUSTAINED RECURRENCE
  persistent simulation carry-over
  → E(t) above threshold, no resolving input

ESCALATION
  repeated simulation under unresolved contradiction
  → E(t) high, contradiction present, no resolution
```

**Say:**
> "These aren't three different systems. They're the same loop under different conditions."

**For escalation, add the boundary immediately:**
> "Escalation here means repeated simulation while a contradiction stays unresolved. It's a description of system behavior. It is not a description of any condition, diagnosis, or disorder, and we don't use it that way."

---

## 3. Work — Dynamic Identification (17 min)

Students classify eight scenarios.

Provide scenarios like:

1. Expected the bus at 7:45, it came at 7:45, forgot about it. → *rapid resolution*
2. Expected a test result all week, no result came. → *sustained recurrence*
3. Expected a game to be cancelled, got mixed messages all day. → *escalation*

Students classify each and **state which variable condition drives it**:

```
Scenario 4: _______________
Dynamic:    _______________
Because E(t) is _____ and contradiction is _____
```

Then students supply two scenarios of their own, one for each of two different dynamics.

---

## 4. Compare — The Missing Piece (6 min)

**Ask:**
> "What would turn a sustained recurrence into a rapid resolution?"

Answers: resolving input arrives, or `E(t)` decays below threshold.

**Say:**
> "Both are in the model. A simulation resolves through congruence with input, through contradiction-driven update, or through affective decay below threshold."

---

## 5. Carry (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Last session: what this model is *not* for. That's assessed."
