# Level F — Final Study Design

**Due and defended:** Session 12 · **Points:** 100 · **Weight:** 45% of course grade

---

## GATE REQUIREMENT

**The design does not pass unless a competent researcher could execute the measurement map without asking the author a clarifying question.**

This is checked first, before any other scoring.

---

## Required Contents

### 1. Claim and Status (5 points)
The specific TFL claim under test. One sentence on the framework's epistemic status.

### 2. Measurement Map (25 points)

    Input(t)      → measured by:
    Minfo(t)      → measured by:
    Sim(t)        → measured by:   (prespecified, checkable, blind-scored)
    E(t)          → measured by:   (measured, bounded, arousal separated)
    F(t)          → measured by:   (independent of its components)
    θ_persistence → defined as:
    Window        → defined as:

Plus the `Sim(t)` protocol with three worked examples, including one unscoreable.

### 3. Resolution Declaration (8 points)

    CLAIM SCALE:
    OBSERVATION RESOLUTION:
    WHAT THIS DESIGN CANNOT SEE:
    CONCLUSIONS I AM NOT ENTITLED TO DRAW:

### 4. Design and Conditions (15 points)
Conditions, manipulated variables, controlled variables, and the discriminating condition identified explicitly.

### 5. Differential Predictions (15 points)

| Account | Predicts | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Uncertainty-only | | |
| Arousal-only | | |
| Habit-based | | |
| TFL | | |

The four must differ.

### 6. Confound Table (10 points)
All seven standard confounds, with controls and residual risk.

### 7. Analysis Plan (12 points)
All ten prespecified items.

### 8. Falsification Condition (10 points)

    CLAIM:
    FALSIFIED IF:
    HOW THIS COULD FIRE UNDER THE ANALYSIS PLAN:

## Scoring Detail

### Measurement Map (25)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 22-25 | All seven lines executable; `Sim(t)` prespecified and blind-scored; `E(t)` measured with arousal separated; `F(t)` independent; three worked examples present |
| 17-21 | Complete; one or two lines requiring inference |
| 11-16 | `Sim(t)` vague, or `E(t)` assumed rather than measured |
| 5-10 | Multiple lines missing |
| 0-4 | Not operationalized |

**Automatic ceiling of 14** if `Sim(t)` is not stateable and checkable. An unmeasurable simulation invalidates the study by the framework's own constraint.

**Automatic ceiling of 18** if `F(t)` is measured by an instrument that is one of its own components.

### Differential Predictions (15)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | Four accounts genuinely distinct; discriminating condition entered by the design |
| 9-12 | Accounts distinct; discriminating condition weakly reached |
| 5-8 | Two or more accounts make identical predictions |
| 0-4 | Rivals not addressed |

### Falsification Condition (10)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Specific observation, controlled, possible, inconsistent; firing pathway demonstrated |
| 6-8 | Observation named; firing pathway asserted rather than shown |
| 3-5 | Vague but observable |
| 0-2 | "No support found" or nothing observable |

## Re-Assessment

Gate failure permits one revision within two weeks, addressing every clarification line identified by the instructor. The revised design is scored normally; the gate is re-checked first.

## Never Scored

Whether the design would vindicate or refute the framework · topic selection · funding feasibility · sample size ambition · writing polish beyond intelligibility

**A design whose author expects it to refute TFL is scored identically to one expecting support. Several of the strongest submissions each term are of the first kind.**
