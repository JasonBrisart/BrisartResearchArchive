# Level F — Session 7: Designing to Adjudicate

**Duration:** 100 min

## Objective

Students build a design in which TFL and its three rivals make different predictions.

---

## 1. Frame — Consistency Is Not Evidence (12 min)

**Say:**
> "In Level E your own data fit the framework and also fit two rivals. A result consistent with everything discriminates nothing. Today you build the condition where they disagree."

---

## 2. Build — The Six Requirements (32 min)

    AN ADJUDICATING DESIGN REQUIRES:

      1. Identifiable Sim(t) content, prespecified
      2. E(t) measured, not assumed
      3. Ambiguity manipulated independently of intensity
      4. Repetition controlled or deliberately varied
      5. A prespecified persistence criterion
      6. Predictions stated in advance for TFL AND each rival

**Say:**
> "Requirement 3 is what most designs miss. If intensity and ambiguity move together — and in natural settings they usually do — you cannot separate TFL from an uncertainty-only account no matter how good your measurement is."

Work the discriminating condition explicitly:

    Low ambiguity + high measured intensity + novel content

    Uncertainty-only predicts: no persistence (nothing unresolved)
    Arousal-only predicts:     persistence only if general activation high
    Habit-based predicts:      no persistence (content is novel)
    TFL predicts:              persistence if E(t) > θ

**Say:**
> "That cell is where the accounts come apart. Any design that never enters it is not adjudicating, whatever else it does well."

---

## 3. Contest — Is the Discriminating Cell Reachable? (25 min)

- **Student A:** Low ambiguity with high intensity is nearly empty in natural settings — people don't care much about things that are already settled. The cell is a theoretical artifact.
- **Student B:** It is producible experimentally. Contrived stakes with unambiguous content is a standard laboratory construction.

**Say:**
> "A's point matters for generalization even if B is right about feasibility. If the discriminating condition is rare in the world, a laboratory result there tells you about the mechanism but little about typical operation. Address that in your limitations, not by pretending it isn't true."

---

## 4. Apply — Full Design Draft (26 min)

    RESEARCH QUESTION:

    CONDITIONS
      Condition 1:
      Condition 2:
      Condition 3:

    MANIPULATED:
    CONTROLLED:
    MEASURED:

    PREDICTIONS
      TFL predicts:
      Uncertainty-only predicts:
      Arousal-only predicts:
      Habit-based predicts:

**The four prediction lines must differ from each other.** Identical predictions mean the design does not adjudicate and the draft is returned.

---

## 5. Propagate (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Next: the audit that kills most of these. Bring the draft."

---
