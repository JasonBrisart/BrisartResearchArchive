# Materials and Setup

Everything needed to run this curriculum. No specialized equipment is required.

## Universal Materials

Used across all bands:

- Whiteboard or chart paper
- Markers
- Student notebooks or loose paper
- A timer or clock with a second hand
- Index cards

That is the complete required list.

## Sound-Based Activities

Several lessons use audio mismatch — a familiar sound stopped early, or replaced.

**Options, in order of preference:**

1. A phone or computer with any audio playback
2. A physical instrument (bell, chime, shaker)
3. Instructor voice — a familiar song stopped mid-phrase works perfectly

The instrument is irrelevant. The break is the content.

## Pattern and Sequence Activities

Bands C and D use pattern-then-break trials.

**Materials:** Index cards with shapes, colors, or numbers. Prepare sequences in advance.

Example set for a contradiction trial:

```
Trial 1-4:  circle, circle, circle, circle
Trial 5:    square
Trial 6-8:  circle, circle, circle
```

The return to circles after the break is what produces observable recurrence.

## The Carry-Forward Chain

**Materials:** One small object to physically pass (a ball, an eraser, anything).

The physical handoff is the instructional point. Students who hold the object must repeat what they received before adding their own prediction.

## Logging and Journals

Bands B, C, and D use multi-day logs.

**Options:**

- Printed table templates (provided in each band's assessments folder)
- A dedicated notebook page
- A shared class document

Printed templates are recommended for Band B, where the column structure itself is scaffolding.

## Room Setup by Activity Type

| Activity type | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| Hook and demonstration | Whole group facing front |
| Carry-forward chain | Line or circle |
| Partner contradiction drill | Pairs |
| Trace work | Individual desks |
| Compare and discuss | Whole group |

## Advance Preparation Time

| Band | Prep per lesson |
|---|---|
| A | 5 minutes |
| B | 5-10 minutes |
| C | 10-15 minutes |
| D | 15-20 minutes |

Band D preparation is primarily reviewing the formal relations before class, not assembling materials.

## Preparing the Engineered Mismatch

Most lessons need one. The rules:

1. Establish a pattern at least three times before breaking it.
2. Make the break unambiguous — students should not wonder whether it happened.
3. Return to the original pattern afterward, so recurrence can be observed.
4. Never make the break embarrassing for any individual student.

## Digital Alternatives

Everything in this curriculum runs offline with paper and voice. If digital tools are available they may speed up logging, but no activity requires them.
