# Micro-Lesson A1: The Frozen Guess

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Band:** A (K-2)
**Materials:** None
**Use as:** Warm-up, transition filler, or unit re-entry after a break

---

## Purpose

Catch a prediction that students never chose to make.

## Script

Stop the class abruptly during any transition.

**Say:**
> "Freeze. Don't look. What's behind you?"

Take three answers.

**Say:**
> "Nobody looked. Everybody had an answer. Your brain already guessed."

**Ask one student:**
> "How did you know?"

Accept any answer involving having been there before.

**Close:**
> "That's remembering turning into expecting. It happens all day."

## Variations

- "Don't look at the clock. What time is it?"
- "Eyes closed. Who's standing at the door?"
- "What's the weather doing right now?"

## Watch For

Students who say "I don't know" — offer two choices instead. Choices unlock participation for students who will not guess freely.
