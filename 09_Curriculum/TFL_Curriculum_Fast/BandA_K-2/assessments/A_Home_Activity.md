# Band A Home Activity

**Format:** Oral. No writing required. No return submission.

---

## Note to Families

Your child has been learning that we are always guessing what happens next, and that what happens changes the next guess.

There is nothing to fill out and nothing to send back. These are conversations.

---

## Activity 1 — The Guess Before

Before opening a door, before turning a page, or before a meal, ask:

> "What do you think is on the other side?"

Then find out together.

**It does not matter whether they are right.** Ask instead:

> "What made you think that?"

That question is the whole activity.

---

## Activity 2 — Finish It Wrong

Start a phrase your child knows well and end it wrong.

> "Twinkle, twinkle, little... sandwich."

They will object. Then ask:

> "What were you expecting?"

This is how we catch a guess — by breaking it.

---

## Activity 3 — What Stuck?

At the end of the day, ask two questions:

> "What's something from today you're still thinking about?"

Then the harder one:

> "What's something from today you completely forgot about until now?"

Both happened. One stuck and one didn't. That's the idea.

---

## Activity 4 — The Never-Stops Question

Ask at any quiet moment:

> "What do you think happens in the next minute?"

They will have an answer. They always do.

> "See? You were already guessing."

---

## What to Avoid

Please keep these conversations light and everyday — routines, stories, weather, food, games.

This is not about feelings, worries, or anything personal. If your child brings up something upsetting, just listen normally. It is not part of the activity.

---

## One Sentence to Remember

If your child can say this, they have the whole idea:

> "My brain is always guessing what happens next, and what happens changes the next guess."
