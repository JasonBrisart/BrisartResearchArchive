# Band A Final Task: Draw the Loop

**When:** End of Day 6
**Duration:** 5-8 minutes
**Materials:** Paper, crayons or pencils

---

## Student Instructions

Read aloud:

> "Draw the circle we walked today. Put in the words you remember. Use arrows to show which way it goes."

That is the entire prompt. Do not give an example drawing — it will be copied.

## What Students May Include

- Words: notice, remember, expect, surprise, stuck
- Arrows showing direction
- Pictures instead of words
- Themselves inside the loop

All are acceptable.

## Scoring

Score on one dimension only: **does the drawing show a cycle?**

| Level | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Secure** | Arrows return to the start. Two or more words or pictures placed around it. |
| **Developing** | Words or pictures present, arrows go one direction only, or no arrows. |
| **Beginning** | Unrelated drawing, or blank. |

**Do not score:**
- Spelling
- Drawing quality
- Word count
- Whether all four words appear

A student who draws two pictures connected by a returning arrow has demonstrated the core outcome. A student who writes all four words in a straight line has not.

## Follow-Up

For students at "Developing": sit with the drawing and ask,

> "What happens after this one?"

Point at the last item. Most students will indicate a return to the start verbally even when they did not draw it. Mark as Secure if they can show it, and note that the gap was drawing, not understanding.

## Sending Work Home

These drawings work well sent home with a one-line note:

> "Ask your child to explain their circle. They can show you how it goes around."
