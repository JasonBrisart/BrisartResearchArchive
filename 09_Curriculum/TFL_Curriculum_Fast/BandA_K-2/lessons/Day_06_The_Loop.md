# Band A — Day 6: The Loop

**Grades:** K-2
**Duration:** 25 minutes
**Materials:** Chart paper, one small object to pass, drawing paper

---

## Objective

Students will assemble the four words into a cycle and demonstrate that it repeats.

## Success Looks Like

Each student can act out or draw the cycle and indicate that it starts again.

---

## 1. Hook — All Four Words (3 min)

Point at the chart with all four words.

**Say:**
> "Notice. Remember. Expect. And sometimes — surprise."

**Say:**
> "Today we find out how they fit together. And there's a trick at the end."

---

## 2. Name — The Circle (6 min)

Draw a large circle on chart paper. Place the words around it:

```
        NOTICE
       /      \
 EXPECT        REMEMBER
       \      /
      (and around again)
```

Walk it with your finger while narrating:

> "You notice something. That brings back a memory. The memory gives you an expectation. Then the next thing happens — and you notice *that*."

Trace the circle again.

**Say:**
> "And around it goes. It doesn't stop. It's going right now while I'm talking."

---

## 3. Work — Walk the Loop (10 min)

**Part A — Physical loop (5 min)**

Set up three stations in a triangle: NOTICE, REMEMBER, EXPECT.

Students walk the triangle in small groups, saying one real thing at each station.

Example as a student walks:
- At NOTICE: "I notice the window."
- At REMEMBER: "I remember it was sunny."
- At EXPECT: "I expect we go outside."

Then send them back to NOTICE and say:
> "Go again. But start from what you just expected."

The second lap is the point. It demonstrates that the loop feeds itself.

**Part B — The passing chain (5 min)**

Students sit in a circle with one small object.

Whoever holds the object says one thing they expect in the next minute, then passes it.

The next student must **repeat what they heard** before adding their own.

**Say:**
> "See that? You had to carry the last one forward before you could add yours. That's what your brain does all day."

---

## 4. Compare — Does It Ever Stop? (4 min)

**Ask:**
> "When does the circle stop?"

Let them work on it. Some will say when you sleep, or when nothing happens.

**Say:**
> "Even right now, while you're sitting still, you're expecting something. What do you expect happens in about one minute?"

They will all have an answer.

**Say:**
> "See? Still going."

---

## 5. Carry — Final Drawing (2 min)

Students draw their own loop with arrows and the words they remember.

Collect these. They are the Band A assessment artifact.

---

## Instructor Notes

- The second lap in Part A is the essential moment of the entire band. Make sure every group does it.
- If the passing chain breaks down because students forget what to repeat, slow it down and coach. The difficulty *is* the lesson — carrying forward takes effort.
- Accept any drawing that shows arrows returning. Word accuracy is secondary to the cycle.

## Variations

**Seated version:** Point at the three stations on the wall instead of walking.

**Shortened version:** Skip Part A and do only the passing chain. It carries the concept on its own.

**Extension for advanced students:** Ask what happens to the loop when a surprise occurs. Accept anything suggesting the expectation changes for next time.

---

## End of Band A

Students who complete this band should be able to name something they noticed, remembered, and expected; report a surprise and what they expected instead; and show that the cycle repeats.

Band B begins by adding **right now** and **carry forward**.
