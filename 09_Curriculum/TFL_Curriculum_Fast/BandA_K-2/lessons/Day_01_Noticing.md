# Band A — Day 1: Noticing

**Grades:** K-2
**Duration:** 20-25 minutes
**Materials:** Chart paper, markers

---

## Objective

Students will identify things they notice right now, and discover that they were already expecting things without trying.

## Success Looks Like

By the end, each student can name at least one thing they noticed in the room.

---

## 1. Hook — The Frozen Question (4 min)

Before saying anything about the unit, stop the class mid-transition.

**Say:**
> "Freeze. Don't look at the door. What do you think the hallway sounds like right now?"

Take three or four answers. Students will offer: quiet, footsteps, someone talking.

**Then say:**
> "Interesting. Nobody looked. But everybody had an answer."

Pause and let that sit.

**Say:**
> "That's what this whole unit is about. Your brain was already guessing, and nobody asked it to."

---

## 2. Name — Noticing (6 min)

**Say:**
> "Today we're going to practice one word. The word is **notice**."

Write **NOTICE** on chart paper.

**Say:**
> "Noticing is what you see, hear, or feel right now. Not before. Not later. Right now."

Run a quick round-robin. Each student names one thing they notice.

**Prompt if they stall:**
- "What do you hear?"
- "What can you feel with your feet?"
- "What color is closest to you?"

Write six to eight student answers on the chart under NOTICE.

**Correction to expect:** Students will offer things from earlier in the day ("I noticed my breakfast"). Redirect gently:
> "That's remembering. We'll do that one tomorrow. What are you noticing *right now*?"

---

## 3. Work — The Noticing Hunt (8 min)

Students move around the room (or stay seated and turn) and find three things they can notice with three different senses.

**Structure:**
```
One thing I can SEE:
One thing I can HEAR:
One thing I can FEEL:
```

For K, do this orally in pairs. For grades 1-2, students may draw or write.

Circulate and ask individuals: "Which sense is that one?"

---

## 4. Compare — What We Missed (5 min)

Bring the group back.

**Say:**
> "Raise your hand if you noticed something nobody else said."

Collect two or three. Add to the chart.

**Key question:**
> "Was that thing there the whole time?"

Students will say yes.

**Say:**
> "So it was there, and you weren't noticing it, and then you were. Noticing changes even when the room doesn't."

---

## 5. Carry — One Sentence Forward (2 min)

**Say:**
> "Here's the thing to hold onto until next time. When I asked about the hallway, you all had a guess. You didn't decide to guess. It was just there."

**Say:**
> "Next time we're going to find out where those guesses come from."

---

## Instructor Notes

- The hook works best if it is genuinely unexpected. Do not announce the unit first.
- If a student says "I don't know" during round-robin, offer a choice: "Do you hear the fan or the hallway?" Choices unlock participation.
- Do not correct students who describe something imaginary. Ask: "Is that something you're noticing now, or something you're thinking about?" Let them sort it.

## Variations

**If the hallway is silent:** Use the cafeteria, the playground, or outside the window instead.

**For sensory-sensitive students:** The hunt can be done entirely by sight from one seat.

**Extension:** Ask students to notice one thing they usually never notice. This previews Day 5's idea that attention is selective.
