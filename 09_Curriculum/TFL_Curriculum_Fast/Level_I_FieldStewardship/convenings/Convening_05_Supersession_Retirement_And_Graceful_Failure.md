# Level I — Convening 5: Supersession, Retirement, and Graceful Failure

**Duration:** Full day
**Artifact:** Retirement policy

## Objective

Participants specify in advance the conditions under which the framework's
examination should conclude, and how that conclusion is executed without
destroying the record.

---

## 1. Frame — The Convening Nobody Schedules (45 min)

**Say:**

> "Research programmes rarely end. They thin out, lose funding, and leave a
> literature nobody can interpret. Ending well is a governance function and it
> has to be specified before anyone is invested in the outcome."

**Say:**

> "You are writing this today precisely because today nobody's career depends on
> the answer."

---

## 2. Build — Three Endings (120 min)

    SUPERSESSION
      A better-specified successor accounts for the same phenomena.
      The framework is not refuted; it is absorbed.
      Record remains interpretable and is carried forward.

    REFUTATION
      Prespecified falsification conditions fire under adequate
      controls, replicated across sites.
      Record becomes evidence of a settled question.

    ABANDONMENT
      Neither support nor refutation accumulates; the programme
      becomes unfundable or uninteresting.
      This is the most common ending and the most damaging,
      because the record decays without a verdict.

**Say:**

> "Abandonment is the failure mode your policy exists to prevent. Refutation is a
> result. Drift is a loss."

Then the conditions:

    A RETIREMENT POLICY MUST SPECIFY
      Observations that would constitute refutation
      Conditions constituting supersession
      A dormancy trigger — how long without accumulation
      Who declares each
      What is preserved regardless of which occurs
      What claims may still be made afterward

---

## 3. Contest — Can a Body Retire Something It Does Not Own? (90 min)

- **Participant A:** A governance body cannot retire a framework it did not
  author. The policy is theatre.
- **Participant B:** The body retires the *field's programme of examination*, not
  the framework. The author's work persists; the coordinated effort concludes.

**Say:**

> "B's distinction has to be written into your policy in the first paragraph. A
> retirement policy that reads as terminating someone else's work will not be
> adopted and should not be."
