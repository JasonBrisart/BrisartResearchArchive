# Level I — Convening 3: Multi-Institution Replication

**Duration:** Full day
**Artifact:** Consortium protocol

## Objective

Participants build a protocol under which independent institutions can produce a
comparable and interpretable replication record.

---

## 1. Frame — Comparable or Merely Numerous (45 min)

**Say:**

> "Ten studies that cannot be compared are not ten studies. They are ten
> anecdotes with statistics attached. The difference is decided before any data
> are collected."

---

## 2. Build — What Must Be Held Common (120 min)

    LOCKED ACROSS SITES
      The claim under test
      The persistence criterion, including partial matches
      The prespecified falsification condition
      The rival accounts and their fair operationalizations
      The primary outcome and analysis path

    ADAPTABLE BY SITE
      Task materials and stimuli
      Feature mappings and retrieval functions
      Equivalent measurement channels
      Local ethics and recruitment procedures

    PRESERVED BY EVERY SITE
      Nulls, failed runs, collapse points
      Deviations from protocol, with reasons
      Unscoreable-response rates

**Say:**

> "The adaptable column is deliberate. A protocol that dictates local
> implementation will not be adopted, and adoption is the only thing that
> produces a record. Each site determines its own operational settings; the
> consortium never distributes them."

---

## 3. Contest — Standardization Versus Adoption (90 min)

- **Participant A:** Loose common elements produce a record too heterogeneous to
  aggregate. Standardize the implementation.
- **Participant B:** Standardized implementation is not adoptable across
  institutions with different populations and instrumentation, and a protocol
  nobody runs produces nothing.

**Say:**

> "Draw the line explicitly in your protocol and defend it. Both failure modes
> are real and the boundary is a judgment your document has to own."

---
