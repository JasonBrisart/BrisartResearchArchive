# Level I — Convening 1: The Framework at Field Scale

**Duration:** Full day
**Artifact:** Field position statement

## Objective

Participants determine what field-level functions the framework's examination
requires, and which of those functions currently have no owner.

---

## 1. Frame — Nobody's Job (60 min)

Deliver the disclosure. Then the premise:

> "Everything below this level assumes someone is watching. At field scale,
> frequently nobody is. Version drift, unpreserved nulls, and unreplicated
> findings do not have a responsible party by default."

State the terminal position of the package:

> "This is the last level. Beyond this there is no further training — only the
> exercise of the functions you define today."

---

## 2. Build — The Five Field Functions (120 min)

    VERSION AUTHORITY
      Which document governs when two disagree, and who says so.

    REPLICATION RECORD
      What is preserved across institutions, including failures.

    PRESPECIFICATION INFRASTRUCTURE
      Where designs are registered before data exist.

    SCOPE ENFORCEMENT AT SCALE
      How invalid application is refused when the proposer is an institution.

    RETIREMENT
      What happens when the framework should stop, and who decides.

For each, work the same three questions:

    Who owns this now?
    What breaks if nobody does?
    What is the minimum instrument that would fix it?

The first question is usually answered "nobody," and that is the convening's
finding rather than a failure of the room.

---

## 3. Contest — Is Field Governance Premature? (90 min)

- **Participant A:** Governance instruments for an unvalidated single-author
  framework are disproportionate. Build them if and when a literature exists.
- **Participant B:** Governance built after a literature exists cannot repair the
  record it inherits. The instruments are cheapest and most honest now.

**Say:**

> "A's position is defensible and your position statement may adopt it. A
> statement recommending minimal coordination, with the trigger conditions that
> would change that, is a complete artifact."

---

## 4. Apply — Field Position Statement (120 min)

    FIELD POSITION STATEMENT

    The framework's current status, stated accurately:

    Field functions currently unowned:

    Functions this body proposes to assume:

    Functions explicitly out of scope for this body:

    What this body does NOT claim authority over:
      (specify: the specification itself, licensing terms,
       individual institutional decisions)

    Trigger conditions for revisiting this position:

Peer exchange. The fourth section is the graded one — a governance body that
does not state its limits will exceed them.

---

## 5. Propagate (30 min)

**Say:**

> "Next: version authority. The function that fails silently and takes the
> literature with it."

---

## Instructor Notes

- The "nobody owns this" finding lands harder when participants discover it than
  when it is asserted. Let the three questions do the work.
- Participants from editorial or funding roles should be distributed across
  discussion groups rather than seated together.
