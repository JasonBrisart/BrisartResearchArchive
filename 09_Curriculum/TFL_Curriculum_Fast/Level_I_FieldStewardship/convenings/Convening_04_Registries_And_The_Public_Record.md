# Level I — Convening 4: Registries, Prespecification, and the Public Record

**Duration:** Full day
**Artifact:** Registry specification

## Objective

Participants specify a registry sufficient to make prespecification verifiable
and nulls recoverable.

---

## 1. Frame — Prespecification Without Infrastructure (45 min)

**Say:**

> "Every level below this one requires prespecification. None of them can verify
> it. A claim to have prespecified is worth exactly as much as the timestamp
> behind it, and timestamps require somewhere to live."

---

## 2. Build — Minimum Registry Fields (120 min)

    PER REGISTERED STUDY
      Claim under test, stated as a claim
      Governing document and version
      Measurement map, executable
      Persistence criterion including partial matches
      Sampling interval and detectable gap
      Rival accounts and their operationalizations
      Falsification condition
      Analysis path, primary and exploratory distinguished
      Stopping rule or target n
      Timestamp, immutable

    PER COMPLETED STUDY
      Outcome, including null
      Deviations from registration, with reasons
      Whether the falsification condition fired
      Data and code availability statement

    REGISTRY PROPERTIES
      Append-only after registration
      Publicly readable
      Registrations discoverable whether or not completed

**Say:**

> "The last property is the one registries usually lack. If an abandoned
> registration is invisible, the registry measures enthusiasm rather than
> evidence."

**Then the exclusion:**

> "Registries hold what a study committed to, not how the framework is run.
> Operational-layer content does not belong in a registration and a registry that
> accepts it has become a distribution channel."

---

## 3. Contest — Does a Registry Suppress Exploration? (90 min)

- **Participant A:** Registration burden deters the exploratory work that
  produces new questions.
- **Participant B:** Registration distinguishes exploratory from confirmatory
  work and protects exploration by labelling it honestly.

**Say:**

> "B is the standard resolution. Your specification should make exploratory
> registration cheap and clearly marked, or A's concern becomes accurate in
> practice."

---

## 4. Apply — Draft the Specification (150 min)

    REGISTRY SPECIFICATION

    REQUIRED FIELDS AT REGISTRATION:
    REQUIRED FIELDS AT COMPLETION:

    IMMUTABILITY RULE:
    AMENDMENT RULE:
      Amendments are permitted when:
      Amendments are visible because:

    ABANDONED REGISTRATIONS:
      Remain discoverable because:

    EXCLUDED CONTENT:
      The following may not be registered:

    OPERATOR:
      Maintained by:
      Funded how:
      Succession if the operator ceases:

The succession field is graded. A registry with no succession plan is a single
point of failure holding the field's record.
