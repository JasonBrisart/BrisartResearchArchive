# Level I — Governance Charter Rubric

**Total: 100 points**

---

## GATE CHECK — Applied First

| Gate | Requirement | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Version authority policy executable by a body that did not write it | Y / N |
| **B** | Retirement policy executable by a body that did not write it | Y / N |

Both are tested by blind adoption in Convening 6, not by reading. **If either is
N, the charter does not pass** regardless of total score.

**Gate A fails if:** the governing document is unnamed, supersession has no
declared authority, or the blind adopter could not resolve the citation dispute.

**Gate B fails if:** no observation constitutes refutation, no dormancy trigger
exists, or the blind adopter could not render a determination.

---

## 1. Field Position Statement (10 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Status stated accurately in both directions; unowned functions identified; **limits of claimed authority explicit**; trigger conditions stated |
| 7-8 | Complete; limits stated but thin |
| 4-6 | Functions listed without ownership analysis, or authority limits absent |
| 0-3 | Overclaims authority, or not adoptable |

A statement recommending minimal coordination, with reasons and triggers, scores
on the same scale and can reach full marks.

---

## 2. Version Authority Policy (20 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | Governing document named with basis; supersession declared, published, noticed; citation requirement specific; conflict rule unambiguous; preservation guaranteed; operational content explicitly excluded |
| 14-17 | Complete; one element requiring inference |
| 9-13 | Conflict rule ambiguous, or preservation not guaranteed |
| 4-8 | Supersession undefined |
| 0-3 | Not adoptable |

**Automatic ceiling of 12** if the policy permits deletion or silent revision of
superseded versions.

**Automatic ceiling of 10** if the policy distributes or authorizes distribution
of operational-layer content.

---

## 3. Consortium Protocol (20 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | Locked and adaptable elements distinguished and defended; preservation required including nulls; onboarding demonstrable; deviation handling specified; aggregation rule stated; withdrawal addressed |
| 14-17 | Complete; the locked/adaptable boundary asserted rather than defended |
| 9-13 | Null preservation optional, or aggregation rule absent |
| 4-8 | Not adoptable by an unaffiliated site |
| 0-3 | Absent |

**Automatic ceiling of 10** if the protocol specifies local operational settings
rather than requiring sites to determine their own.

---

## 4. Registry Specification (15 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | All registration and completion fields; immutability and amendment rules distinct; abandoned registrations discoverable; excluded content named; **succession plan present** |
| 10-12 | Complete; succession thin |
| 6-9 | Abandoned registrations invisible, or amendment rule permits silent revision |
| 2-5 | Fields insufficient to verify prespecification |
| 0-1 | Absent |

**Automatic ceiling of 9** if no succession plan exists. A registry without one
is a single point of failure holding the field's record.

---

## 5. Retirement Policy (20 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 18-20 | Scope limited to the programme rather than the framework, in the opening; refutation names observations; supersession defined; dormancy trigger specified; preservation guaranteed regardless of ending; post-retirement claims bounded |
| 14-17 | Complete; dormancy trigger vague |
| 9-13 | Refutation stated without nameable observations |
| 4-8 | Reads as terminating the framework rather than the programme |
| 0-3 | Absent |

---

## 6. Charter Defense (15 points)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | All seven presentation items; withstands all four challenge roles; answers the author's-advocate challenge with a defensible account of by what right the body acts |
| 10-12 | Strong on most; one challenge lands |
| 5-9 | Presents instruments without defending their authority basis |
| 0-4 | Cannot account for the charter's structure |

---

## Grade Bands

*Applies only if both gates are met.*

| Points | Level |
|---|---|
| 87-100 | Secure — charter is adoptable as written |
| 73-86 | Proficient |
| 60-72 | Developing |
| Below 60 | Not yet proficient |

## Re-Assessment

| Gate failed | Required before retake |
|---|---|
| A | Rewrite policy; pass a second blind adoption with a different participant |
| B | Rewrite policy; pass a second blind adoption |
| Both | Both, plus review of Convenings 2 and 5 |

## Never Scored

- Whether the participant endorses the framework
- Whether the charter recommends proceeding
- Institutional prestige, discipline, or resource level
- Writing polish beyond intelligibility

**A charter recommending against coordinated field-level examination is a
complete and passing artifact when its reasoning and instruments are sound.**
