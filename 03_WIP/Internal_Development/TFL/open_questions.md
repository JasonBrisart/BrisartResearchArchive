# Historical Context

This document records unresolved questions identified during development.

Purpose:

Not every important question can be answered during a single development cycle.

This file was used to preserve unanswered questions so that they would remain visible rather than be forgotten.

Historical Importance:

The questions recorded here help explain where uncertainty existed during development and what issues remained under investigation.

Relationship To Released TFL:

The presence of a question does not imply a deficiency in the released framework.

Many questions simply fell outside the scope of the final release or remained topics for future investigation.

## Persistence

- Is there a true persistence threshold, or is the threshold an artifact of measurement resolution?
- Is persistence governed entirely by affect, or are additional variables required?

## Contradiction & belief

- What determines whether contradiction leads to revision vs suppression?
- What is the exact relationship between belief and simulation?

## Cue overlap & reentry

- How should cue overlap be formally defined?
- What drives delayed reentry: affect, context, or both?

## Simulation existence & detection

- Can simulations exist without conscious report, and how do we detect them?
- Are multiple simulations processed in parallel or serially?

## Resolution

- Is resolution fundamentally discrete or continuous?

## Measurement

- How should simulation strength be measured reliably?

## Structural distinctions

- Are internally generated simulations structurally different from externally driven ones?

## Learning effects

- How do learning effects modify persistence dynamics over time?
