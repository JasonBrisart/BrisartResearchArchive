# Level E — Session 14: Final Assessment

**Duration:** 75 min

## Objective

Students demonstrate integrated command of the framework, its alternatives, its limits, and its status.

---

## 1. Orientation (8 min)

Restate the disclosure one final time.

> "TFL is an original framework. It has not been independently validated. You were never asked to believe it — you were asked to be able to evaluate it."

**Then the gates:**

> "Two requirements. State a falsification condition naming an observation that could actually occur. Correctly identify an invalid application and name the boundary it violates. Missing either means the final doesn't pass regardless of everything else."

**Say:**
> "That's deliberate. Being able to argue against a framework competently is the outcome of this course."

---

## 2. Final Examination (62 min)

Administer the **Level E Final Examination**.

**Permitted:** the two relations, written on the board.

    F(t) = w1·Input(t) + w2·Minfo(t) + w3·Sim(t)
    Sim(t+1) = Sim(t), if E(t) > θ_persistence

**Not permitted:** notes, prior work, consultation.

---

## 3. Close (5 min)

**Say:**
> "You arrived able to describe a loop. You're leaving able to specify it, measure it at a basic level, name what would refute it, and say where it doesn't apply."

**Say:**
> "You are also leaving without knowing whether it's true. That's not a gap in the course. That's the actual state of the question, and being able to sit in it with a defensible position is the skill."

---

## End of Level E

Students completing Level E should be able to:

- State and explain both core relations
- Explain why `E(t)` is structurally excluded from integration
- Apply the identifiability rule to accept or reject examples
- Distinguish persistence, recurrence, and resolution
- State the closure constraint and its cost
- Articulate uncertainty-only, arousal-only, and habit-based alternatives
- State falsification conditions meeting all four requirements
- Correctly reject invalid applications and name boundaries
- Describe the framework's epistemic status accurately

Level F begins from these and adds measurement, design, and operationalization at research scale.
