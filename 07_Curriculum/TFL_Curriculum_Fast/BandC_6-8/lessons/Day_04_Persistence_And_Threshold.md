# Band C — Day 4: Persistence and the Threshold

**Duration:** 45 min
**Materials:** Chart paper

---

## Objective

Students understand `E(t)` as a persistence gate with a threshold.

---

## 1. Quiz 1 (10 min)

Administer Quiz 1.

---

## 2. Hook — What Survived? (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Without looking at notes — what do you remember predicting yesterday?"

Some survive. Most don't.

**Say:**
> "Same class. Same day. Some made it to today, some didn't. Today: what decides that."

---

## 3. Name — The Persistence Condition (12 min)

Write the relation:

```
Sim(t+1) = Sim(t),  if  E(t) > θ_persistence
```

Decode it piece by piece:

> "`Sim(t+1)` — the simulation in the next window.
> Equals `Sim(t)` — the same simulation as now.
> **If** `E(t)` — the affective intensity —
> is greater than `θ_persistence` — the threshold."

**Say:**
> "In plain terms: if it mattered enough, it carries over. If it didn't, it doesn't."

Write what happens below threshold:

```
If E(t) does not exceed the threshold:
  - simulations decay
  - alternative simulations are generated
  - prior simulations are suppressed
```

**State the constraint again:**
> "`E(t)` appears here and nowhere else. It is not part of building the prediction. It functions exclusively as a persistence gate."

---

## 4. Work — Threshold Sorting (13 min)

Students list eight recent predictions and sort them.

| Prediction | E(t) high or low? | Persisted? |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

Then answer:

> Did every high-`E(t)` item persist? Did any low-`E(t)` item persist?

Students usually find the pattern holds with occasional exceptions. Both results are useful.

---

## 5. Carry (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Tomorrow is the most important session in this band. We're going to test whether being *right* has anything to do with what you just charted."

---

## Instructor Notes

- Write `θ` on the board and name it: "theta." Students handle Greek letters fine once named.
- Do not let the discussion drift into what sets the threshold. That varies by domain, individual, and context and is outside Band C.
