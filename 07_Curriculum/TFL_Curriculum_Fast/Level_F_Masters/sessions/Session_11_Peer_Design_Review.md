# Level F — Session 11: Peer Design Review

**Duration:** 100 min

## Objective

Students conduct a formal, structured audit of another student's complete design and produce a written review.

---

## 1. Frame — Return the First Drafts (12 min)

Hand back the Session 1 measurement maps with their partner's original markings.

**Say:**
> "Compare that to what you have now. That distance is the course."

Then set the task:

> "Today you are a reviewer, not a colleague. Your job is to determine whether this design could produce an interpretable result. Politeness that withholds a fatal flaw is not kindness."

---

## 2. Build — The Review Protocol (20 min)

Reviews follow a fixed structure, distributed as a template.

    SECTION 1 — Executability
      Could you run this without asking the author anything?
      List every line requiring clarification.

    SECTION 2 — Measurement
      Is Sim(t) prespecified, checkable, blindly scored?
      Is E(t) measured, bounded, separated from general arousal?
      Is the persistence criterion fixed in advance?
      Is the sampling interval justified and its limits stated?

    SECTION 3 — Adjudication
      Do the four accounts make different predictions here?
      Is the discriminating condition actually entered?
      Which rival best explains the anticipated result?

    SECTION 4 — Falsifiability
      Is the condition stated, specific, possible, and inconsistent?
      Could it fire under the stated analysis plan?

    SECTION 5 — Scope
      Does any claim exceed what the framework models?
      Does any conclusion exceed the declared resolution?

    SECTION 6 — Recommendation
      Executable as written / executable with revisions / not executable
      Three required revisions, ranked.

---

## 3. Apply — The Review (45 min)

Two rounds. Each student reviews one design in writing (20 min), then a second in a paired verbal round (15 min), with 10 minutes for authors to record and question.

**Rules:**
- Authors do not defend during the written round
- Every criticism must name the section it falls under
- "Not executable" requires naming the specific blocking line

---

## 4. Contest — Calibration (18 min)

Instructor selects two reviews: one that found a real fatal flaw, one that was polite and missed it. Read both anonymously.

**Say:**
> "Same design, two verdicts. One of these reviewers would have let a study run that couldn't produce an interpretable result. Reviewing gently is a failure mode with real cost."

---

## 5. Propagate (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Revise against your reviews. Next session you defend the result."

---

## Due

**Peer Review Report** submitted at the end of session.

## Instructor Notes

- The calibration step is essential. Without it, half the class writes encouraging reviews and learns nothing about review.
- Keep the Session 1 drafts. The visible progress does substantial motivational work in the final two weeks.
