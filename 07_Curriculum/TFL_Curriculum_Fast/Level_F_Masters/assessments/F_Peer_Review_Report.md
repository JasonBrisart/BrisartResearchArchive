# Level F — Peer Review Report

**Due:** Session 11 · **Points:** 40 · **Weight:** 15% of course grade

---

## Task

Produce a formal review of one classmate's complete study design, following the protocol below. You are reviewing for executability and interpretability, not for ambition or polish.

---

## Required Structure

### Section 1 — Executability

Could you run this without asking the author anything? List every line requiring clarification. If the list is empty, say so explicitly.

### Section 2 — Measurement

- Is `Sim(t)` prespecified, checkable, and blindly scored?
- Is `E(t)` measured rather than assumed, bounded, and separated from general arousal?
- Is the persistence criterion fixed in advance, including partial matches?
- Is the sampling interval justified, and are its limits stated?

### Section 3 — Adjudication

- Do the four accounts make different predictions in this design?
- Is the discriminating condition actually entered?
- Which rival best explains the anticipated result?

### Section 4 — Falsifiability

- Is the condition stated, specific, possible, and inconsistent with the claim?
- Could it fire under the stated analysis plan?

### Section 5 — Scope

- Does any claim exceed what the framework models?
- Does any conclusion exceed the declared observation resolution?

### Section 6 — Recommendation

    Executable as written / Executable with revisions / Not executable

Three required revisions, ranked by severity. "Not executable" must name the specific blocking element.

---

## Rubric (40 points)

### Executability audit (8)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 7-8 | Every ambiguous line identified; verdict supported by specifics |
| 5-6 | Major gaps found, minor ones missed |
| 3-4 | Generic observations; ambiguities not located |
| 0-2 | Absent or purely affirming |

### Measurement audit (10)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | All four checks applied with specific findings; circularity and contamination risks named |
| 6-8 | Most checks applied; one area superficial |
| 3-5 | Checks named but not applied to the actual design |
| 0-2 | Not performed |

### Adjudication audit (8)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 7-8 | Correctly determines whether the design discriminates; names the best rival explanation |
| 5-6 | Determines discrimination but does not name the rival |
| 3-4 | Asserts without analysis |
| 0-2 | Not performed |

### Falsifiability audit (8)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 7-8 | Tests the condition against all four requirements AND against the analysis plan |
| 5-6 | Tests requirements only |
| 3-4 | Notes the condition exists |
| 0-2 | Not performed |

### Recommendation quality (6)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5-6 | Verdict follows from findings; three revisions ranked and actionable |
| 3-4 | Verdict reasonable; revisions vague |
| 0-2 | Verdict unsupported, or softened to avoid discomfort |

---

## Calibration Note

A review that recommends "executable as written" for a design with an unspecified persistence criterion is scored as a failed review regardless of its prose quality. Missing a fatal flaw is the error this assessment exists to detect.

## Never Scored

Whether the reviewed design is good · the relationship between reviewer and author · tone, provided criticisms are specific and attached to sections
