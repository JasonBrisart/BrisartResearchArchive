# Level G — Seminar 2: Specification Audit

**Duration:** 3 hours

## Objective

Participants produce a documented, evidenced catalogue of the framework's underspecification.

---

## 1. Frame — Silence Is Data (20 min)

**Say:**
> "A specification is judged as much by what it declines to say as by what it asserts. Some silences are legitimate scoping. Others are gaps the framework needs to fill before it can be tested. Today you separate them."

Three categories, written on the board:

    DELIBERATE EXCLUSION — stated, scoped, defensible
    ACKNOWLEDGED OPEN     — the framework flags it as undetermined
    UNDOCUMENTED GAP      — silent where silence prevents testing

---

## 2. Build — Sorting the Gaps (55 min)

Collect the three gaps each participant brought. Sort them live into the three categories.

Predictable results:

| Item | Category |
|---|---|
| Consciousness, identity, groups, neural implementation | Deliberate exclusion |
| Weight values | Acknowledged open — requires empirical determination |
| Window interval derivation | **Undocumented gap** |
| What sets `θ_persistence` | **Undocumented gap** |
| How `Belief(t)` updates across cycles | **Undocumented gap** |
| Functional form of integration beyond linearity | **Undocumented gap** |
| Partial-match criteria | **Undocumented gap** |

**Say:**
> "Five undocumented gaps in one session is a substantial finding and it is neither an attack nor a defence. It is what specification audit produces. A mature framework would have this document; this one doesn't yet, so you're writing it."

---

## 3. Contest — Are Gaps Fatal or Ordinary? (50 min)

- **Participant A (assigned defender this week):** Every framework at this stage has open parameters. The gaps are ordinary and the framework's virtue is that closure prevents filling them opportunistically.
- **Participant B:** Gaps in the *threshold* specifically are not ordinary. `θ_persistence` carries the framework's central claim; leaving it undetermined means the claim cannot be quantitatively evaluated.

**Say:**
> "B has located the asymmetry that matters. Not all gaps are equal — a gap in the load-bearing parameter is different from a gap in a peripheral one. Rank your gaps by how much of the framework rests on them."

---

## 4. Apply — The Audit Document (45 min)

Participants draft a formal audit entry for each gap.

    GAP:
    WHERE THE SPECIFICATION IS SILENT:   (textual location)
    CATEGORY:                            exclusion / open / undocumented
    WHAT RESTS ON IT:
    WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT:                 (theoretical work / empirical work / both)
    CONSEQUENCE IF LEFT OPEN:

Peer exchange and audit of one another's entries.

---

## 5. Propagate (10 min)

Assign: each participant identifies two rival accounts from their **own field's** literature, with readings, for Seminar 3.

---

## Instructor Notes

- The audit document is the most transferable artifact in Level G. Several participants each term extend it into their critique paper directly.
- Insist on textual location. "It doesn't say" without a reference is an impression, not an audit.
