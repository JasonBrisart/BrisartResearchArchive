# Level G — Seminar 7: The Critique Paper Workshop

**Duration:** 3 hours

## Objective

Participants develop original critique to publishable standard through structured peer workshop.

---

## 1. Frame — What Makes a Critique Original (20 min)

**Say:**
> "Restating the framework's unvalidated status is not a critique. Neither is preferring a different theory. An original critique identifies something true about this specification that its author has not addressed and that consequentially affects whether it can be tested."

Three qualifying forms:

    SPECIFICATION CRITIQUE    A gap that prevents testing
    STRUCTURAL CRITIQUE       An internal tension between commitments
    ADJUDICATION CRITIQUE     A rival the framework cannot be separated from

---

## 2. Workshop — Round One (65 min)

Papers distributed in advance and read before the meeting.

Four papers workshopped, fifteen minutes each.

**Fixed protocol per paper:**

    1. Author is silent (10 min)
       - Reader 1 states the paper's central claim as they understood it
       - Reader 2 states the strongest objection to it
       - Reader 3 states what the paper needs to be publishable

    2. Author responds (5 min)

**Say:**
> "If Reader 1 cannot state your claim, that is your finding, not theirs."

---

## 3. Workshop — Round Two (55 min)

Remaining papers, same protocol.

---

## 4. Apply — Revision Plan (30 min)

Each author writes:

    MY CENTRAL CLAIM, AS READERS UNDERSTOOD IT:
    MY CENTRAL CLAIM, AS INTENDED:
    GAP BETWEEN THEM:
    THE STRONGEST OBJECTION RAISED:
    MY RESPONSE, OR MY CONCESSION:
    THREE REVISIONS, RANKED:

Concession is a legitimate entry and is scored equally with response.

---

## 5. Propagate (10 min)

**Say:**
> "Next: the proposal. You've found what's wrong. Now design the thing that would settle it."

---

## Due

**Critique Paper** submitted at the start of this seminar.

## Instructor Notes

- The author-silent rule is essential. Authors who explain during the reading round prevent the diagnostic that the round exists to produce.
- Credit concession visibly. A participant who abandons a critique under good argument has done the seminar correctly and others should see that rewarded.
