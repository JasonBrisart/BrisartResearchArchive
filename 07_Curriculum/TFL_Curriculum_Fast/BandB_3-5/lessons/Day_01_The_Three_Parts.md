# Band B — Day 1: The Three Parts

**Duration:** 30-40 min
**Materials:** Chart paper, notebooks

---

## Objective

Students identify notice, remember, and expect in a real situation.

---

## 1. Hook — The Hallway Test (5 min)

**Say:**
> "Don't look at the door. Write down what you think the hallway sounds like right now."

Students write. Then check.

**Say:**
> "Nobody looked. Everybody wrote something. Where did that come from?"

Collect two answers. Steer toward: *I've been in that hallway before.*

**Say:**
> "So you used something from before to guess about something you couldn't see. That's what we're studying."

---

## 2. Name — Three Parts (8 min)

Write the three words on chart paper with definitions.

```
NOTICE     what is coming in right now
REMEMBER   what comes back from before
EXPECT     what I think happens next
```

**Say:**
> "These three are happening constantly. Right now. While I'm talking."

Model with a live example:
> "I notice you're all watching me. I remember that this class ends at [time]. So I expect somebody is going to look at the clock in the next minute."

---

## 3. Work — The Three-Column Log (15 min)

Students set up three columns in their notebooks.

| Notice | Remember | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

Fill four rows about right now, in this room, in the next few minutes.

**Requirements:**
- Each row must connect across. The expectation must come from the memory.
- At least one row must be about something in the next 60 seconds.

**Circulate and ask:** "Why does that memory give you that expectation?"

**Common error:** Students write three unrelated things per row. Correct it by pointing at their own memory column and asking what it makes them expect.

---

## 4. Compare — Same Room, Different Rows (7 min)

Collect three students' rows on the board.

**Ask:**
> "Everyone is in the same room. Why is nobody's chart the same?"

Steer toward: different memories produce different expectations.

**Say:**
> "The noticing is almost the same for all of you. The remembering is different. That's what makes the expecting different."

---

## 5. Carry — Forward (3 min)

**Say:**
> "Tomorrow: what happens when all three of these get combined into one thing."

---

## Instructor Notes

- If students completed Band A, run this in 15 minutes and bank the time for Day 3.
- Do not accept "I just knew" as a final answer for the remember column. Push once: "Knew from what?"
