# Micro-Lesson B4: Stuck Versus Right

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Band:** B (3-5)
**Materials:** Board or chart paper

---

## Purpose

Separate persistence from accuracy — the most important distinction in the curriculum.

## Script

Draw four boxes on the board:

```
              Stuck with me    Faded fast
I was right   [          ]    [          ]
I was wrong   [          ]    [          ]
```

**Say:**
> "Everyone give me one prediction from the last two days. Tell me which box it goes in."

Fill all four boxes with real student examples. All four will fill.

**Say:**
> "Look at this. If being right decided whether something stuck, two of these boxes would be empty."

**Ask:**
> "Are they?"

No.

**Close:**
> "Sticking and being right are two separate things. What decides sticking is how much it mattered."

## Common Student Objection

> "But I remember the ones I got right!"

**Response:**
> "Sometimes. Give me one you got right and forgot instantly."

They always can. The chair, the door, the bell.

## Use

Essential before Quiz 2 and before the Band B unit task. Also the best single re-teach if Quiz 2 scores are low.
