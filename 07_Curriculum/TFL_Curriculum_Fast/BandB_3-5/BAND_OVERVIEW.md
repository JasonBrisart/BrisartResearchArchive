# Band B Overview — Grades 3-5

**Unit title:** The Loop That Never Stops

**Sessions:** 8
**Session length:** 30-40 minutes
**Suggested pacing:** Two to three weeks

---

## What Students Learn

Band B teaches **carry-forward**: each moment becomes the starting condition for the next one, so nothing begins from zero.

Band A established that we predict. Band B establishes that predictions are inherited.

## Vocabulary

Everything from Band A, plus two new terms:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Notice | What is coming in right now |
| Remember | What comes back from before |
| Expect | What I think happens next |
| **Right now** | Everything put together into this moment |
| **Carry forward** | Right now becomes the start of what comes next |

No notation. No variable names. Those belong to Band C.

## Session Arc

| Day | Title | Core move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Three Parts | Establish notice / remember / expect quickly |
| 2 | Right Now | The three combine into one state |
| 3 | Carry Forward | The hard idea — nothing starts from zero |
| 4 | The Chain | Physical demonstration of inheritance |
| 5 | When the Guess Is Right | Correct predictions are invisible |
| 6 | When the Guess Is Wrong | Contradiction and what follows it |
| 7 | What Sticks | Persistence introduced informally |
| 8 | The Whole Loop | Assembly and unit task |

Days 3 and 4 are the load-bearing sessions. Do not compress them.

## Prerequisites

None. Band B assumes no prior TFL instruction.

If students completed Band A, Day 1 can be shortened to 15 minutes and the time moved to Day 3.

## What Carries Into Band C

Students finishing Band B should be able to:

- Name all five parts in plain language
- Explain that each moment starts from the previous one
- Describe what happens when a prediction is contradicted
- Say that some predictions stick and some fade

Band C renames these with formal variables and adds the persistence threshold.

## Assessment Summary

| Instrument | Weight | When |
|---|---|---|
| Homework sets (3) | 30% | After Days 2, 4, 6 |
| Quiz 1 — the parts | 15% | After Day 3 |
| Quiz 2 — carry-forward | 15% | After Day 6 |
| Unit task — loop trace | 40% | Day 8 |

## Standing Classroom Rule

State on Day 1 and repeat every session:

> **We never grade whether your prediction was right.** We grade whether you recorded it and explained your reasoning.

## Materials

- Notebooks or printed log templates
- Chart paper and markers
- One small object to pass (Day 4)
- Index cards with a repeating pattern (Day 6)
