## Section 2 — The Persistence Gate (14)

**4.** (6)

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|

| 4-5 | Correct gating claim, exclusivity not explicit |

```
Simulations decay · Alternative simulations are generated · Prior simulations are suppressed
```

| Pts | Criteria |
|---|---|
