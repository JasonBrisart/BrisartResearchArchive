# Micro-Lesson D2: Threshold Direction

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Band:** D (9-12)

---

## Purpose

Correct the most common reversal error in Band D.

## Script

**Ask:**
> "`θ_persistence` is raised. More persistence or less?"

Expect a split class. The intuitive answer is wrong.

**Resolve:**

```
θ_persistence ↑  →  higher bar  →  fewer simulations clear it  →  LESS persistence
θ_persistence ↓  →  lower bar   →  more simulations clear it   →  MORE persistence
```

**Analogy that works:**
> "Raising the passing grade doesn't mean more people pass."

## Follow-Up Items

| Change | Result |
|---|---|
| `E(t)` rises, `θ` fixed | More persistence |
| `θ` rises, `E(t)` fixed | Less persistence |
| Both rise proportionally | Depends on which crosses |
| `E(t)` falls below `θ` | Decay, replacement, or suppression |

## Use

Before Quiz 1 and before the midterm. Two minutes here prevents a predictable cluster of errors.
