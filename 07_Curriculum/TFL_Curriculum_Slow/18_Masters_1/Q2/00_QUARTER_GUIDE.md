## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 10 — The Analysis Plan.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 7:** at least one marker returns zero questions
- **Session 8:** the prediction lines differ and the cell verdict is honest
- **Session 9:** residual risk is acknowledged rather than dismissed
- **Session 10:** every plan contains all ten items including the unscoreable rule
- **Session 11:** every student names why the invalid version was tempting
- **Session 12:** the cohort identifies its own weakest area

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | assume one clean run suffices | Require two independent markers. |
| 8 | claim the design enters the cell when it does not | Check the four criteria one at a time. |
| 9 | mark most confounds not applicable | Require a written reason for each dismissal. |
| 10 | omit the unscoreable-response rule | Supply three vague responses and ask how each is handled. |
| 11 | reject somebody else's design | Require their own. |
| 12 | review only recent sessions | Sample from every session. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
