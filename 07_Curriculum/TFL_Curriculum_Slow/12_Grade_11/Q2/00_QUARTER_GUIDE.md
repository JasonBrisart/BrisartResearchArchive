## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — The Generalisation Problem.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student has four rows with genuinely distinct predictions
- **Lesson 10:** every rival statement carries a recorded objection from a checker
- **Lesson 11:** every student applies all three to at least two of their own results
- **Lesson 12:** every student states honestly whether their design enters it
- **Lesson 13:** every student addresses the limit rather than ignoring it
- **Lesson 14:** every student's redesign enters the cell
- **Lesson 15:** every student identifies two outside accounts with divergence lines
- **Lesson 16:** the class completes at least four rows with distinct predictions

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | state a rival in a form its proponents would reject | Ask the check question: would somebody who believes that accept your version? |
| 10 | select a checker who already agrees with them | Assign checkers rather than letting students choose. |
| 11 | argue the rivals are less plausible | Redirect to prediction: plausibility is not the test. |
| 12 | claim their design enters the cell when it does not | Check against the four criteria one at a time. |
| 13 | claim the cell is common in natural settings | Press for frequency: how often does that actually happen? |
| 14 | claim entry without meeting all four criteria | Check all four criteria explicitly. |
| 15 | present an account with no distinguishable prediction | Name the three possibilities: same theory, special case, or underspecified. |
| 16 | produce coinciding rows | Mark coinciding rows as a design finding, not an error. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
