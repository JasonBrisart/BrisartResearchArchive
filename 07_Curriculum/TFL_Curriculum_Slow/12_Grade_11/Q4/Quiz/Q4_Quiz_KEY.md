# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 11** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify framework claims that currently resist measurement. Evidence: every student lists four with requirements

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students determine whether their design can recover parameters. Evidence: every student states honestly whether estimation is feasible

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students match what they claim to what they observed. Evidence: every student names real excluded conclusions

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students write a result with limits, confounds, and alternatives stated. Evidence: every write-up contains all four elements

**5.** *(2 points)* the framework applies only where all variables can be measured.

**6.** *(2 points)* specify how a variable will actually be measured.
