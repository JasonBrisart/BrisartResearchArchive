# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 2** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build a four-column comparison unaided. Evidence: every student has four rows with evidence throughout

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students audit somebody else's table. Evidence: every student receives a checked table with a specific note

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students repair a checked table. Evidence: every student repairs and crosses out at least one

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students build a comparison table on non-TFL material. Evidence: every student completes four rows on outside material

**5.** *(2 points)* put two things side by side and say how they differ.

**6.** *(2 points)* the thing you point at to show your answer is right.
