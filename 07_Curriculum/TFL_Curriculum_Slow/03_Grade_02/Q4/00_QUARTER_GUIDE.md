## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 29 — Explaining The Table.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 25:** every student has four rows with evidence throughout
- **Lesson 26:** every student receives a checked table with a specific note
- **Lesson 27:** every student repairs and crosses out at least one
- **Lesson 28:** every student completes four rows on outside material
- **Lesson 29:** every student records a question they could not answer
- **Lesson 30:** every student reads a finding naming a cause
- **Lesson 31:** every student identifies one area to review
- **Lesson 32:** all four outcomes are demonstrated

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | leave the evidence column blank | Refuse the row: point at something or cross it out. |
| 26 | write this is good | Require a located note: name the row and the column. |
| 27 | argue instead of repairing | Allow one defence if they can point at the evidence now. |
| 28 | think it only works here | Build the first row together from the other subject. |
| 29 | report no question | Brief the listener to ask one. |
| 30 | read a finding with no cause | Ask the one question in front of the class. |
| 31 | panic | Say clearly this one does not count. |
| 32 | run out of time | The cause item is the Grade 3 prerequisite. Flag it first. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
