**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student compares two pairs

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: students can state that difference is not error

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student sorts three cases correctly

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every pair names a cause

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the class completes at least four correctly

**9.** *(2 points)* put two things side by side and say how they differ.

**10.** *(2 points)* the thing you point at to show your answer is right.

**11.** *(2 points)* when an expectation is different the second time.

**12.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the class completes at least four correctly
