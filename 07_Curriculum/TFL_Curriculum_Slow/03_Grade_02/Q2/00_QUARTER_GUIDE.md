## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 13 — Evidence Somebody Else Can Check.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 9:** every student has four evidenced claims or a justified crossing-out
- **Lesson 10:** every student ranks at least four correctly
- **Lesson 11:** every student marks at least one with a question mark
- **Lesson 12:** every student's claim follows from their three observations
- **Lesson 13:** every student receives a checked sheet
- **Lesson 14:** every student revises at least one claim
- **Lesson 15:** every student evidences four from other subjects
- **Lesson 16:** the class sorts at least six correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | offer certainty as evidence | Apply the test aloud: point at it, where is it? |
| 10 | rank by how confident the speaker sounded | Ask what each one lets you actually see. |
| 11 | invent evidence to avoid question marks | Praise question marks explicitly and visibly. |
| 12 | write the claim first and find evidence after | Collect the observations physically before the claim sheet is handed out. |
| 13 | accept a partner's evidence without checking | Require them to physically point at each item. |
| 14 | defend the claim regardless | Name it: you are arguing, look at what you can point at. |
| 15 | think it only applies here | Do the first one yourself from another subject. |
| 16 | accept confidence as evidence | Re-run the pointing test on every disputed item. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
