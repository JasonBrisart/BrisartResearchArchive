# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 3** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students analyse designs that could not separate accounts. Evidence: every student names the failing requirement for three cases

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students write a comparison that could favour any account. Evidence: a reader cannot infer the author's preference

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state what would resolve the contest. Evidence: every student ranks three requirements

**4.** *(2 points)* designing so competing accounts make different predictions.

**5.** *(2 points)* stating a rival as its proponents would.
