## Exit outcomes

- Show that a consistent result discriminates nothing
- State a rival in a form its proponents accept, externally checked
- Settle the redundancy charge with a differential prediction
- State results that would favour each account, including the rivals

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **adjudication** | designing so competing accounts make different predictions |
| **fair operationalisation** | stating a rival as its proponents would |
| **redundancy charge** | the claim that a framework restates existing accounts |
| **mediation defence** | attributing an effect to propagation rather than weighting |
| **unfalsifiable rescue** | a defence that makes a claim immune to any result |

**Not used at this grade:** Referee-quality review, publication-standard measurement audit.

## How this grade is taught

Rivals stated as their proponents would state them, and checked by somebody who finds them plausible.

## How students show they have learned it

Differential tables, adjudicating designs, problem sets, midterm and final.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "Three rivals is the whole competition"

**Why it happens.** The package supplies three, so students stop there.

**How to fix it.** Require two field-specific rivals with readings.

**What to say.** "Your field already has a literature explaining something here."

### "My field's account never diverges, so they agree"

**Why it happens.** Non-divergence reads as agreement.

**How to fix it.** Name the three possibilities.

**What to say.** "Determine which one is true. That is a paper."

### "The mediation defence settles the gating challenge"

**Why it happens.** It is available, so students treat it as a resolution.

**How to fix it.** Pose the unfalsifiability charge directly.

**What to say.** "Specify the within-window evidence, or the charge stands."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide one worked field-rival comparison with the divergence line completed.

**Extension.** Ask them to determine whether their field rival is a special case of TFL. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
