## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 11 — Answering The Adversarial Round.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 8:** the prediction lines differ from one another
- **Session 9:** every student addresses all seven rows with reasons
- **Session 10:** every design is attacked from every rival position
- **Session 11:** every student revises against at least two attacks
- **Session 12:** every account has a favouring result stated
- **Session 13:** every student names a resolving observation
- **Session 14:** the class identifies its own weakest area

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | manipulate intensity and ambiguity together | Check the manipulation: when intensity went up, did ambiguity? |
| 9 | mark most confounds not applicable | Require a written reason for each dismissal. |
| 10 | defend in real time | Enforce the rule. Designers who argue stop listening. |
| 11 | dismiss every attack | Require at least two substantive revisions. |
| 12 | state only the framework-favouring direction | Require the reverse for every row. |
| 13 | discard the inconvenient result | Require both in the write-up. |
| 14 | review only recent sessions | Sample from every session. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
