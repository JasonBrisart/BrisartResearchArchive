## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 18 — Predictions For Every Account.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 15:** every student marks at least one weakening
- **Session 16:** the restated rival changes at least one prediction
- **Session 17:** every redesign enters the cell and states a cost
- **Session 18:** every student has five distinct prediction rows
- **Session 19:** every student defends their fair operationalisation
- **Session 20:** every verdict is supported by a specific differential prediction
- **Session 21:** every student analyses one real attempt

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | pass their own operationalisation | Have the assigned defender audit it instead. |
| 16 | restate without re-deriving | Require the prediction to be recalculated. |
| 17 | claim entry without meeting all four criteria | Check all four explicitly. |
| 18 | reuse the same prediction for two accounts | Mark coinciding rows and require a redesign or an honest note. |
| 19 | defend fair operationalisation by citing intent | Require the external check: who checked it? What did they say? |
| 20 | deliver a verdict without the prediction | Return it: where is the prediction that settles it? |
| 21 | choose a settled contest | Require one that was genuinely open when the study ran. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
