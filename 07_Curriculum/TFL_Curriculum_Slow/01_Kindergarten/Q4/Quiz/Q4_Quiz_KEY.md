# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children report the guess an engineered break revealed. Evidence: every child completes the surprise frame

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children separate what stayed from what faded. Evidence: each child names a stuck and a gone thing

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children use all three parts on one real event. Evidence: each child has three connected boxes, not three unrelated ones

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children show the three parts repeating. Evidence: every group walks the second lap and can say why

**5.** *(2 points)* what I see, hear, or feel right now.

**6.** *(2 points)* what comes back from before.
