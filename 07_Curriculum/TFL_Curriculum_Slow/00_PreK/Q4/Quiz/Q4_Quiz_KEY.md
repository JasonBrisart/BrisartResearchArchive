# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Pre-Kindergarten** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children guess repeatedly in a chain. Evidence: the object goes all the way round at least once

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children connect an outcome to the next guess. Evidence: every pair completes a three-step chain

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children physically walk the cycle. Evidence: every group walks the second lap

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Children draw the cycle with arrows. Evidence: every child's drawing shows something returning to the start

**5.** *(2 points)* saying what you think happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* when something different happened than you guessed.
