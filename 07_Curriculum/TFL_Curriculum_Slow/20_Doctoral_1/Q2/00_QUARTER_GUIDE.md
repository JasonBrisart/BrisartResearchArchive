## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 7 — Your Field's Rivals.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 5:** every entry carries a checkable textual location
- **Seminar 6:** every audit receives at least one marked entry
- **Seminar 7:** every divergence line is pressed and either holds or fails
- **Seminar 8:** every participant has a genuine differential prediction

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | write an entry with a paraphrased location | Require the exact reference. |
| 6 | pass a colleague's document | Require one marked entry per document. |
| 7 | present a rival with no distinguishable prediction | Name the three possibilities and assign the determination. |
| 8 | assert novelty without the prediction | Require it: name the observation where they differ. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
