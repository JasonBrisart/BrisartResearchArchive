## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 3 — Sorting The Gaps.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 1:** every objection is specific enough to be addressed
- **Seminar 2:** the pooled list contains at least five candidate gaps
- **Seminar 3:** every entry carries a textual location
- **Seminar 4:** every ranking is justified with a dependency

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | submit a status objection | Reject it by name and require a claim-level objection. |
| 2 | list stylistic complaints as gaps | Apply the test: does that silence prevent testing? |
| 3 | classify a scoped exclusion as a gap | Read the exclusion aloud from the specification. |
| 4 | rank by how interesting the gap is | Require the dependency: what else fails if it stays open? |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
