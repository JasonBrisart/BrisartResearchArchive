## The pivot seminar of this quarter

**Seminar 11 — The Scope Section.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Seminar 9:** every limitation names an excluded conclusion
- **Seminar 10:** every participant finds and repairs at least one breach
- **Seminar 11:** every rejection names a boundary and an alternative level
- **Seminar 12:** every dissertation passes a peer gate check on both items

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Seminar | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | list sample size and generalisability without specifics | Require the phenomenon: what specifically would this design miss? |
| 10 | find no breaches | Require at least one. There always is. |
| 11 | reject an application that is actually valid | Check validity before accepting the rejection. |
| 12 | assume the gates will pass | Run the check before any other reading. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
