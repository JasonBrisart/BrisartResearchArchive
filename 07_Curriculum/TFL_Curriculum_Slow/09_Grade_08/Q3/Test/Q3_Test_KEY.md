# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 8** · **Total: 46 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every claim has two genuine assumptions

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student justifies a weakest assumption for three claims

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student receives an audit naming at least one per claim

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student names at least three method assumptions

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student audits three outside claims

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student states what survives for three claims

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student has a five-part claim, audited and repaired

**8.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the class finds at least four hidden assumptions

**9.** *(2 points)* a simulation being modified after contradiction.

**10.** *(2 points)* a simulation being pushed aside rather than deleted.

**11.** *(2 points)* previously identified content reappearing after absence.

**12.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: the class finds at least four hidden assumptions
