# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 1** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students receive and record the framework's epistemic status. Evidence: every objection is specific rather than a statement of status

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the status without overclaiming or underclaiming. Evidence: all three versions are accurate in both directions

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students separate checkable claims from unchecked ones. Evidence: the pooled list contains at least four genuinely unchecked claims

**4.** *(2 points)* what is currently known about whether a framework is correct.

**5.** *(2 points)* confirmation by researchers unconnected to the author.
