# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 12** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students learn the gate requirements for the year. Evidence: every student has recorded both gates

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the four requirements of a falsification condition. Evidence: every condition names an observation rather than an absence

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify what makes a claim unfalsifiable. Evidence: every student names all three escape features

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students distinguish a null result from a falsification. Evidence: every student distinguishes at least three correctly

**5.** *(2 points)* a result that would be inconsistent with the claim.

**6.** *(2 points)* a claim built so that no result could contradict it.
