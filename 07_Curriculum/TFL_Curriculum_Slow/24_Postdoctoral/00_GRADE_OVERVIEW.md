# Postdoctoral

**Course:** Programme Design · **Stage:** Post-Doctoral
**12 meetings of 3 hours** · **Four quarters**

## What this year owns

Structural programme failure modes, and stop conditions that name observations.

> A bad study wastes a year. A bad programme wastes a decade and several careers.

## Exit outcomes

- Distinguish structural programme failure from individual error
- Audit a programme for single-point dependency and repair it
- Specify a change log capable of detecting specification drift
- Write stop conditions that name observations with decision authorities

## Vocabulary released this year

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **single-point dependency** | every project assuming the framework is correct |
| **confirmation drift** | designs becoming less discriminating over time |
| **scope creep under success** | boundaries eroding from inside a programme |
| **stop condition** | an observation that would end the programme |
| **reputational entanglement** | members' standing tied to the framework being true |

**Not used at this grade:** Supervision plans, syllabi, review protocols, governance charters.

## How this grade is taught

Programmes must produce value under refutation. Stop conditions must name observations, not feelings.

## How students show they have learned it

A programme memo, failure-mode analyses, a defended recommendation.

## Predicted misconceptions

### "A programme is a sequence of studies"

**Why it happens.** It looks like one from outside.

**How to fix it.** A programme is a structure with dependencies, drift risks, and an ending.

**What to say.** "The structure determines whether it survives a bad result."

### "Vigilance prevents drift"

**Why it happens.** Drift feels like a discipline problem.

**How to fix it.** Drift never announces itself and vigilance does not scale.

**What to say.** "The log is the only reliable detection."

### "Stop conditions are pessimism"

**Why it happens.** Planning to end feels like planning to fail.

**How to fix it.** A programme without them is single-point dependency with extra steps.

**What to say.** "Without them, continuing is not a choice you are making."


## Support and extension

**More support.** Provide the five failure modes with one structural fix worked.

**Extension.** Ask them to cost the programme in people-years. Do not use extension to release later vocabulary.
