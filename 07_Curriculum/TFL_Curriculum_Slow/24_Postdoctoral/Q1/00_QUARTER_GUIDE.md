## The pivot meeting of this quarter

**Meeting 2 — Single-Point Dependency.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Meeting 1:** every participant lists at least three candidates
- **Meeting 2:** every study produces a publishable result under refutation
- **Meeting 3:** every programme names an enforcer with authority to stop

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Meeting | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | list individual mistakes rather than structural failures | Distinguish: what fails even when everybody is competent? |
| 2 | hedge only the first study | Require it throughout: study six, what does it produce if study three refuted the claim? |
| 3 | name themselves as the enforcer | Reject it: you are the person whose programme is at risk. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
