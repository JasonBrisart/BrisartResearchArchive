## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 29 — Explaining Right Now.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 25:** every student identifies the noticing as the changed part
- **Lesson 26:** every student produces three varied moments
- **Lesson 27:** every student has two reconstructions from consecutive moments
- **Lesson 28:** every student has written and sealed a dated answer
- **Lesson 29:** every student records a question they could not answer
- **Lesson 30:** every student compares an early and a late reconstruction
- **Lesson 31:** every student identifies one area to review
- **Lesson 32:** all four outcomes are demonstrated

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | say all three parts changed | Walk the three: did your memory change in half a second? |
| 26 | change more than one part per experiment | Restrict it explicitly: one part at a time. |
| 27 | reconstruct two unrelated moments | Require them to be within the same minute. |
| 28 | demand the answer | Hold the line. The sealed prediction is the Grade 4 hook. |
| 29 | report no question | Brief the listener to ask one. |
| 30 | cannot find early work | Keep everything in one folder from Lesson 1. |
| 31 | panic | Say clearly this one does not count. |
| 32 | run out of time | The decomposition item is the Grade 4 prerequisite. Flag it first. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
