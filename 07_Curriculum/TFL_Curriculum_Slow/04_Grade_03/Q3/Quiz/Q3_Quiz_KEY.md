# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 3** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students support a reconstruction with pointable evidence. Evidence: every student marks at least one line with a question mark

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students rank the lines of a reconstruction by evidential strength. Evidence: every student ranks all five and justifies the weakest

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students use a second person's account as evidence. Evidence: every student strengthens at least one line with external evidence

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students handle two accounts that conflict. Evidence: every student names a resolving observation

**5.** *(2 points)* everything combined into the moment you are actually having.

**6.** *(2 points)* three things arriving together as one.
