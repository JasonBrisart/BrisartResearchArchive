# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 3** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students decompose a described moment into three parts. Evidence: every student decomposes at least four into connected parts

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students verify a decomposition by running it forwards. Evidence: every student receives a forward-checked set with at least one marked

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students decompose a character's moment from a text. Evidence: every student decomposes three character moments

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students decompose a moment from a still image. Evidence: every student produces three forward-checking decompositions

**5.** *(2 points)* everything combined into the moment you are actually having.

**6.** *(2 points)* three things arriving together as one.
