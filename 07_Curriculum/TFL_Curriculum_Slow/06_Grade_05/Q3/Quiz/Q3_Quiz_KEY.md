# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 5** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students design an engineered mismatch of their own. Evidence: every student runs a break satisfying all four rules

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students analyse a break that produced no surprise. Evidence: every student names a cause for a failed break

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students revise a break design after it failed. Evidence: every student re-runs and compares

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students name a different explanation for their own result. Evidence: every student has three genuine alternatives, not strawmen

**5.** *(2 points)* a correct expectation that leaves no trace.

**6.** *(2 points)* when what happened does not match what was expected.
