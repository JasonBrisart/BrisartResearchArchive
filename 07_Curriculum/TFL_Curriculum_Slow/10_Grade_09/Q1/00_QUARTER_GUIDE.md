## The pivot lesson of this quarter

**Lesson 5 — Conditional Determinism.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Lesson 1:** every student has written and sealed a prediction
- **Lesson 2:** every student reads it aloud as a sentence without the board
- **Lesson 3:** every student has the dominant term correct in all six
- **Lesson 4:** every student states a failure mode for each extreme case
- **Lesson 5:** every student states that the condition is fixed parameters
- **Lesson 6:** every student lists all three sources with examples
- **Lesson 7:** every student identifies at least two objections that miss the claim
- **Lesson 8:** the class marks at least six correctly

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Lesson | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | assume the absence is an oversight | Flag it as deliberate: it is not a typo. It is a commitment, and it is checkable. |
| 2 | skip symbols when reading aloud | Restart the read: every symbol, including the subscripts. |
| 3 | describe a high-w3 case as a kind of person | Correct it every time: that is a structural description, not a person. |
| 4 | describe extremes as impossible rather than analysing them | Treat them as thought experiments: not real, informative. |
| 5 | state it without the parameter condition | Supply a counter-case: same input, different weights. Same output? |
| 6 | attribute variability to randomness in the architecture | Point at the guarantee: the architecture contains no stochastic term. |
| 7 | accept every objection as landing | Ask what the claim actually says, then compare. |
| 8 | misplace the subscripts | Require it written from memory, checked by a partner, every session. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
