# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 4 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 9** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students argue formally about a proposed modification. Evidence: every student's testability section names what the change would absorb

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students argue for the modification they argued against. Evidence: every student produces a genuine argument for the other side

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students evaluate structural arguments by criteria. Evidence: every student ranks six arguments with criteria cited

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students defend a structural argument against classmates. Evidence: every student answers three challenges specifically

**5.** *(2 points)* F(t) = w1 Input(t) + w2 Minfo(t) + w3 Sim(t).

**6.** *(2 points)* w1, w2, w3; relative contribution of the three inputs.
