## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 22 — The Design Defence.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 19:** every design passes a peer gate check on both items
- **Session 20:** every student records their unanswered questions
- **Session 21:** every unanswered question produces a revision
- **Session 22:** both gates are satisfied and all four roles answered
- **Session 23:** every divergence is classified as fact or standard
- **Session 24:** every student compares first and last review

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | assume the gates will pass | Run the check before any other scoring. |
| 20 | defend by restating the design | Redirect to the specific question asked. |
| 21 | dismiss the questions as unfair | Require a revision for each. |
| 22 | omit the blind-spot element | Ask for it in front of the room. |
| 23 | attribute divergence to taste | Remove the option: fact or standard. Taste is not one. |
| 24 | cannot find early reviews | One folder from Session 1. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
