# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 3 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**Master's Year 2** · **Total: 38 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every review carries located findings in every section

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every self-review names at least three serious findings

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every design is correctly labelled hypothetical or approved

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every verdict names the failing element

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: the second verdict is better than the first, or the reason is stated

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every design contains all eight components

**7.** *(2 points)* a defect that makes the result uninterpretable.

**8.** *(2 points)* comparing your review against others of the same work.

**9.** *(2 points)* the fixed structure every review follows.

**10.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: every design contains all eight components
