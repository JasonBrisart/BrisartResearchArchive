# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 2** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students apply the testability constraint to their own intentions. Evidence: every student lists at least three unmeasured variables in their own proposal

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students argue whether in-principle falsifiability is enough. Evidence: every student names evidence that would change their position

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the objection that threatens all self-report. Evidence: every student states the objection without weakening it

**4.** *(2 points)* constructing the expectation after the outcome.

**5.** *(2 points)* recording what will be measured before the data exist.
