# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Test

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 2** · **Total: 42 points**

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student lists at least three unmeasured variables in their own proposal

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student names evidence that would change their position

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student states the objection without weakening it

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student quantifies how much of their old data survives

**5.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every protocol includes an unscoreable worked example

**6.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every protocol names who scores and what they know

**7.** *(4 points)* Looking for: every student identifies a hypothesis report cannot carry

**8.** *(2 points)* constructing the expectation after the outcome.

**9.** *(2 points)* recording what will be measured before the data exist.

**10.** *(2 points)* scoring by somebody who does not know the condition.

**11.** *(8 points)* Score on completeness and reasoning, not outcome. Reference: every student identifies a hypothesis report cannot carry
