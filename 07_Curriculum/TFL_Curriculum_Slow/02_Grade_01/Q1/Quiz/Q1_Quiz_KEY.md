# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 1 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 1** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students use expect in place of guess. Evidence: every student writes EXPECT in at least two sentences

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students identify notice, remember, and expect in one moment. Evidence: most students have two rows connecting across all three columns

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students find expectations about routine events. Evidence: every student lists five routine expectations

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students map expectations onto the school day. Evidence: every student has three dated expectations

**5.** *(2 points)* the proper word for a guess about what happens next.

**6.** *(2 points)* the word that joins a guess to where it came from.
