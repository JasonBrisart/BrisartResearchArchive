# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**Grade 7** · **Total: 20 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students define accuracy and persistence as independent. Evidence: every student finds at least three of the four combinations

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students test the independence claim on their own prospective data. Evidence: every student has all four cells populated

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students examine the inaccurate-and-persisted cell. Evidence: every student writes about at least two entries from that cell

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students examine the accurate-and-faded cell. Evidence: every student compares E(t) across both diagonals

**5.** *(2 points)* the persistence threshold required for carry-over.

**6.** *(2 points)* whether Sim(t) matched what actually happened.
