**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students repair a long chain after audit. Evidence: every student repairs from the first break downward

**4.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students find carry-forward in non-TFL material. Evidence: every student produces six rows on outside material

**6.** *(2 points)* what the new moment receives from the one before it.
