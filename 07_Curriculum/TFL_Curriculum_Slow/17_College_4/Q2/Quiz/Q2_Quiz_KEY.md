# ANSWER KEY — Quarter 2 Quiz

> **Teacher only.**

**College Year 4** · **Total: 16 points**

*Prediction accuracy is never scored. Reasoning is.*

---

**1.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students select a critique form and target. Evidence: the target statement passes the reader test

**2.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students support every critique claim with a location or citation. Evidence: every claim is evidenced or deleted

**3.** *(4 points)* Looking for: Students state the framework's best available response. Evidence: every defence is externally checked with the objection recorded

**4.** *(2 points)* the highest claim a design licenses.

**5.** *(2 points)* what a null result does and does not license.
