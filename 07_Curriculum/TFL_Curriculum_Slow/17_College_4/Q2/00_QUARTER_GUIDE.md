## The pivot session of this quarter

**Session 11 — Responding Or Conceding.** If you set homework only once this
quarter, set it after this one. If you are short of time, cut anything before
cutting this.

## Do not move on until

- **Session 8:** the target statement passes the reader test
- **Session 9:** every claim is evidenced or deleted
- **Session 10:** every defence is externally checked with the objection recorded
- **Session 11:** every student either answers or concedes with narrowing
- **Session 12:** every path distinguishes the two kinds of work
- **Session 13:** every assessment is checked in both directions
- **Session 14:** the class identifies its own weakest area

## Errors to expect this quarter

| Session | Students will | Correct it by |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | submit a status objection as a critique | Reject it by name: that is a status. What is wrong with the claim? |
| 9 | retain an unsupported claim as background | Remove the category: background claims are claims. |
| 10 | construct a defence designed to lose | Require the sympathetic check and record the objection. |
| 11 | refuse to concede anything | Score concession publicly at full value when it happens. |
| 12 | name a vague direction | Require actionability: who does what, and what do they then know? |
| 13 | inflate a peripheral gap | Ask what rests on it: if that closed tomorrow, what changes? |
| 14 | review only recent sessions | Sample from every session. |

## Honest data rule

If a classroom activity does not produce the expected pattern, preserve the
result. Do not re-run it to get a tidier outcome, and do not change any student's
classification. Ask instead whether the method could have detected the pattern at
all, and record the answer. A result that does not fit is a result.
