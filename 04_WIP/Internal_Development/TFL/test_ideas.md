# Historical Context

This document contains proposed investigations generated during framework development.

Not every proposed test was executed.

Many entries represent attempts to identify ways of distinguishing between competing explanations.

Purpose:

This file functioned as an idea queue for future investigation and method development.

Historical Importance:

The document preserves questions that appeared important at the time development was occurring, including questions that were never fully answered.

Relationship To Released TFL:

The presence of a proposed test does not imply that the underlying mechanism was adopted into the released framework.

## Persistence

- Persistence curves should be plotted against affective intensity to determine whether a true threshold exists or whether the apparent threshold is an artifact of measurement scaling.
- Persistence should be measured under controlled affect decay conditions to determine whether persistence exhibits hysteresis independent of E(t).
- Persistence should be tested in low-affect conditions to determine whether alternative drivers exist.

## Contradiction & belief

- Contradiction strength should be parametrically varied across fine-grained levels to detect whether there is a bifurcation point where revision shifts to suppression.
- Belief structure should be independently manipulated to determine whether contradiction effects are mediated or overridden by prior weighting.
- Belief stability should be operationalized and tested as a function of cumulative persistence across cycles.
- Confirmation and contradiction should be asymmetrically scaled to detect differences in reinforcement vs suppression mechanisms.

## Cue overlap & reentry

- Cue overlap should be quantitatively defined and systematically varied to determine whether reentry probability scales with similarity or follows categorical gating.
- Delayed reentry should be tested under conditions where affect is held constant but cue overlap varies, and vice versa, to isolate contributing factors.

## Memory & retrieval

- Memory retrieval probabilities should be measured conditional on active simulation to test whether retrieval is simulation-biased rather than input-driven.

## Simulation dynamics

- Competing simulations should be induced simultaneously to test whether the system resolves via dominance, alternation, or coexistence.
- Simulation alternation patterns should be recorded over extended windows to determine whether oscillatory dynamics emerge naturally.
- Internally generated simulations should be isolated and compared against externally cued simulations under identical conditions.

## Ambiguity & uncertainty

- Input uncertainty should be manipulated independently of content to test whether Sim(t) weighting increases as uncertainty rises.

## Measuring simulation strength

- Behavioral bias and self-report should be decoupled experimentally to determine whether simulation strength is better indexed by action than by introspection.

## Resolution

- Resolution trajectories should be tracked at high temporal resolution to determine whether transitions are discrete or continuous.

## Learning & modeling

- Learning effects should be measured across repeated trials to detect systematic changes in persistence thresholds.
- Nonlinear interaction models should be tested against additive models for belief and affect contributions.
