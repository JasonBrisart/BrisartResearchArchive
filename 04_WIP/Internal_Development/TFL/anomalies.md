# Historical Context

This document records situations where expectations produced by the developing model did not match observation.

During development, anomalies were treated as valuable information rather than errors. An anomaly often indicated one of three possibilities:

1. An incomplete explanation.
2. A missing variable.
3. An incorrect assumption.

This file was maintained to ensure contradictory evidence was preserved rather than removed from the record.

Historical Role:

The anomaly log served as a challenge mechanism against emerging TFL concepts. It was specifically intended to identify situations where the current interpretation appeared insufficient.

Relationship To Released TFL:

Anomalies contained within this document should not be interpreted as failures of the released framework. Most entries were recorded before formal framework boundaries had been established.

This file exists primarily as a historical record of model refinement.

## Persistence anomalies

- Persistence is observed under conditions where affective intensity appears insufficient to meet the expected threshold.
- Persistence decay occurs independently of observed affective decay rates.
- Trial-to-trial persistence variability is higher than predicted.

## Contradiction anomalies

- Contradiction fails to produce suppression in cases where the model predicts immediate resolution.
- Confirmation signals occasionally fail to strengthen persistence.
- Strong contradiction does not always produce revision or suppression.
- Interaction effects between belief and affect do not follow expected patterns.

## Reentry & simulation anomalies

- Reentry occurs in the absence of measurable cue overlap.
- Simulations fail to form under conditions that should reliably induce them.
- Increased ambiguity does not consistently increase simulation influence.
- Multiple simulations persist without converging or resolving.
- Alternation between competing simulations occurs without clear triggering conditions.

## Memory & behavioral anomalies

- Behavioral bias emerges without clearly identifiable simulation content.
- Memory retrieval occasionally contradicts rather than reinforces the active simulation.
- Self-report data diverges from behavioral indicators of simulation presence.
