- Promote to framework when an effect is observed consistently across multiple conditions.

- Promote to framework when a variable is required to explain anomalies.

- Promote to framework when a mechanism outperforms existing explanation in test conditions.

- Promote to framework when behavior cannot be explained by baseline assumptions.

- Reject observation when it fails to reproduce under controlled manipulation.

- Flag for further testing when evidence is inconsistent or context-dependent.

- Mark as candidate parameter when phenomenon appears continuous and measurable.

- Mark as structural element when phenomenon appears categorical or rule-based.
