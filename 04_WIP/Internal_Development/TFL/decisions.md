## Persistence

- Evidence suggests persistence follows threshold behavior rather than linear scaling. Pending formal integration into framework as parameter.
- Persistence appears to exhibit inertia independent of momentary affect. Possible new state variable required.
- Resolution likely occurs through discrete transitions rather than continuous decay.

## Contradiction & confirmation

- Contradiction effects are not uniform; revision vs suppression split appears condition-dependent. Requires formal separation in model.
- Confirmation and contradiction effects appear asymmetric. Requires non-symmetric update modeling.

## Cue overlap & reentry

- Cue overlap appears to function as a gating mechanism for reentry. Needs explicit representation.

## Simulation dynamics

- Behavioral bias is a more reliable indicator of simulation strength than self-report. Should be prioritized in measurement.
- Multiple simulations may coexist or alternate across windows. Single-dominance assumption needs reevaluation.

## Memory

- Memory retrieval appears simulation-biased. Neutral retrieval assumption likely invalid.
