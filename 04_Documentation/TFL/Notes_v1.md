# Temporal Feedback Loop (TFL) Notes

This section provides clarifications and boundary conditions relevant to interpreting the Temporal Feedback Loop framework.

## Scope of the Framework

TFL describes the temporal and recursive structure of conscious processing as a loop over `Input(t)`, `Minfo(t)`, `E(t)`, `Sim(t)`, and `F(t)`. It does not, by itself, specify full network-level dynamics beyond the circuit-level grounding in hippocampal-prefrontal interaction.

## Implementation-Ready but Non-Prescriptive

Although the Institutional Edition includes a runnable baseline model with default parameters and update rules, the framework remains a structural template. Laboratories are expected to adapt retrieval functions, feature mappings, and parameter values to their own tasks and data streams.

## Affective Modulation Limits

`E(t)` is modeled as a scalar gain parameter capturing valence-weighted intensity. It does not encode the full richness of emotional phenomenology. It represents the minimal affective structure required for loop persistence and reentry.

## Belief as Structural Prior

Beliefs are treated as affectively stabilized priors that bias `Minfo(t)`, `Sim(t)`, and `Input(t)` filtering. This is a functional definition and does not commit the framework to any particular philosophical stance on selfhood or personal identity.

## Temporal Window Approximation

The 100 to 300 ms loop window is based on empirical ranges associated with perceptual updating and hippocampal-prefrontal communication. It is an approximate operating range, not a fixed constant.

## Integration with Other Frameworks

TFL is intended to serve as a substrate for other frameworks in the Archive, including identity-related models and social-simulation theories. It can be embedded within larger predictive coding, global workspace, or temporospatial architectures.

## Model Boundaries

TFL does not, on its own, account for large-scale network transitions, such as DMN-CEN switching, sleep-state dynamics, collective or multi-agent cognition, or full emotional granularity. These domains require additional frameworks or extensions.

## Interpretive Guidance

TFL should be read as a structural model of recursive, affectively modulated simulation. It explains how internal representations persist, update, and resolve over time, but does not prescribe specific therapeutic protocols or behavioral interventions.
