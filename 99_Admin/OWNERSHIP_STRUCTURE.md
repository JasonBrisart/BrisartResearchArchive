# Brisart Research Archive
## Ownership and Investment Structure

- Version: 1.0
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Legal entity: Brisart Research Archive

## Ownership and Pricing

Brisart Research Archive is structured around 100 total outstanding shares.

The founder intends to retain 99 shares indefinitely. Fractions of the
remaining one share may be sold to approved investors. Together, all
outside investors will hold no more than 1% of the company.

The founder-selected asking valuation is $100 trillion. This is an
asking valuation, not an independent appraisal or established market value.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| One whole share | $1,000,000,000,000 |
| Minimum purchase | $100 |
| Shares purchased for $100 | 0.0000000001 share |
| Company ownership purchased for $100 | 0.0000000001% |

Additional purchases can be made in $100 increments, subject to approval
and availability.

These figures assume exactly 100 outstanding shares.

## Who Can Invest

Investment is available only to active Brisart Research Lab subscribers
on the $499.99 per month Research Lab tier or higher.

Your qualifying subscription must remain active for as long as you hold
shares.

If your qualifying subscription lapses, you have one full month from
the date of the subscription lapse to restore it.

If the subscription remains inactive for longer than one full month,
your entire investment holding becomes subject to mandatory forfeiture
and return to Brisart.

Once the forfeiture is completed, the applicable shares will return to
Brisart and the public ownership ledger will be updated to record the
change.

The subscription requirement applies for the entire period during which
you remain invested, not only when you make the original purchase.

## Approval

Every purchase, sale, transfer, and buyout requires prior written approval
from Brisart or an expressly authorized representative.

Before any money changes hands, both parties must confirm over email:

- the purchase or sale amount;
- the exact fractional shareholding.

## Investor Sales

You may request to sell your holding back to Brisart at any time.

Brisart may approve, defer, or deny a sale request.

If you have held the investment for less than one year, an approved sale
back to Brisart will return the amount you originally paid.

If you have held the investment for one year or longer, an approved sale
back to Brisart will be priced at 150% of the original purchase price.

For example, if you purchased a holding for $100 and Brisart approves
your sale after you have held it for at least one year, the sale price
would be $150.

Approval of the sale, final pricing, payment, and transfer will be recorded
as part of the transaction.

## Company-Initiated Buyout

Brisart may initiate a buyout of all or part of your holding at any time.

The buyout price is 150% of the original purchase price attributable to
the holding being purchased.

For example, a holding originally purchased for $100 would have a
company-initiated buyout price of $150.

Once completed, the public ownership ledger will be updated to reflect
the returned shares.

## Public Ownership Ledger

Brisart will maintain a public ownership ledger on GitHub using Markdown
files and Git version history.

Anyone may view the published ledger and its recorded changes.

The ledger will record:

- approved purchases;
- exact fractional holdings;
- completed sales;
- company-initiated buyouts;
- subscription-related returns;
- subsequent ownership changes.

The ledger may also contain identifying information that an investor has
expressly agreed may be published for verification of the investment.

Git history will preserve the historical record of changes made to the
published ledger.

## Revisions and No Automatic Grandfathering

Brisart reserves the right to revise these terms.

The intended policy is that the newest version applies from its effective 
date regardless of when an investment was originally purchased. Earlier 
investments do not automatically receive grandfathering.

Each revision will identify:

- its version;
- its effective date;
- the material changes being introduced.

## Investor Participation and Company Control

Investing provides a financial ownership interest. It does not provide
a management position, board seat, or authority over company operations,
research, development, releases, or business strategy.

Investor holdings are intended to be nonvoting.

## Before You Invest

Investment in Brisart Research Archive requires:

1. an active qualifying Brisart Research Archive subscription;
2. approval from Brisart or an authorized representative;
3. agreement on the purchase amount and exact fractional shareholding.
